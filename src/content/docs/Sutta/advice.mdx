<TabItem label="My Translation">
933\. [The Bhagavā] was dwelling at Rājagaha, in the Bamboo Grove.

Then āyasmā Mahākassapa approached the Bhagavā. Having approached, he bowed to the Bhagavā and sat down to one side. The Bhagavā said this to āyasmā Mahākassapa seated to one side:

> "Kassapa, admonish the bhikkhū; Kassapa, give the bhikkhū a discourse on the Dhamma. Either I, Kassapa, would admonish the bhikkhū, or you would; either I would give the bhikkhū a discourse on the Dhamma, or you would."

> 934\. "Indeed, Bhante, nowadays the bhikkhū are difficult to admonish, they are endowed with qualities that make them difficult to admonish, they are impatient, and they do not take instruction in the right way. Here, Bhante, I saw a bhikkhu named Bhaṇḍa, a pupil of Ānanda, and a bhikkhu named Abhijika, a pupil of Anuruddha, out-talking each other about what they had heard, saying:
>
> > ‘Come, bhikkhu, who will speak more, who will speak more eloquently, who will speak longer?’"

935\. Then the Bhagavā addressed a certain bhikkhu:

> "Come, bhikkhu, in my name, summon the bhikkhu Bhaṇḍa, a pupil of Ānanda, and the bhikkhu Abhijika, a pupil of Anuruddha, saying: ‘The Teacher summons the āyasmanto.’"

> "Yes, Bhante,"

that bhikkhu replied to the Bhagavā, and he approached those bhikkhū. Having approached, he said this to those bhikkhū:

> "The Teacher summons the āyasmanto."

> 936\. "Yes, friend,"

those bhikkhū replied to that bhikkhu, and they approached the Bhagavā. Having approached, they bowed to the Bhagavā and sat down to one side. The Bhagavā said this to those bhikkhū seated to one side:

> "Is it really true, Bhikkhave, that you out-talk each other about what you have heard, saying:
>
> > ‘Come, bhikkhu, who will speak more, who will speak more eloquently, who will speak longer?’"

> "Yes, Bhante."

> "Have you, Bhikkhave, understood the Dhamma taught by me in this way —
>
> > ‘Come, Bhikkhave, out-talk each other about what you have heard, saying: Come, bhikkhu, who will speak more, who will speak more eloquently, who will speak longer?’"

> "Of course not, Bhante."

> "If indeed you, Bhikkhave, have not understood the Dhamma taught by me in this way, then for what reason, you foolish men, knowing what, seeing what, having gone forth in such a well-proclaimed Dhamma and Discipline, do you out-talk each other about what you have heard, saying:
>
> > ‘Come, bhikkhu, who will speak more, who will speak more eloquently, who will speak longer?’"

937\. Then those bhikkhū fell with their heads at the Bhagavā's feet and said this to the Bhagavā:

> "Bhante, an offence has overtaken us, as if we were foolish, deluded, and unskilful, we who, having gone forth in such a well-proclaimed Dhamma and Discipline, out-talked each other about what we had heard, saying:
>
> > ‘Come, bhikkhu, who will speak more, who will speak more eloquently, who will speak longer.’
>
> Bhante, may the Bhagavā accept our offence as an offence for future restraint."

> 938\. "Indeed, Bhikkhave, an offence has overtaken you, as if you were foolish, deluded, and unskilful, you who, having gone forth in such a well-proclaimed Dhamma and Discipline, out-talked each other about what you had heard, saying:
>
> > ‘Come, bhikkhu, who will speak more, who will speak more eloquently, who will speak longer.’
>
> And since indeed you, Bhikkhave, seeing the offence as an offence, rectify it in accordance with the Dhamma, we accept that from you. For this is growth in the Discipline of the Liberated One, Bhikkhave: when one sees an offence as an offence, rectifies it in accordance with the Dhamma, and undertakes future restraint."

<TabItem label="Pāḷi (Roman IAST)">
933\. Rājagahe veḷuvane. Atha kho āyasmā mahākassapo yena bhagavā tenupasaṅkami; upasaṅkamitvā bhagavantaṃ abhivādetvā ekamantaṃ nisīdi. Ekamantaṃ nisinnaṃ kho āyasmantaṃ mahākassapaṃ bhagavā etadavoca—  “ovada, kassapa, bhikkhū; karohi, kassapa, bhikkhūnaṃ dhammiṃ kathaṃ. Ahaṃ vā, kassapa, bhikkhū ovadeyyaṃ tvaṃ vā; ahaṃ vā bhikkhūnaṃ dhammiṃ kathaṃ kareyyaṃ tvaṃ vā”ti.

934\. “Dubbacā kho, bhante, etarahi bhikkhū, dovacassakaraṇehi dhammehi samannāgatā, akkhamā, appadakkhiṇaggāhino anusāsaniṃ. Idhāhaṃ, bhante, addasaṃ bhaṇḍañca nāma bhikkhuṃ ānandassa saddhivihāriṃ abhijikañca nāma bhikkhuṃ anuruddhassa saddhivihāriṃ aññamaññaṃ sutena accāvadante—  ‘ehi, bhikkhu, ko bahutaraṃ bhāsissati, ko sundarataraṃ bhāsissati, ko cirataraṃ bhāsissatī’”ti.

935\. Atha kho bhagavā aññataraṃ bhikkhuṃ āmantesi—  “ehi tvaṃ, bhikkhu, mama vacanena bhaṇḍañca bhikkhuṃ ānandassa saddhivihāriṃ abhijikañca bhikkhuṃ anuruddhassa saddhivihāriṃ āmantehi—  ‘satthā āyasmante āmantetī’”ti. “Evaṃ, bhante”ti kho so bhikkhu bhagavato paṭissutvā yena te bhikkhū tenupasaṅkami; upasaṅkamitvā te bhikkhū etadavoca—  “satthā āyasmante āmantetī”ti.

936\. “Evamāvuso”ti kho te bhikkhū tassa bhikkhuno paṭissutvā yena bhagavā tenupasaṅkamiṃsu; upasaṅkamitvā bhagavantaṃ abhivādetvā ekamantaṃ nisīdiṃsu. Ekamantaṃ nisinne kho te bhikkhū bhagavā etadavoca—  “saccaṃ kira tumhe, bhikkhave, aññamaññaṃ sutena accāvadatha—  ‘ehi, bhikkhu, ko bahutaraṃ bhāsissati, ko sundarataraṃ bhāsissati, ko cirataraṃ bhāsissatī’”ti? “Evaṃ, bhante”. “Kiṃ nu kho me tumhe, bhikkhave, evaṃ dhammaṃ desitaṃ ājānātha—  ‘etha tumhe, bhikkhave, aññamaññaṃ sutena accāvadatha—  ehi, bhikkhu, ko bahutaraṃ bhāsissati, ko sundarataraṃ bhāsissati, ko cirataraṃ bhāsissatī’”ti? “No hetaṃ, bhante”. “No ce kira me tumhe, bhikkhave, evaṃ dhammaṃ desitaṃ ājānātha, atha kiñcarahi tumhe, moghapurisā, kiṃ jānantā kiṃ passantā evaṃ svākkhāte dhammavinaye pabbajitā samānā aññamaññaṃ sutena accāvadatha—  ‘ehi, bhikkhu, ko bahutaraṃ bhāsissati, ko sundarataraṃ bhāsissati, ko cirataraṃ bhāsissatī’”ti.

937\. Atha kho te bhikkhū bhagavato pādesu sirasā nipatitvā bhagavantaṃ etadavocuṃ—  “accayo no, bhante, accagamā, yathābāle yathāmūḷhe yathāakusale, ye mayaṃ evaṃ svākkhāte dhammavinaye pabbajitā samānā aññamaññaṃ sutena accāvadimha—  ‘ehi, bhikkhu, ko bahutaraṃ bhāsissati, ko sundarataraṃ bhāsissati, ko cirataraṃ bhāsissatī’ti. Tesaṃ no, bhante, bhagavā accayaṃ accayato paṭiggaṇhātu āyatiṃ saṃvarāyā”ti.

938\. “Taggha tumhe, bhikkhave, accayo accagamā yathābāle yathāmūḷhe yathāakusale, ye tumhe evaṃ svākkhāte dhammavinaye pabbajitā samānā aññamaññaṃ sutena accāvadittha—  ‘ehi, bhikkhu, ko bahutaraṃ bhāsissati, ko sundarataraṃ bhāsissati, ko cirataraṃ bhāsissatī’ti. Yato ca kho tumhe, bhikkhave, accayaṃ accayato disvā yathādhammaṃ paṭikarotha, taṃ vo mayaṃ paṭiggaṇhāma. Vuddhi hesā, bhikkhave, ariyassa vinaye yo accayaṃ accayato disvā yathādhammaṃ paṭikaroti āyatiñca saṃvaraṃ āpajjatī”ti.

933\. 𑀭𑀸𑀚𑀕𑀳𑁂 𑀯𑁂𑀍𑀉𑀯𑀦𑁂. 𑀅𑀣 𑀔𑁄 𑀆𑀬𑀲𑁆𑀫𑀸 𑀫𑀳𑀸𑀓𑀲𑁆𑀲𑀧𑁄 𑀬𑁂𑀦 𑀪𑀕𑀯𑀸 𑀢𑁂𑀦𑀼𑀧𑀲𑀗𑁆𑀓𑀫𑀺; 𑀉𑀧𑀲𑀗𑁆𑀓𑀫𑀺𑀢𑁆𑀯𑀸 𑀪𑀕𑀯𑀦𑁆𑀢𑀁 𑀅𑀪𑀺𑀯𑀸𑀤𑁂𑀢𑁆𑀯𑀸 𑀏𑀓𑀫𑀦𑁆𑀢𑀁 𑀦𑀺𑀲𑀻𑀤𑀺. 𑀏𑀓𑀫𑀦𑁆𑀢𑀁 𑀦𑀺𑀲𑀺𑀦𑁆𑀦𑀁 𑀔𑁄 𑀆𑀬𑀲𑁆𑀫𑀦𑁆𑀢𑀁 𑀫𑀳𑀸𑀓𑀲𑁆𑀲𑀧𑀁 𑀪𑀕𑀯𑀸 𑀏𑀢𑀤𑀯𑁄𑀘—  “𑀑𑀯𑀤, 𑀓𑀲𑁆𑀲𑀧, 𑀪𑀺𑀓𑁆𑀔𑀽; 𑀓𑀭𑁄𑀳𑀺, 𑀓𑀲𑁆𑀲𑀧, 𑀪𑀺𑀓𑁆𑀔𑀽𑀦𑀁 𑀥𑀫𑁆𑀫𑀺𑀁 𑀓𑀣𑀁. 𑀅𑀳𑀁 𑀯𑀸, 𑀓𑀲𑁆𑀲𑀧, 𑀪𑀺𑀓𑁆𑀔𑀽 𑀑𑀯𑀤𑁂𑀬𑁆𑀬𑀁 𑀢𑁆𑀯𑀁 𑀯𑀸; 𑀅𑀳𑀁 𑀯𑀸 𑀪𑀺𑀓𑁆𑀔𑀽𑀦𑀁 𑀥𑀫𑁆𑀫𑀺𑀁 𑀓𑀣𑀁 𑀓𑀭𑁂𑀬𑁆𑀬𑀁 𑀢𑁆𑀯𑀁 𑀯𑀸”𑀢𑀺.

934\. “𑀤𑀼𑀩𑁆𑀩𑀘𑀸 𑀔𑁄, 𑀪𑀦𑁆𑀢𑁂, 𑀏𑀢𑀭𑀳𑀺 𑀪𑀺𑀓𑁆𑀔𑀽, 𑀤𑁄𑀯𑀘𑀲𑁆𑀲𑀓𑀭𑀡𑁂𑀳𑀺 𑀥𑀫𑁆𑀫𑁂𑀳𑀺 𑀲𑀫𑀦𑁆𑀦𑀸𑀕𑀢𑀸, 𑀅𑀓𑁆𑀔𑀫𑀸, 𑀅𑀧𑁆𑀧𑀤𑀓𑁆𑀔𑀺𑀡𑀕𑁆𑀕𑀸𑀳𑀺𑀦𑁄 𑀅𑀦𑀼𑀲𑀸𑀲𑀦𑀺𑀁. 𑀇𑀥𑀸𑀳𑀁, 𑀪𑀦𑁆𑀢𑁂, 𑀅𑀤𑁆𑀤𑀲𑀁 𑀪𑀡𑁆𑀟𑀜𑁆𑀘 𑀦𑀸𑀫 𑀪𑀺𑀓𑁆𑀔𑀼𑀁 𑀆𑀦𑀦𑁆𑀤𑀲𑁆𑀲 𑀲𑀤𑁆𑀥𑀺𑀯𑀺𑀳𑀸𑀭𑀺𑀁 𑀅𑀪𑀺𑀚𑀺𑀓𑀜𑁆𑀘 𑀦𑀸𑀫 𑀪𑀺𑀓𑁆𑀔𑀼𑀁 𑀅𑀦𑀼𑀭𑀼𑀤𑁆𑀥𑀲𑁆𑀲 𑀲𑀤𑁆𑀥𑀺𑀯𑀺𑀳𑀸𑀭𑀺𑀁 𑀅𑀜𑁆𑀜𑀫𑀜𑁆𑀜𑀁 𑀲𑀼𑀢𑁂𑀦 𑀅𑀘𑁆𑀘𑀸𑀯𑀤𑀦𑁆𑀢𑁂—  ‘𑀏𑀳𑀺, 𑀪𑀺𑀓𑁆𑀔𑀼, 𑀓𑁄 𑀩𑀳𑀼𑀢𑀭𑀁 𑀪𑀸𑀲𑀺𑀲𑁆𑀲𑀢𑀺, 𑀓𑁄 𑀲𑀼𑀦𑁆𑀤𑀭𑀢𑀭𑀁 𑀪𑀸𑀲𑀺𑀲𑁆𑀲𑀢𑀺, 𑀓𑁄 𑀘𑀺𑀭𑀢𑀭𑀁 𑀪𑀸𑀲𑀺𑀲𑁆𑀲𑀢𑀻’”𑀢𑀺.

935\. 𑀅𑀣 𑀔𑁄 𑀪𑀕𑀯𑀸 𑀅𑀜𑁆𑀜𑀢𑀭𑀁 𑀪𑀺𑀓𑁆𑀔𑀼𑀁 𑀆𑀫𑀦𑁆𑀢𑁂𑀲𑀺—  “𑀏𑀳𑀺 𑀢𑁆𑀯𑀁, 𑀪𑀺𑀓𑁆𑀔𑀼, 𑀫𑀫 𑀯𑀘𑀦𑁂𑀦 𑀪𑀡𑁆𑀟𑀜𑁆𑀘 𑀪𑀺𑀓𑁆𑀔𑀼𑀁 𑀆𑀦𑀦𑁆𑀤𑀲𑁆𑀲 𑀲𑀤𑁆𑀥𑀺𑀯𑀺𑀳𑀸𑀭𑀺𑀁 𑀅𑀪𑀺𑀚𑀺𑀓𑀜𑁆𑀘 𑀪𑀺𑀓𑁆𑀔𑀼𑀁 𑀅𑀦𑀼𑀭𑀼𑀤𑁆𑀥𑀲𑁆𑀲 𑀲𑀤𑁆𑀥𑀺𑀯𑀺𑀳𑀸𑀭𑀺𑀁 𑀆𑀫𑀦𑁆𑀢𑁂𑀳𑀺—  ‘𑀲𑀢𑁆𑀣𑀸 𑀆𑀬𑀲𑁆𑀫𑀦𑁆𑀢𑁂 𑀆𑀫𑀦𑁆𑀢𑁂𑀢𑀻’”𑀢𑀺. “𑀏𑀯𑀁, 𑀪𑀦𑁆𑀢𑁂”𑀢𑀺 𑀔𑁄 𑀲𑁄 𑀪𑀺𑀓𑁆𑀔𑀼 𑀪𑀕𑀯𑀢𑁄 𑀧𑀝𑀺𑀲𑁆𑀲𑀼𑀢𑁆𑀯𑀸 𑀬𑁂𑀦 𑀢𑁂 𑀪𑀺𑀓𑁆𑀔𑀽 𑀢𑁂𑀦𑀼𑀧𑀲𑀗𑁆𑀓𑀫𑀺; 𑀉𑀧𑀲𑀗𑁆𑀓𑀫𑀺𑀢𑁆𑀯𑀸 𑀢𑁂 𑀪𑀺𑀓𑁆𑀔𑀽 𑀏𑀢𑀤𑀯𑁄𑀘—  “𑀲𑀢𑁆𑀣𑀸 𑀆𑀬𑀲𑁆𑀫𑀦𑁆𑀢𑁂 𑀆𑀫𑀦𑁆𑀢𑁂𑀢𑀻”𑀢𑀺.

936\. “𑀏𑀯𑀫𑀸𑀯𑀼𑀲𑁄”𑀢𑀺 𑀔𑁄 𑀢𑁂 𑀪𑀺𑀓𑁆𑀔𑀽 𑀢𑀲𑁆𑀲 𑀪𑀺𑀓𑁆𑀔𑀼𑀦𑁄 𑀧𑀝𑀺𑀲𑁆𑀲𑀼𑀢𑁆𑀯𑀸 𑀬𑁂𑀦 𑀪𑀕𑀯𑀸 𑀢𑁂𑀦𑀼𑀧𑀲𑀗𑁆𑀓𑀫𑀺𑀁𑀲𑀼; 𑀉𑀧𑀲𑀗𑁆𑀓𑀫𑀺𑀢𑁆𑀯𑀸 𑀪𑀕𑀯𑀦𑁆𑀢𑀁 𑀅𑀪𑀺𑀯𑀸𑀤𑁂𑀢𑁆𑀯𑀸 𑀏𑀓𑀫𑀦𑁆𑀢𑀁 𑀦𑀺𑀲𑀻𑀤𑀺𑀁𑀲𑀼. 𑀏𑀓𑀫𑀦𑁆𑀢𑀁 𑀦𑀺𑀲𑀺𑀦𑁆𑀦𑁂 𑀔𑁄 𑀢𑁂 𑀪𑀺𑀓𑁆𑀔𑀽 𑀪𑀕𑀯𑀸 𑀏𑀢𑀤𑀯𑁄𑀘—  “𑀲𑀘𑁆𑀘𑀁 𑀓𑀺𑀭 𑀢𑀼𑀫𑁆𑀳𑁂, 𑀪𑀺𑀓𑁆𑀔𑀯𑁂, 𑀅𑀜𑁆𑀜𑀫𑀜𑁆𑀜𑀁 𑀲𑀼𑀢𑁂𑀦 𑀅𑀘𑁆𑀘𑀸𑀯𑀤𑀣—  ‘𑀏𑀳𑀺, 𑀪𑀺𑀓𑁆𑀔𑀼, 𑀓𑁄 𑀩𑀳𑀼𑀢𑀭𑀁 𑀪𑀸𑀲𑀺𑀲𑁆𑀲𑀢𑀺, 𑀓𑁄 𑀲𑀼𑀦𑁆𑀤𑀭𑀢𑀭𑀁 𑀪𑀸𑀲𑀺𑀲𑁆𑀲𑀢𑀺, 𑀓𑁄 𑀘𑀺𑀭𑀢𑀭𑀁 𑀪𑀸𑀲𑀺𑀲𑁆𑀲𑀢𑀻’”𑀢𑀺? “𑀏𑀯𑀁, 𑀪𑀦𑁆𑀢𑁂”. “𑀓𑀺𑀁 𑀦𑀼 𑀔𑁄 𑀫𑁂 𑀢𑀼𑀫𑁆𑀳𑁂, 𑀪𑀺𑀓𑁆𑀔𑀯𑁂, 𑀏𑀯𑀁 𑀥𑀫𑁆𑀫𑀁 𑀤𑁂𑀲𑀺𑀢𑀁 𑀆𑀚𑀸𑀦𑀸𑀣—  ‘𑀏𑀣 𑀢𑀼𑀫𑁆𑀳𑁂, 𑀪𑀺𑀓𑁆𑀔𑀯𑁂, 𑀅𑀜𑁆𑀜𑀫𑀜𑁆𑀜𑀁 𑀲𑀼𑀢𑁂𑀦 𑀅𑀘𑁆𑀘𑀸𑀯𑀤𑀣—  𑀏𑀳𑀺, 𑀪𑀺𑀓𑁆𑀔𑀼, 𑀓𑁄 𑀩𑀳𑀼𑀢𑀭𑀁 𑀪𑀸𑀲𑀺𑀲𑁆𑀲𑀢𑀺, 𑀓𑁄 𑀲𑀼𑀦𑁆𑀤𑀭𑀢𑀭𑀁 𑀪𑀸𑀲𑀺𑀲𑁆𑀲𑀢𑀺, 𑀓𑁄 𑀘𑀺𑀭𑀢𑀭𑀁 𑀪𑀸𑀲𑀺𑀲𑁆𑀲𑀢𑀻’”𑀢𑀺? “𑀦𑁄 𑀳𑁂𑀢𑀁, 𑀪𑀦𑁆𑀢𑁂”. “𑀦𑁄 𑀘𑁂 𑀓𑀺𑀭 𑀫𑁂 𑀢𑀼𑀫𑁆𑀳𑁂, 𑀪𑀺𑀓𑁆𑀔𑀯𑁂, 𑀏𑀯𑀁 𑀥𑀫𑁆𑀫𑀁 𑀤𑁂𑀲𑀺𑀢𑀁 𑀆𑀚𑀸𑀦𑀸𑀣, 𑀅𑀣 𑀓𑀺𑀜𑁆𑀘𑀭𑀳𑀺 𑀢𑀼𑀫𑁆𑀳𑁂, 𑀫𑁄𑀖𑀧𑀼𑀭𑀺𑀲𑀸, 𑀓𑀺𑀁 𑀚𑀸𑀦𑀦𑁆𑀢𑀸 𑀓𑀺𑀁 𑀧𑀲𑁆𑀲𑀦𑁆𑀢𑀸 𑀏𑀯𑀁 𑀲𑁆𑀯𑀸𑀓𑁆𑀔𑀸𑀢𑁂 𑀥𑀫𑁆𑀫𑀯𑀺𑀦𑀬𑁂 𑀧𑀩𑁆𑀩𑀚𑀺𑀢𑀸 𑀲𑀫𑀸𑀦𑀸 𑀅𑀜𑁆𑀜𑀫𑀜𑁆𑀜𑀁 𑀲𑀼𑀢𑁂𑀦 𑀅𑀘𑁆𑀘𑀸𑀯𑀤𑀣—  ‘𑀏𑀳𑀺, 𑀪𑀺𑀓𑁆𑀔𑀼, 𑀓𑁄 𑀩𑀳𑀼𑀢𑀭𑀁 𑀪𑀸𑀲𑀺𑀲𑁆𑀲𑀢𑀺, 𑀓𑁄 𑀲𑀼𑀦𑁆𑀤𑀭𑀢𑀭𑀁 𑀪𑀸𑀲𑀺𑀲𑁆𑀲𑀢𑀺, 𑀓𑁄 𑀘𑀺𑀭𑀢𑀭𑀁 𑀪𑀸𑀲𑀺𑀲𑁆𑀲𑀢𑀻’”𑀢𑀺.

937\. 𑀅𑀣 𑀔𑁄 𑀢𑁂 𑀪𑀺𑀓𑁆𑀔𑀽 𑀪𑀕𑀯𑀢𑁄 𑀧𑀸𑀤𑁂𑀲𑀼 𑀲𑀺𑀭𑀲𑀸 𑀦𑀺𑀧𑀢𑀺𑀢𑁆𑀯𑀸 𑀪𑀕𑀯𑀦𑁆𑀢𑀁 𑀏𑀢𑀤𑀯𑁄𑀘𑀼𑀁—  “𑀅𑀘𑁆𑀘𑀬𑁄 𑀦𑁄, 𑀪𑀦𑁆𑀢𑁂, 𑀅𑀘𑁆𑀘𑀕𑀫𑀸, 𑀬𑀣𑀸𑀩𑀸𑀮𑁂 𑀬𑀣𑀸𑀫𑀽𑀍𑀳𑁂 𑀬𑀣𑀸𑀅𑀓𑀼𑀲𑀮𑁂, 𑀬𑁂 𑀫𑀬𑀁 𑀏𑀯𑀁 𑀲𑁆𑀯𑀸𑀓𑁆𑀔𑀸𑀢𑁂 𑀥𑀫𑁆𑀫𑀯𑀺𑀦𑀬𑁂 𑀧𑀩𑁆𑀩𑀚𑀺𑀢𑀸 𑀲𑀫𑀸𑀦𑀸 𑀅𑀜𑁆𑀜𑀫𑀜𑁆𑀜𑀁 𑀲𑀼𑀢𑁂𑀦 𑀅𑀘𑁆𑀘𑀸𑀯𑀤𑀺𑀫𑁆𑀳—  ‘𑀏𑀳𑀺, 𑀪𑀺𑀓𑁆𑀔𑀼, 𑀓𑁄 𑀩𑀳𑀼𑀢𑀭𑀁 𑀪𑀸𑀲𑀺𑀲𑁆𑀲𑀢𑀺, 𑀓𑁄 𑀲𑀼𑀦𑁆𑀤𑀭𑀢𑀭𑀁 𑀪𑀸𑀲𑀺𑀲𑁆𑀲𑀢𑀺, 𑀓𑁄 𑀘𑀺𑀭𑀢𑀭𑀁 𑀪𑀸𑀲𑀺𑀲𑁆𑀲𑀢𑀻’𑀢𑀺. 𑀢𑁂𑀲𑀁 𑀦𑁄, 𑀪𑀦𑁆𑀢𑁂, 𑀪𑀕𑀯𑀸 𑀅𑀘𑁆𑀘𑀬𑀁 𑀅𑀘𑁆𑀘𑀬𑀢𑁄 𑀧𑀝𑀺𑀕𑁆𑀕𑀡𑁆𑀳𑀸𑀢𑀼 𑀆𑀬𑀢𑀺𑀁 𑀲𑀁𑀯𑀭𑀸𑀬𑀸”𑀢𑀺.

938\. “𑀢𑀕𑁆𑀖 𑀢𑀼𑀫𑁆𑀳𑁂, 𑀪𑀺𑀓𑁆𑀔𑀯𑁂, 𑀅𑀘𑁆𑀘𑀬𑁄 𑀅𑀘𑁆𑀘𑀕𑀫𑀸 𑀬𑀣𑀸𑀩𑀸𑀮𑁂 𑀬𑀣𑀸𑀫𑀽𑀍𑀳𑁂 𑀬𑀣𑀸𑀅𑀓𑀼𑀲𑀮𑁂, 𑀬𑁂 𑀢𑀼𑀫𑁆𑀳𑁂 𑀏𑀯𑀁 𑀲𑁆𑀯𑀸𑀓𑁆𑀔𑀸𑀢𑁂 𑀥𑀫𑁆𑀫𑀯𑀺𑀦𑀬𑁂 𑀧𑀩𑁆𑀩𑀚𑀺𑀢𑀸 𑀲𑀫𑀸𑀦𑀸 𑀅𑀜𑁆𑀜𑀫𑀜𑁆𑀜𑀁 𑀲𑀼𑀢𑁂𑀦 𑀅𑀘𑁆𑀘𑀸𑀯𑀤𑀺𑀢𑁆𑀣—  ‘𑀏𑀳𑀺, 𑀪𑀺𑀓𑁆𑀔𑀼, 𑀓𑁄 𑀩𑀳𑀼𑀢𑀭𑀁 𑀪𑀸𑀲𑀺𑀲𑁆𑀲𑀢𑀺, 𑀓𑁄 𑀲𑀼𑀦𑁆𑀤𑀭𑀢𑀭𑀁 𑀪𑀸𑀲𑀺𑀲𑁆𑀲𑀢𑀺, 𑀓𑁄 𑀘𑀺𑀭𑀢𑀭𑀁 𑀪𑀸𑀲𑀺𑀲𑁆𑀲𑀢𑀻’𑀢𑀺. 𑀬𑀢𑁄 𑀘 𑀔𑁄 𑀢𑀼𑀫𑁆𑀳𑁂, 𑀪𑀺𑀓𑁆𑀔𑀯𑁂, 𑀅𑀘𑁆𑀘𑀬𑀁 𑀅𑀘𑁆𑀘𑀬𑀢𑁄 𑀤𑀺𑀲𑁆𑀯𑀸 𑀬𑀣𑀸𑀥𑀫𑁆𑀫𑀁 𑀧𑀝𑀺𑀓𑀭𑁄𑀣, 𑀢𑀁 𑀯𑁄 𑀫𑀬𑀁 𑀧𑀝𑀺𑀕𑁆𑀕𑀡𑁆𑀳𑀸𑀫. 𑀯𑀼𑀤𑁆𑀥𑀺 𑀳𑁂𑀲𑀸, 𑀪𑀺𑀓𑁆𑀔𑀯𑁂, 𑀅𑀭𑀺𑀬𑀲𑁆𑀲 𑀯𑀺𑀦𑀬𑁂 𑀬𑁄 𑀅𑀘𑁆𑀘𑀬𑀁 𑀅𑀘𑁆𑀘𑀬𑀢𑁄 𑀤𑀺𑀲𑁆𑀯𑀸 𑀬𑀣𑀸𑀥𑀫𑁆𑀫𑀁 𑀧𑀝𑀺𑀓𑀭𑁄𑀢𑀺 𑀆𑀬𑀢𑀺𑀜𑁆𑀘 𑀲𑀁𑀯𑀭𑀁 𑀆𑀧𑀚𑁆𑀚𑀢𑀻”𑀢𑀺.
