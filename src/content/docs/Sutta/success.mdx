[14S5/7.1.1 Apārasutta](https://tipitaka2500.github.io/tipitaka/14S5/7/7.1/7.1.1.html)

<Tabs syncKey="paliquote">
<TabItem label="My Translation">
1341\. “Bhikkhave, these `cattāro iddhipādā` (four bases of success), when cultivated and practised often, lead to going from the near shore to the far shore. What four? Here, Bhikkhave, a bhikkhu
>
> * cultivates the basis of success that possesses `samādhi` (focus) born of `chanda` (desire), along with the `padhānasaṅkhāra` (mental constructions of exertion);
> * cultivates the basis of success that possesses focus born of `vīriya` (energy), along with the mental constructions of exertion;
> * cultivates the basis of success that possesses focus born of `citta` (mind), along with the mental constructions of exertion;
> * cultivates the basis of success that possesses focus born of `vīmaṃsā` (discrimination), along with the mental constructions of exertion.
>
> These four bases of success, Bhikkhave, when cultivated and practised often, lead to going from the near shore to the far shore.”
</TabItem>

<TabItem label="Pāḷi (Roman IAST)">
1341\. “Cattārome, bhikkhave, iddhipādā bhāvitā bahulīkatā apārā pāraṃ gamanāya saṃvattanti. Katame cattāro? Idha, bhikkhave, bhikkhu chandasamādhippadhānasaṅkhārasamannāgataṃ iddhipādaṃ bhāveti, vīriyasamādhippadhānasaṅkhārasamannāgataṃ iddhipādaṃ bhāveti, cittasamādhippadhānasaṅkhārasamannāgataṃ iddhipādaṃ bhāveti, vīmaṃsāsamādhippadhānasaṅkhārasamannāgataṃ iddhipādaṃ bhāveti. Ime kho, bhikkhave, cattāro iddhipādā bhāvitā bahulīkatā apārā pāraṃ gamanāya saṃvattantī”ti.
</TabItem>
