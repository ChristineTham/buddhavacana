[12S2/3.3.3 Pañcasikkhāpadasutta](https://tipitaka2500.github.io/tipitaka/12S2/3/3.3/3.3.3.html)

:::note
This is the only reference I can find to the concept of the "five precepts" in the Pāḷi canon. Indeed, the Abhidhamma Vibhaṅga does not even list a sutta explanation of the five precepts, only an analytical explanation.

This strongly suggests the five precepts did not originally have prominence in the Buddha's teachings, and subsequently the notion of the five precepts as a "basic code of ethics to be respected by lay followers of Buddhism" came much later. 
:::

768\. [The Bhagavā] was dwelling at Sāvatthī.

> “It is by nature, Bhikkhave, that beings associate and come together.
>
> * Killers of living beings associate and come together with killers of living beings;
> * those who take what is not given associate and come together with those who take what is not given;
> * those who misconduct themselves in sensual pleasures associate and come together with those who misconduct themselves in sensual pleasures;
> * liars associate and come together with liars; and
> * those who indulge in wine, spirits, drugs and states of intoxication associate and come together with those who indulge in wine, spirits, drugs and states of intoxication.
>
769\.
>
> * Those abstaining from killing living beings associate and come together with those abstaining from killing living beings;
> * those abstaining from taking what is not given associate and come together with those abstaining from taking what is not given;
> * those abstaining from sexual misconduct associate and come together with those abstaining from sexual misconduct;
> * those abstaining from false speech associate and come together with those abstaining from false speech; and
> * those abstaining from wine, spirits, drugs and states of intoxication associate and come together with those abstaining from wine, spirits, drugs and states of intoxication.”
</TabItem>

768\. Sāvatthiyaṃ viharati. “Dhātusova, bhikkhave, sattā saṃsandanti samenti. Pāṇātipātino pāṇātipātīhi saddhiṃ saṃsandanti samenti; adinnādāyino adinnādāyīhi saddhiṃ saṃsandanti samenti; kāmesumicchācārino kāmesumicchācārīhi saddhiṃ saṃsandanti samenti; musāvādino musāvādīhi saddhiṃ saṃsandanti samenti; surāmerayamajjappamādaṭṭhāyino surāmerayamajjappamādaṭṭhāyīhi saddhiṃ saṃsandanti samenti.

769\. Pāṇātipātā paṭiviratā pāṇātipātā paṭiviratehi saddhiṃ saṃsandanti samenti; adinnādānā paṭiviratā adinnādānā paṭiviratehi saddhiṃ saṃsandanti samenti; kāmesumicchācārā paṭiviratā kāmesumicchācārā paṭiviratehi saddhiṃ saṃsandanti samenti; musāvādā paṭiviratā musāvādā paṭiviratehi saddhiṃ saṃsandanti samenti; surāmerayamajjappamādaṭṭhānā paṭiviratā surāmerayamajjappamādaṭṭhānā paṭiviratehi saddhiṃ saṃsandanti samentī”ti.
