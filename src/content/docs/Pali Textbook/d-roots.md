Pāḷi verbs, and most nouns, are built on a `dhātu` (root). Kaccāyana names the roots in {457} (`Bhūvādayo dhātavo`, "`bhū` and the rest are roots") and sorts them into classes (`gaṇa`), each named after its first root. The class decides which `vikaraṇa` (class suffix) stands between the root and the verb ending in the active voice {445}–{452}; in the passive and the impersonal every root takes `ya` instead {440}. The Basics chapter shows how a root such as `gamu` is turned into `gacchati`; this appendix lists the classes and the roots that Kaccāyana and the Padarūpasiddhi (Buddhappiya's commentary on Kaccāyana) use as models.

## Root classes (`dhātugaṇa`) {445}–{452}

| class | class suffix (`vikaraṇa`) | sutta | model root | sense | ▶️🤟⨀ |
| --- | --- | :-: | :-: | --- | --- |
| `bhūvādi` (the `bhū` class) | `a` | {445} | `bhū` | to be | `bhavati` |
| `rudhādi` (the `rudha` class) | `a`, with a `niggahīta` inserted before the root's final consonant | {446} | `rudha` | to obstruct | `rundhati` |
| `divādi` (the `divu` class) | `ya` | {447} | `divu` | to play, to shine | `dibbati` |
| `svādi` (the `su` class) | `ṇu`, `ṇā`, `uṇā` | {448} | `su` | to hear | `suṇoti`, `suṇāti` |
| `kiyādi` (the `kī` class) | `nā` | {449} | `kī` | to buy | `kiṇāti` |
| `gahādi` (the `gaha` class) | `ppa`, `ṇhā` | {450} | `gaha` | to take | `gheppati`, `gaṇhāti` |
| `tanādi` (the `tanu` class) | `o`, `yira` | {451} | `tanu` | to stretch | `tanoti`; `kara` gives `kayirati` |
| `curādi` (the `cura` class) | `ṇe`, `ṇaya` (also called `kārita`) | {452} | `cura` | to steal | `coreti`, `corayati` |

The `ṇ` of `ṇu`, `ṇā`, `ṇhā`, `ṇe` and `ṇaya` is an indicatory letter (`anubandha`): it is elided {523} and marks the suffix as one that strengthens (`vuddhi`) the root's vowel {483}, so `cura` + `ṇe` + `ti` gives `coreti`. The Padarūpasiddhi further divides the `bhū` class into the `bhū`, `tuda`, `hū` and `juhoti` groups, whose roots take the same suffix `a` but differ in how the root itself changes.
