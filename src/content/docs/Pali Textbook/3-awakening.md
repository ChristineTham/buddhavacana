## Paṭhamabodhisutta

1.1 Evaṁ me sutaṁ—  
⏏️(evaṁ) *️⃣👆⚧⨀③(ahaṁ) 🚻⨀①🔽(ta)(suta)  
thus | by me | heard  
Thus have I heard:

1.2 ekaṁ samayaṁ bhagavā uruvelāyaṁ viharati najjā nerañjarāya tīre bodhirukkhamūle paṭhamābhisambuddho.  
*️⃣🤟🚹⨀②(eka) 🚹⨀②(samaya) 🚹⨀①(bhagavant) 🚺⨀⑦(uruvelā) 🟢⨀🤟▶️(viharati) 🚺⨀⑥(nadī) 🚺⨀⑥(nerañjarā) 🚻⨀⑦(tīra) 🚻⨀⑦🆎(bodhirukkha+mūla) 🚹⨀①🆎(paṭhama+abhisambuddha)  
one | time | the Bhagavā | at Uruvelā | dwells | of the river | Nerañjarā | on the bank | at the foot of the bodhi tree | newly fully awakened  
At one time the Bhagavā dwells at Uruvelā on the bank of the river Nerañjarā, at the foot of the bodhi tree, newly fully awakened.

1.3 Tena kho pana samayena bhagavā sattāhaṁ ekapallaṅkena nisinno hoti vimuttisukhapaṭisaṁvedī.  
*️⃣🤟🚹⨀③(ta) ⏏️(kho) ⏏️(pana) 🚹⨀③(samaya) 🚹⨀①(bhagavant) 🚻⨀②🆎(satta+aha) 🚹⨀③🆎(eka+pallaṅka) 🚹⨀①🔽(na)(nisinna) 🟢⨀🤟▶️(hoti) 🚹⨀①🆎(vimuttisukha+paṭisaṁvedī)  
by that | indeed | moreover | time | the Bhagavā | for seven days | in one cross-legged posture | seated | is | experiencing the bliss of liberation  
At that time the Bhagavā is seated for seven days in one cross-legged posture, experiencing the bliss of liberation.

1.4 Atha kho bhagavā tassa sattāhassa accayena tamhā samādhimhā vuṭṭhahitvā rattiyā paṭhamaṁ yāmaṁ paṭiccasamuppādaṁ anulomaṁ sādhukaṁ manasākāsi:  
⏏️(atha) ⏏️(kho) 🚹⨀①(bhagavant) *️⃣🤟🚹⨀⑥(ta) 🚻⨀⑥🆎(satta+aha) 🚹⨀③(accaya) *️⃣🤟🚹⨀⑤(ta) 🚹⨀⑤(samādhi) 🔽(tvā)(vuṭṭhahati) 🚺⨀⑥(ratti) 🚹⨀②(paṭhama) 🚹⨀②(yāma) 🚹⨀②🆎(paṭicca+samuppāda) 🚹⨀②(anuloma) ⏏️(sādhukaṁ) 🟢⨀🤟⏮(manasikaroti)  
then | indeed | the Bhagavā | of that | week | after the passing | from that | concentration | having emerged | of the night | first | watch | dependent origination | in forward order | carefully | applied his mind  
Then, after the passing of that week, the Bhagavā, having emerged from that `samādhi` (concentration), in the first watch of the night carefully applied his mind to `paṭiccasamuppāda` (dependent origination) in forward order:

2.1 “Iti imasmiṁ sati idaṁ hoti, imassuppādā idaṁ uppajjati, yadidaṁ—  
⏏️(iti) *️⃣🤟🚻⨀⑦(ima) 🚻⨀⑦🔽(anta)(santa) *️⃣🤟🚻⨀①(ima) 🟢⨀🤟▶️(hoti) , *️⃣🤟🚻⨀⑥(ima) 🚹⨀⑤(uppāda) *️⃣🤟🚻⨀①(ima) 🟢⨀🤟▶️(uppajjati) , *️⃣🤟🚻⨀①(ya) *️⃣🤟🚻⨀①(ima)  
thus | in this | existing | this | is | , | of this | from the arising | this | arises | , | which | this  
Thus, when this exists, that comes to be; from the arising of this, that arises, namely:

2.2 avijjāpaccayā saṅkhārā,  
🚹⨀⑤🆎(avijjā+paccaya) 🚹⨂①(saṅkhāra)  
caused by ignorance | mental constructions  
Caused by `avijjā` (ignorance), `saṅkhārā` (mental constructions) arise;

2.3 saṅkhārapaccayā viññāṇaṁ,  
🚹⨀⑤🆎(saṅkhāra+paccaya) 🚻⨀①(viññāṇa)  
caused by mental constructions | consciousness  
Caused by mental constructions, `viññāṇaṁ` (consciousness);

2.4 viññāṇapaccayā nāmarūpaṁ,  
🚹⨀⑤🆎(viññāṇa+paccaya) 🚻⨀①🆎(nāma+rūpa)  
caused by consciousness | phenomenal objects  
Caused by consciousness, `nāmarūpaṁ` (phenomenal objects);

2.5 nāmarūpapaccayā saḷāyatanaṁ,  
🚹⨀⑤🆎(nāmarūpa+paccaya) 🚻⨀①🆎(cha+āyatana)  
caused by phenomenal objects | the six sense bases  
Caused by phenomenal objects, `saḷāyatanaṁ` (the six sense bases);

2.6 saḷāyatanapaccayā phasso,  
🚹⨀⑤🆎(saḷāyatana+paccaya) 🚹⨀①(phassa)  
caused by the six sense bases | contact  
Caused by the six sense bases, `phasso` (contact);

2.7 phassapaccayā vedanā,  
🚹⨀⑤🆎(phassa+paccaya) 🚺⨀①(vedanā)  
caused by contact | feeling  
Caused by contact, `vedanā` (feeling);

2.8 vedanāpaccayā taṇhā,  
🚹⨀⑤🆎(vedanā+paccaya) 🚺⨀①(taṇhā)  
caused by feeling | craving  
Caused by feeling, `taṇhā` (craving);

2.9 taṇhāpaccayā upādānaṁ,  
🚹⨀⑤🆎(taṇhā+paccaya) 🚻⨀①(upādāna)  
caused by craving | clinging  
Caused by craving, `upādānaṁ` (clinging);

2.10 upādānapaccayā bhavo,  
🚹⨀⑤🆎(upādāna+paccaya) 🚹⨀①(bhava)  
caused by clinging | existence  
Caused by clinging, `bhavo` (existence);

2.11 bhavapaccayā jāti,  
🚹⨀⑤🆎(bhava+paccaya) 🚺⨀①(jāti)  
caused by existence | birth  
Caused by existence, `jāti` (birth);

2.12 jātipaccayā jarāmaraṇaṁ sokaparidevadukkhadomanassupāyāsā sambhavanti.  
🚹⨀⑤🆎(jāti+paccaya) 🚻⨀①🆎(jarā+maraṇa) 🚹⨂①🆎(soka+parideva+dukkha+domanassa+upāyāsa) 🟢⨂🤟▶️(sambhavati)  
caused by birth | old age and death | sorrow, lamentation, pain, displeasure and despair | arise  
Caused by birth, old age and death, sorrow, lamentation, pain, displeasure, and despair arise.

2.13 Evametassa kevalassa dukkhakkhandhassa samudayo hotī”ti.  
⏏️(evaṁ) *️⃣🤟🚹⨀⑥(eta) 🚹⨀⑥(kevala) 🚹⨀⑥🆎(dukkha+khandha) 🚹⨀①(samudaya) 🟢⨀🤟▶️(hoti) ⏏️(iti)  
thus | of this | of the entire | of the mass of suffering | origin | is | (end quote)  
Thus is the origin of this entire mass of `dukkha` (suffering).”

3.1 Atha kho bhagavā etamatthaṁ viditvā tāyaṁ velāyaṁ imaṁ udānaṁ udānesi:  
⏏️(atha) ⏏️(kho) 🚹⨀①(bhagavant) *️⃣🤟🚹⨀②(eta) 🚹⨀②(attha) 🔽(tvā)(vidati) *️⃣🤟🚺⨀⑦(ta) 🚺⨀⑦(velā) *️⃣🤟🚹⨀②(ima) 🚹⨀②(udāna) 🟢⨀🤟⏮(udāneti)  
then | indeed | the Bhagavā | this | matter | having understood | at that | time | this | inspired utterance | uttered  
Then the Bhagavā, having understood this matter, at that time uttered this inspired utterance:

4.1 “Yadā have pātubhavanti dhammā,  
⏏️(yadā) ⏏️(have) 🟢⨂🤟▶️(pātubhavati) 🚹⨂①(dhamma)  
when | indeed | appear | phenomena

4.2 Ātāpino jhāyato brāhmaṇassa;  
🚹⨀④(ātāpī) 🚹⨀④🔽(anta)(jhāyanta) 🚹⨀④(brāhmaṇa)  
to the ardent | meditating | brāhmaṇa

4.3 Athassa kaṅkhā vapayanti sabbā,  
⏏️(atha) *️⃣🤟🚹⨀⑥(ima) 🚺⨂①(kaṅkhā) 🟢⨂🤟▶️(vapayāti) *️⃣🤟🚺⨂①(sabba)  
then | his | doubts | vanish | all

4.4 Yato pajānāti sahetudhamman”ti.  
⏏️(yato) 🟢⨀🤟▶️(pajānāti) 🚹⨀②🆎(sahetu+dhamma) ⏏️(iti)  
since | knows | phenomena with their causes | (end quote)

When indeed phenomena appear  
to the ardent, meditating brāhmaṇa,  
then all his doubts vanish,  
since he knows phenomena with their causes.”

Then, after the passing of that week, the Bhagavā, having emerged from that concentration, in the middle watch of the night carefully applied his mind to dependent origination in reverse order:

⏏️(iti) *️⃣🤟🚻⨀⑦(ima) 🚻⨀⑦🔽(anta)(asanta) *️⃣🤟🚻⨀①(ima) ⏏️(na) 🟢⨀🤟▶️(hoti) , *️⃣🤟🚻⨀⑥(ima) 🚹⨀⑤(nirodha) *️⃣🤟🚻⨀①(ima) 🔴⨀🤟▶️(nirujjhati) , *️⃣🤟🚻⨀①(ya) *️⃣🤟🚻⨀①(ima)  
thus | in this | not existing | this | not | is | , | of this | from the cessation | this | ceases | , | which | this  
Thus, when this does not exist, that does not come to be; from the cessation of this, that ceases, namely:

2.2 avijjānirodhā saṅkhāranirodho,  
🚹⨀⑤🆎(avijjā+nirodha) 🚹⨀①🆎(saṅkhāra+nirodha)  
with the cessation of ignorance | the cessation of mental constructions
