## Bodhikathā

The text is the World Tipiṭaka Edition's [Bodhikathā, Mahāvagga 1.1](https://tipitaka2500.github.io/tipitaka/3V/1/1.1.html), paragraphs 2–13; the paragraph numbers link to it. Compare the translation at [tidipa.github.io](https://tidipa.github.io/Khandhaka/1.1).

### 1. The first watch of the night

<a id="u2-1"></a>[2.1](https://tipitaka2500.github.io/tipitaka/3V/1/1.1.html#2) Tena samayena buddho bhagavā uruvelāyaṁ viharati najjā nerañjarāya tīre bodhirukkhamūle paṭhamābhisambuddho.  
*️⃣🤟🚹⨀③(ta) 🚹⨀③(samaya) 🚹⨀①(buddha) 🚹⨀①(bhagavant) 🚺⨀⑦(uruvelā) 🟢⨀🤟▶️(viharati) 🚺⨀⑥(nadī) 🚺⨀⑥(nerañjarā) 🚻⨀⑦(tīra) 🚻⨀⑦🆎(bodhirukkha+mūla) 🚹⨀①🆎(paṭhama+abhisambuddha)  
at that | time | the Buddha | the Bhagavā | at Uruvelā | dwells | of the river | Nerañjarā | on the bank | at the root of the bodhi tree | newly fully awakened  
At that time the Buddha, the Bhagavā, was dwelling at Uruvelā on the bank of the Nerañjarā river at the root of the Bodhi tree, having just attained full understanding (`abhisambuddha`).

<a id="u2-2"></a>2.2 Atha kho bhagavā bodhirukkhamūle sattāhaṁ ekapallaṅkena nisīdi vimuttisukhapaṭisaṁvedī.  
⏏️(atha) ⏏️(kho) 🚹⨀①(bhagavant) 🚻⨀⑦🆎(bodhirukkha+mūla) 🚻⨀②🆎(satta+aha) 🚹⨀③🆎(eka+pallaṅka) 🟢⨀🤟⏮(nisīdati) 🚹⨀①🆎(vimuttisukha+paṭisaṁvedī)  
then | indeed | the Bhagavā | at the root of the bodhi tree | for seven days | in one cross-legged posture | sat down | experiencing the bliss of liberation  
Then the Bhagavā sat at the root of the Bodhi tree for seven days in one cross-legged posture, experiencing the bliss of liberation (`vimuttisukhapaṭisaṁvedī`).

<a id="u2-3"></a>2.3 Atha kho bhagavā rattiyā paṭhamaṁ yāmaṁ paṭiccasamuppādaṁ anulomapaṭilomaṁ manasākāsi—  
⏏️(atha) ⏏️(kho) 🚹⨀①(bhagavant) 🚺⨀⑥(ratti) 🚹⨀②(paṭhama) 🚹⨀②(yāma) 🚹⨀②🆎(paṭicca+samuppāda) 🚹⨀②🆎(anuloma+paṭiloma) 🟢⨀🤟⏮(manasikaroti)  
then | indeed | the Bhagavā | of the night | first | watch | dependent origination | in forward and reverse order | attended to  
Then the Bhagavā, during the first watch of the night, attended to `paṭiccasamuppādaṁ` (dependent origination) in forward and reverse order —

<a id="u3-1"></a>[3.1](https://tipitaka2500.github.io/tipitaka/3V/1/1.1.html#3) “Avijjāpaccayā saṅkhārā,  
🚹⨀⑤🆎(avijjā+paccaya) 🚹⨂①(saṅkhāra)  
with ignorance as condition | mental constructions  
With `avijjā` (ignorance) as condition, `saṅkhārā` (mental constructions) [arise];

<a id="u3-2"></a>3.2 saṅkhārapaccayā viññāṇaṁ,  
🚹⨀⑤🆎(saṅkhāra+paccaya) 🚻⨀①(viññāṇa)  
with mental constructions as condition | consciousness  
With mental constructions as condition, `viññāṇaṁ` (consciousness) [arises];

<a id="u3-3"></a>3.3 viññāṇapaccayā nāmarūpaṁ,  
🚹⨀⑤🆎(viññāṇa+paccaya) 🚻⨀①🆎(nāma+rūpa)  
with consciousness as condition | phenomenal objects  
With consciousness as condition, `nāmarūpaṁ` (phenomenal objects) [arises];

<a id="u3-4"></a>3.4 nāmarūpapaccayā saḷāyatanaṁ,  
🚹⨀⑤🆎(nāmarūpa+paccaya) 🚻⨀①🆎(cha+āyatana)  
with phenomenal objects as condition | the six sense bases  
With phenomenal objects as condition, `saḷāyatanaṁ` (the six sense bases) [arise];

<a id="u3-5"></a>3.5 saḷāyatanapaccayā phasso,  
🚹⨀⑤🆎(saḷāyatana+paccaya) 🚹⨀①(phassa)

With the six sense bases as condition, `phasso` (contact) [arises];

<a id="u3-6"></a>3.6 phassapaccayā vedanā,  
🚹⨀⑤🆎(phassa+paccaya) 🚺⨀①(vedanā)  
with contact as condition | feeling  
With contact as condition, `vedanā` (feeling) [arises];

<a id="u3-7"></a>3.7 vedanāpaccayā taṇhā,  
🚹⨀⑤🆎(vedanā+paccaya) 🚺⨀①(taṇhā)

With feeling as condition, `taṇhā` (craving) [arises];

<a id="u3-8"></a>3.8 taṇhāpaccayā upādānaṁ,  
🚹⨀⑤🆎(taṇhā+paccaya) 🚻⨀①(upādāna)  
with craving as condition | fuel  
With craving as condition, `upādānaṁ` (fuel) [arises];

<a id="u3-9"></a>3.9 upādānapaccayā bhavo,  
🚹⨀⑤🆎(upādāna+paccaya) 🚹⨀①(bhava)

With fuel as condition, `bhavo` (becoming) [arises];

<a id="u3-10"></a>3.10 bhavapaccayā jāti,  
🚹⨀⑤🆎(bhava+paccaya) 🚺⨀①(jāti)  
with becoming as condition | birth  
With becoming as condition, `jāti` (birth) [arises];

<a id="u3-11"></a>3.11 jātipaccayā jarāmaraṇaṁ sokaparidevadukkhadomanassupāyāsā sambhavanti.  
🚹⨀⑤🆎(jāti+paccaya) 🚻⨀①🆎(jarā+maraṇa) 🚹⨂①🆎(soka+parideva+dukkha+domanassa+upāyāsa) 🟢⨂🤟▶️(sambhavati)  
with birth as condition | aging and death | sorrow, lamentation, pain, displeasure and despair | come into being  
With birth as condition, `jarāmaraṇaṁ` (aging and death), `soka` (sorrow), `parideva` (lamentation), `dukkha` (pain), `domanassa` (displeasure), and `upāyāsā` (despair) come into being.

<a id="u3-12"></a>3.12 Evametassa kevalassa dukkhakkhandhassa samudayo hoti.  
⏏️(evaṁ) *️⃣🤟🚹⨀⑥(eta) 🚹⨀⑥(kevala) 🚹⨀⑥🆎(dukkha+khandha) 🚹⨀①(samudaya) 🟢⨀🤟▶️(hoti)  
thus | of this | of the entire | of the mass of suffering | origin | is  
Thus is the origin of this whole mass of `dukkha` (suffering).

<a id="u6-1"></a>[6.1](https://tipitaka2500.github.io/tipitaka/3V/1/1.1.html#6) “Yadā have pātubhavanti dhammā,  
⏏️(yadā) ⏏️(have) 🟢⨂🤟▶️(pātubhavati) 🚹⨂①(dhamma)

<a id="u6-2"></a>6.2 Ātāpino jhāyato brāhmaṇassa;  
🚹⨀④(ātāpī) 🚹⨀④🔽(anta)(jhāyanta) 🚹⨀④(brāhmaṇa)

<a id="u6-3"></a>6.3 Athassa kaṅkhā vapayanti sabbā,  
⏏️(atha) *️⃣🤟🚹⨀⑥(ima) 🚺⨂①(kaṅkhā) 🟢⨂🤟▶️(vapayāti) *️⃣🤟🚺⨂①(sabba)

<a id="u6-4"></a>6.4 Yato pajānāti sahetudhamman”ti.  
⏏️(yato) 🟢⨀🤟▶️(pajānāti) 🚹⨀②🆎(sahetu+dhamma) ⏏️(iti)

When indeed `dhammā` (phenomena) become manifest  
to the ardent, contemplating brāhmaṇa;  
then all his `kaṅkhā` (doubts) disappear,  
since he understands the dependent nature of phenomena.”
