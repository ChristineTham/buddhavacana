The usage examples provided here are not exhaustive, other usages are possible.

A primary derivative (`kitanāma`) is a noun made from a root by a `kita` or
an `uṇādi` affix (`paccaya`): the root takes the changes the affix calls for,
and the new word takes a `vibhatti` ending of its own {601}. This section
covers the rules of Kaccāyana's last chapter that say what the affixes
denote: the agent {624}, the action and the object {625}–{626}, a command, a
permission, the time having come, a necessity and a debt {635}–{636}, fitness
and ability with `tuṁ` {637}, the elision of `kvi` and the changes of shape
that follow it {639}–{642}, present and past time {650}, and the future
senses, where the action is the purpose of a going {651}–{655}. Section
[6.8](../6-8-unadi-words/) lists the affixes that form individual words, and
Appendix K tables every `uṇādi` rule.

### 6.7.1 The `kita` affixes denote the agent {624}

* The `kita` affixes are added to a root in the sense of the agent (`kattu`),
  the one who does the action: `kārako` ("a doer") is `√kara` with `ṇvu`,
  `kattā` ("a doer, a maker") is `√kara` with `tu`, and `kāru` ("a craftsman")
  is `√kara` with `ṇu`.
* An affix marked with `ṇ` counts as a causative {621}: the root's vowel is
  strengthened (`vuddhi`), `kar` to `kār` {483}, and the `ṇ` itself is elided
  {523}. `ṇvu` becomes `aka` {622}, and before `tu` the `r` of `kara` becomes
  `t` {619}.

#### Example

> **kārako**

🚹⨀①🔽(ṇvu)(kārako)  
a doer  
*`kārako` ("a doer") is `√kara` with the affix `ṇvu` {624} in the sense of the agent: the `ṇ` is elided {523}, the vowel strengthened, `kār` {483}, and `vu` becomes `aka` {622}.*

1. pācako
2. kattā
3. netā
4. kāru

#### Answers

*Note: please do the exercises first before checking the answers.*

1. > **pācako**

🚹⨀①🔽(ṇvu)(pācako)  
a cook  
*`pācako` ("a cook", one who cooks) is `√paca` with the affix `ṇvu` {624} in the sense of the agent, as `kārako`: `pāc` by `vuddhi` {483}, and `vu` as `aka` {622}.*

2. > **kattā**

🚹⨀①🔽(tu)(kattā)  
a doer, a maker  
*`kattā` ("a doer, a maker") is `√kara` with the affix `tu` {624} in the sense of the agent: the `r` of `kara` becomes `t` before `tu` {619}, and the final `u` becomes `ā` in the ① ending, the `si` elided {199}.*

3. > **netā**

🚹⨀①🔽(tu)(netā)  
a leader  
*`netā` ("a leader", one who leads) is `√nī` with the affix `tu` {624} in the sense of the agent; the `ī` of the root is strengthened to `e`, and the final `u` becomes `ā` in the ① ending {199}.*

4. > **kāru**

🚹⨀①🔽(ṇu)(kāru)  
a craftsman  
*`kāru` ("a craftsman", a maker) is `√kara` with the affix `ṇu` {624} in the sense of the agent: the `ṇ` is elided {523} and the vowel strengthened {483}; the ① ending `si` is elided after the `u` {220}.*

### 6.7.2 The `kicca` affixes, `tta` and the `kha`-type affixes denote the action and the object {625}

* The `kicca` affixes (`tabba`, `anīya`, `ṇya` and the rest, Appendix J), the
  affix `tta` (the affix of the past participle, written `ta` in Appendix J)
  and the affixes with the sense of `kha` are used in the sense of the action
  itself (`bhāva`) and in the sense of the object (`kamma`).
* In the sense of the action the derived word is neuter singular and the
  agent stands in the ③ ending (`bhavatā`, "by you"); in the sense of the
  object it agrees with the object in gender and number.

#### Example

> **bhottabbo** odano bhavatā

🚹⨀①🔽(tabba)(bhottabbo) 🚹⨀①(odano) 🚹⨀③(bhavatā)  
is to be eaten / the rice / by you  
*`bhottabbo odano bhavatā` ("the rice is to be eaten by you"): `bhottabbo` is `√bhuja` with the `kicca` affix `tabba` {625} in the sense of the object (`kamma`), agreeing with `odano`; the agent `bhavatā` stands in the ③ ending.*

1. sayitabbaṁ bhavatā
2. pacito odano bhavatā
3. asitaṁ bhavatā
4. sussayo bhavatā

#### Answers

*Note: please do the exercises first before checking the answers.*

1. > **sayitabbaṁ** bhavatā

🚻⨀①🔽(tabba)(sayitabbaṁ) 🚹⨀③(bhavatā)  
lying down is to be done / by you  
*`sayitabbaṁ bhavatā` ("lying down is to be done by you"): `sayitabbaṁ` is `√sī` with `tabba` {625} in the sense of the action (`bhāva`), neuter singular, with `i` inserted between root and affix {605}.*

2. > **pacito** odano bhavatā

🚹⨀①🔽(tta)(pacito) 🚹⨀①(odano) 🚹⨀③(bhavatā)  
was cooked / the rice / by you  
*`pacito odano bhavatā` ("the rice was cooked by you"): `pacito` is `√paca` with `tta` {625} in the sense of the object, agreeing with `odano`, with `i` inserted {605}.*

3. > **asitaṁ** bhavatā

🚻⨀①🔽(tta)(asitaṁ) 🚹⨀③(bhavatā)  
eating was done / by you  
*`asitaṁ bhavatā` ("eating was done by you"): `asitaṁ` is `√asa` with `tta` {625} in the sense of the action, neuter singular.*

4. > **sussayo** bhavatā

🚹⨀①🔽(kha)(sussayo) 🚹⨀③(bhavatā)  
lying is easy / by you  
*`sussayo bhavatā` ("lying is easy for you"): `sussayo` is `su` with `√sī` and the affix `kha` {625} in the sense of the action, "easy to lie on"; the affix leaves only `a`, its `kh` elided {517}, and the `s` is doubled after `su` {28}.*

### 6.7.3 `tta` denotes the agent when the object stands in the ② ending {626}

* When the object (`kamma`) stands in the ② ending, the affix `tta` is used in
  the sense of the agent, not of the object: in `dānaṁ dinno devadatto`
  ("Devadatta has given a gift") `dinno` agrees with the agent `devadatto`,
  and the object `dānaṁ` keeps the ② ending.
* This is an exception (`apavāda`) to {625}, under which `tta` denotes the
  object.

#### Example

> dānaṁ **dinno** devadatto

🚻⨀②(dānaṁ) 🚹⨀①🔽(tta)(dinno) 🚹⨀①(devadatto)  
a gift / has given / Devadatta  
*`dānaṁ dinno devadatto` ("Devadatta has given a gift"): `dinno` is `√dā` with `tta` {626} in the sense of the agent, agreeing with `devadatto`, since the object `dānaṁ` stands in the ② ending; the root's `ā` with the affix becomes `inna` {582}.*

🚻⨀②(sīlaṁ) 🚹⨀①🔽(tta)(rakkhito) 🚹⨀①(devadatto)  
virtue / has kept / Devadatta  
*`sīlaṁ rakkhito devadatto` ("Devadatta has kept virtue"): `rakkhito` is `√rakkha` with `tta` {626} in the sense of the agent, the object `sīlaṁ` in the ② ending, with `i` inserted {605}.*

2. > bhattaṁ **bhutto** devadatto

🚻⨀②(bhattaṁ) 🚹⨀①🔽(tta)(bhutto) 🚹⨀①(devadatto)

*`bhattaṁ bhutto devadatto` ("Devadatta has eaten the meal"): `bhutto` is `√bhuja` with `tta` {626} in the sense of the agent; the root's final `j` is dropped and the `t` doubled {578}.*

3. > garuṁ **upāsito** devadatto

🚹⨀②(garuṁ) 🚹⨀①🔽(tta)(upāsito) 🚹⨀①(devadatto)  
the teacher / has attended on / Devadatta  
*`garuṁ upāsito devadatto` ("Devadatta has attended on the teacher"): `upāsito` is `upa` with `√āsa` and `tta` {626} in the sense of the agent, the object `garuṁ` in the ② ending.*

### 6.7.4 The `kicca` affixes in the senses of command, permission and due time {635}

* The `kicca` affixes are also used in the senses of a command (`pesa`), a
  permission (`atisagga`) and the time having come (`pattakāla`). The vutti
  gives each sense with `tabba` and with `anīya`; the derived word agrees with
  its noun, and the agent stands in the ③ ending.

#### Example

> **kattabbaṁ** kammaṁ bhavatā

🚻⨀①🔽(tabba)(kattabbaṁ) 🚻⨀①(kammaṁ) 🚹⨀③(bhavatā)  
is to be done / the work / by you  
*`kattabbaṁ kammaṁ bhavatā` ("the work is to be done by you"), a command: `kattabbaṁ` is `√kara` with the `kicca` affix `tabba` {635}, agreeing with `kammaṁ`; the `r` of `kara` becomes `t` before `tabba` {620}.*

🚻⨀①🔽(anīya)(karaṇīyaṁ) 🚻⨀①(kiccaṁ) 🚹⨀③(bhavatā)  
is to be done / the task / by you  
*`karaṇīyaṁ kiccaṁ bhavatā` ("the task is to be done by you"), a command: `karaṇīyaṁ` is `√kara` with the `kicca` affix `anīya` {635}, agreeing with `kiccaṁ`, its `n` becoming `ṇ`.*

2. > **bhottabbaṁ** bhojanaṁ bhavatā

🚻⨀①🔽(tabba)(bhottabbaṁ) 🚻⨀①(bhojanaṁ) 🚹⨀③(bhavatā)  
may be eaten / the food / by you  
*`bhottabbaṁ bhojanaṁ bhavatā` ("the food may be eaten by you"), a permission: `bhottabbaṁ` is `√bhuja` with `tabba` {635}; the `u` is strengthened to `o` {485}, the root's final `j` dropped and the `t` doubled {578}.*

3. > **bhojanīyaṁ** bhojanaṁ bhavatā

🚻⨀①🔽(anīya)(bhojanīyaṁ) 🚻⨀①(bhojanaṁ) 🚹⨀③(bhavatā)  
may be eaten / the food / by you  
*`bhojanīyaṁ bhojanaṁ bhavatā` ("the food may be eaten by you"), a permission: `bhojanīyaṁ` is `√bhuja` with `anīya` {635}, the `u` strengthened to `o` {485}.*

4. > **ajjhayitabbaṁ** ajjheyyaṁ bhavatā

🚻⨀①🔽(tabba)(ajjhayitabbaṁ) 🚻⨀①(ajjheyyaṁ) 🚹⨀③(bhavatā)  
is now to be studied / the lesson / by you  
*`ajjhayitabbaṁ ajjheyyaṁ bhavatā` ("the lesson is now to be studied by you"), the time having come: `ajjhayitabbaṁ` is `adhi` with `√i` and `tabba` {635}, `i` inserted {605}, the root's `i` strengthened to `e` {485} and then `aya` before the vowel {514}, and `adhi` becoming `ajjh` {45}.*

### 6.7.5 `ṇī` and the `kicca` affixes in the senses of necessity, debt and the like {636}

* The affix `ṇī` is used in the senses of necessity (`avassaka`) and of a
  debtor (`adhamiṇa`), and so are the `kicca` affixes: `kārī` ("one who must
  do") with `asi` ("you are") makes `kārīsi`, "you are bound to do".
* As a `ṇ`-marked affix, `ṇī` strengthens the root's vowel, `kār`, `hār`
  {483}, and its `ṇ` is elided {523}; after the final `ī` the ① ending `si` is
  elided {220}.

#### Example

> **kārī’si** me kammaṁ avassaṁ

🚹⨀①🔽(ṇī)(kārī) 🟢⨀🤘▶️(asi) *️⃣👆⚧⨀⑥(me) 🚻⨀②(kammaṁ) ⏏️(avassaṁ)  
one who must do / you are / my / work / without fail  
*`kārīsi me kammaṁ avassaṁ` ("you are bound to do my work without fail"): `kārī` is `√kara` with the affix `ṇī` {636} in the sense of necessity (`avassaka`), the `ṇ` elided {523} and the vowel strengthened {483}; `kārī asi` is joined by sandhi, the `a` of `asi` elided after `ī` {13}.*

*`hārīsi me bhāraṁ avassaṁ` ("you are bound to carry my load without fail"): `hārī` is `√hara` with `ṇī` {636} in the sense of necessity, `hār` by `vuddhi` {483}.*

2. > **dāyī’si** me sataṁ iṇaṁ

🚹⨀①🔽(ṇī)(dāyī) 🟢⨀🤘▶️(asi) *️⃣👆⚧⨀④(me) 🚻⨀②(sataṁ) 🚻⨀②(iṇaṁ)

*`dātabbaṁ me bhavatā sataṁ iṇaṁ` ("a hundred, a debt, is to be paid me by you"): `dātabbaṁ` is `√dā` with the `kicca` affix `tabba` {636} in the sense of a debtor.*

*`kāriyaṁ me bhavatā sayanaṁ` ("a bed is to be made for me by you"): `kāriyaṁ` is `√kara` with the `kicca` affix `ṇya` {636}, the `ṇ` elided {523} and the vowel strengthened, `kār` {483}.*

### 6.7.6 `tuṁ` with any root when `arahati`, `sakkā` and the like are used {637}

* When a word such as `araha` ("fit, worthy"), `sakka` ("able, possible") or
  `bhabba` ("capable") is used in the construction (`payoga`), the affix `tuṁ`
  is added to any root: `arahā bhavaṁ vattuṁ`, "you, sir, are fit to speak".
* A word in `tuṁ` is uninflected: the `vibhatti` ending added to it is elided
  {221}. Before `tuṁ` the final of `gamu` becomes `n` {596}, and `i` is
  inserted after `bhū` and the like {605}.

#### Example

> arahā bhavaṁ **vattuṁ**

🚹⨀①(arahā) 🚹⨀①(bhavaṁ) 🔽(tuṁ)(vattuṁ)  
fit / you, sir / to speak  
*`arahā bhavaṁ vattuṁ` ("you, sir, are fit to speak"): `vattuṁ` is `√vada` with the affix `tuṁ` {637}, used because `arahā` ("fit") stands in the construction.*

🚹⨀①(arahā) 🚹⨀①(bhavaṁ) 🔽(tuṁ)(kattuṁ)

*`arahā bhavaṁ kattuṁ` ("you, sir, are fit to act"): `kattuṁ` is `√kara` with `tuṁ` {637}, the `r` of `kara` becoming `t` before `tuṁ` {620}.*

2. > sakkā bhavaṁ **hantuṁ**

⏏️(sakkā) 🚹⨀①(bhavaṁ) 🔽(tuṁ)(hantuṁ)

*`sakkā bhavaṁ hantuṁ` ("you can strike"): `hantuṁ` is `√hana` with `tuṁ` {637}, used because `sakkā` ("it is possible") stands in the construction.*

3. > sakkā bhavaṁ **gantuṁ**

⏏️(sakkā) 🚹⨀①(bhavaṁ) 🔽(tuṁ)(gantuṁ)

*`sakkā bhavaṁ gantuṁ` ("you can go"): `gantuṁ` is `√gamu` with `tuṁ` {637}; the `m` of `gamu` becomes `n` before `tuṁ` {596}.*

*`bhabbo bhavaṁ janetuṁ` ("you are capable of begetting"): `janetuṁ` is `√jana` with the causative `ṇe` and `tuṁ` {637}, used because `bhabbo` ("capable") stands in the construction.*

### 6.7.7 The elision of `kvi` {639}

* The affix `kvi` is elided altogether, and the word is then formed again as
  it stands: nothing of the affix remains, and the root, its final consonant
  elided before `kvi` {615}, follows a prefix or a noun. `bhujena kuṭilena
  gacchati` ("it goes with a crooked coil") gives `bhujago`, "a snake", from
  `bhuja` and `√gamu`.
* The vutti glosses each word with the sentence it stands for: `sayambhū`
  ("self-become") is `sayaṁ attanā bhavati`, `sabhā` ("an assembly hall") is
  `saha bhāsanti ettha`.

#### Example

> bhujena kuṭilena gacchati → **bhujago**

🚹⨀③(bhujena) 🚹⨀③(kuṭilena) 🟢⨀🤟▶️(gacchati) → 🚹⨀①🔽(kvi)(bhujago)  
with a coil / crooked / it goes → a snake  
*`bhujena kuṭilena gacchati` ("it goes with a crooked coil") becomes `bhujago` ("a snake"): `bhuja` with `√gamu` and the affix `kvi` {639}, the root's final `m` elided before `kvi` {615} and `kvi` itself elided {639}, so that only `ga` remains.*

*Note: please do the exercises first before checking the answers.*

1. > kammaṁ **karonto** vajati

🚻⨀②(kammaṁ) 🚹⨀①🔽(ntu)(karonto) 🟢⨀🤟▶️(vajati)  
the work / doing / he goes  
*`kammaṁ karonto vajati` ("he goes, doing the work"): `karonto` is `√kara` with the affix `ntu` {655} in the future sense, the object `kammaṁ` in the ② ending; the `o` of the `tanu` class stands before the affix {451}, and `ntu` takes `a` before `si` {94}.*

2. > kammaṁ **kurumāno** vajati

🚻⨀②(kammaṁ) 🚹⨀①🔽(māna)(kurumāno) 🟢⨀🤟▶️(vajati)  
the work / doing / he goes  
*`kammaṁ kurumāno vajati` ("he goes, doing the work"): `kurumāno` is `√kara` with the affix `māna` {655} in the future sense; the `o` of the `tanu` class becomes `u` {511}, and the `a` of `kara` also becomes `u` {512}.*

3. > kammaṁ **karāno** vajati

🚻⨀②(kammaṁ) 🚹⨀①🔽(āna)(karāno) 🟢⨀🤟▶️(vajati)  
the work / doing / he goes  
*`kammaṁ karāno vajati` ("he goes, doing the work"): `karāno` is `√kara` with the affix `āna` {655} in the future sense, the object `kammaṁ` in the ② ending.*

4. > bhojanaṁ bhuñjissati → bhojanaṁ **bhuñjissaṁ** vajati

🚻⨀②(bhojanaṁ) 🟢⨀🤟⏭(bhuñjissati) → 🚻⨀②(bhojanaṁ) 🚹⨀①🔽(ssaṁ)(bhuñjissaṁ) 🟢⨀🤟▶️(vajati)  
the meal / he will eat → the meal / going to eat / he goes  
*`bhojanaṁ bhuñjissati` ("he will eat the meal") gives `bhojanaṁ bhuñjissaṁ vajati` ("he goes, going to eat the meal"): `bhuñjissaṁ` is `√bhuja` with the affix `ssaṁ` {655} in the future sense, as `karissaṁ`; the niggahīta inserted in the `rudha` class {446} becomes `ñ` before `j` {31}.*
