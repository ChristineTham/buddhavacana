The `uṇādi` suffixes {642}–{673} are the `kita` affixes "beginning with `uṇ`": a long tail of suffixes, each attached to a few named roots, that form individual nouns (`kāru` "craftsman", `samatha` "calm", `geha` "house", `vitaṇḍā` "sophistry" …). Kaccāyana's last chapter opens with the general rules for the sense of the `kita` suffixes {624}–{641} and then lists the `uṇādi` suffixes themselves. Each row gives the sutta number `{N}`, the sutta's own words, a one-line summary and the example words Kaccāyana cites; after "not" come the counter-examples, where the rule does not apply. The summaries use the book's symbols: ①–⑦ for the inflection forms, ⨀ singular, ⨂ plural, 🚹🚺🚻 for the genders. The full rules, with every derivation, are at <https://kaccayana.github.io/kaccayana/8-unadikappa>.

## The senses of the `kita` suffixes {624}–{641}

| sutta | rule | summary | examples |
| :-: | --- | --- | --- |
| {624} | `Kattari kita` | the `kita` suffixes denote the agent | `ayaṁ kho brahmadatto kāsirājā bahuno amhākaṁ anatthassa kārako`, `sarasi tvaṁ, dabba, evarūpaṁ kattā`, `netā vinetā anunetā, tādiso labhate yasaṁ`, `kāru` |
| {625} | `Bhāvakammesu kiccattakkhatthā` | the `kicca` suffixes, `tta` and the `kha`-type suffixes denote the action and the object | `na, bhikkhave, ūnavīsativasso puggalo upasampādetabbo`, `asitaṁ pītaṁ khāyitaṁ sāyitaṁ sammā pariṇāmaṁ gacchati`, `upasampādetabbaṁ upasampādanīyaṁ bhavatā`, `sayitabbaṁ bhavatā` |
| {626} | `Kammani dutiyāya tto` | `tta` denotes the agent when the object stands in the second form | `dānaṁ dinno devadatto`, `sīlaṁ rakkhito devadatto`, `bhattaṁ bhutto devadatto`, `garuṁ upāsito devadatto` |
| {627} | `Khyādīhi mana ma ca to vā` | `mana` after `khi` and the like; and optionally `t` for its `m` | `dhajo rathassa paññāṇaṁ, dhūmo paññāṇamaggino`, `attā hi attano nātho`, `khemo`, `bhemo` |
| {628} | `Samādīhi tha mā` | `tha` and `ma` after `samu` and the like | `samatho ca vipassanā ca`, `āpadāsu kho, mahārāja, thāmo veditabbo`, `abhikkantā, bhante, ratti, nikkhanto paṭhamo yāmo`, `samatho` |
| {629} | `Gahassu’padhasse vā` | optionally `e` for the penultimate `a` of `gaha` | `nanu nāma, tāta sudinna, sakaṁ gehaṁ gantabbaṁ`, `gehaṁ`, `gahaṁ` |
| {630} | `Masussa sussa cchara ccherā` | `cchara`, `cchera` for the `su` of `masu` | `saṅgāhako mittakaro, vadaññū vītamaccharo`, `maccharo`, `macchero`, `masu` |
| {631} | `Āpubbacarassa ca` | and [`cchariya`, `cchara`, `cchera`] for `cara` after `ā`, the `ā` shortened | `acchariyaṁ, bhante, abbhutaṁ, bhante`, `passa mātali accheraṁ, cittaṁ kammaphalaṁ idaṁ`, `balavā puriso appakasireneva accharaṁ pahareyya`, `acchariyaṁ` |
| {632} | `Ala kala salehi layā` | `la` and `ya` after `ala`, `kala`, `sala` | `allaṁ kaṭṭhaṁ sasnehaṁ udake nikkhittaṁ`, `kallaṁ nu taṁ samanupassituṁ`, `na tāvāhaṁ imaṁ sallaṁ āharissāmi`, `allaṁ` |
| {633} | `Yāṇa lāṇā` | `yāṇa` and `lāṇa` after `kala` and `sala` | `kataṁ tayā kalyāṇaṁ, akataṁ tayā pāpaṁ`, `paṭisallānā vuṭṭhito`, `kalyāṇaṁ`, `paṭisalyāṇaṁ` |
| {634} | `Mathissa thassa lo ca` | `l` for the `th` of `matha`; and the insertion of `k` | `rojo mallo āyasmato ānandassa sahāyo hoti`, `mallo`, `mallaṁ` |
| {635} | `Pesātisaggapattakālesukiccā` | the `kicca` suffixes in the senses of command, permission and due time | `kattabbaṁ kusalaṁ, caritabbaṁ brahmacariyaṁ`, `natthi cassa kiñci uttari karaṇīyaṁ`, `kattabbaṁ kammaṁ bhavatā`, `karaṇīyaṁ kiccaṁ bhavatā` |
| {636} | `Avassakā’dhamiṇesu ṇī ca` | `ṇī`, and the `kicca` suffixes, in the senses of necessity and of a debtor | `sīlesu paripūrakārī hoti samādhismiṁ mattaso kārī`, `sace, gahapati, bhojanaṁ dātabbaṁ detha`, `kārīsi me kammaṁ avassaṁ`, `hārīsi me bhāraṁ avassaṁ` |
| {637} | `Arahasakkādīhi tuṁ` | `tuṁ` with any root when `arahati`, `sakkā` and the like are used | `arahā bhavaṁ vattuṁ`, `na hi so sakkā hantuṁ`, `arahā bhavaṁ kattuṁ`, `sakkā bhavaṁ hantuṁ` |
| {638} | `Vajādīhipabbajjādayo nippajjante` | `pabbajjā` and the like are formed from `vaja` and the like as they stand | `idheva me maraṇaṁ bhavissati pabbajjā vā`, `saddhā dutiyā purisassa hoti`, `pabbajjā`, `ejjā` |
| {639} | `Kvilopo ca` | and the elision of `kvi`; the words again formed as they stand | `ahamasmi brahmā mahābrahmā abhibhū anabhibhūto`, `nesā sabhā yattha na santi santo`, `bhujanto gacchatīti bhujago; urena gacchatīti urago`, `vibhū` |
| {640} | `Sacajānaṁ kagā ṇānubandhe` | `k`, `g` for a root-final `c`, `j` before a `ṇ`-marked suffix | `rāgo, doso, moho — ime kho, āvuso, tayo dhammā`, `sukho viveko tuṭṭhassa, sutadhammassa passato`, `katamo ca, bhikkhave, soko`, `oko` |
| {641} | `Nudādīhi yuṇvūnamanānanākānanakā sakāritehi ca` | `ana`, `ānana`, `aka`, `ānanaka` for `yu` and `ṇvu` after `nuda` etc., and after causatives | `ahañcamhi na bhagavato sāvako`, `laddhā hi so upādānaṁ, mahā hutvāna pāvako`, `eko attavasī khippaṁ, pavisissāmi kānanaṁ`, `panūdano` |

## The `uṇādi` suffixes {642}–{673}

| sutta | rule | summary | examples |
| :-: | --- | --- | --- |
| {642} | `I ya ta ma ki e sāna’mantassaro dīghaṁ kvaci du sa ssa guṇaṁ do raṁ sakkhī ca` | lengthened pronoun + `disa`, `risa`, `dikkha`, `dī`: `īdiso`, `tādiso`, `kīdiso` | `kīdiso, bhaṇe dovārika, vejjo`, `so tādiso sīlavisuddhiyā ṭhito`, `īdiso nirayo āsi, yattha dūsī apaccatha`, `na mādisā tuyhaṁ dadanti jamma` |
| {643} | `Bhyādīhi mati budhi pūjādīhi ca tto` | `tta` after `bhī` etc., and after roots of thinking, knowing and honouring | `disvāna bhīto aṭṭhāsi`, `sammato saṁghena āyasmā dabbo mallaputto senāsanapaññāpako ca bhattuddesako ca`, `sakkato garukato mānito pūjito apacito`, `bhīto` |
| {644} | `Vepu sī dava vamu ku dā bhū hvādīhi thu ttima ṇimā nibbatte` | `thu`, `ttima`, `ṇima`, "produced by", after `vepu`, `sī`, `dava`, `vamu`, `ku`, `dā`, `bhū`, `hu` etc. | `vepathu`, `sayathu`, `davathu`, `vamathu` |
| {645} | `Akkose namhāni` | `āni` in reproach, when `na` is present | `agamāni te jamma desaṁ`, `akarāṇi te jamma kammaṁ`, `adhammena tuvaṁ jamma, brahmadattassa kujjhasi` — not `vipatti te jamma`, `vikati te jamma` |
| {646} | `Ekādito sakissa kkhattuṁ` | `kkhattuṁ` for `sakiṁ` after `eka` and the other numerals: "once", "twice" | `rattiyā paṭhamaṁ yāmaṁ tikkhattuṁ saddamanussāvehi`, `pañcakkhattuṁ codetvā dvikkhattuṁ ṭhātabbaṁ`, `bhagavato kāyaṁ sattakkhattuṁ bhogehi parikkhipitvā`, `ekakkhattuṁ` |
| {647} | `Sunassunassoṇa vānuvānūnunakhunānā` | `soṇo`, `svāno`, `suvāno`, `sūno`, `sunakho`, `suno`, `sā`, `sāno`: the shapes of `suna`, "dog" | `kukkuṭā sūkarā soṇā siṅgālā`, `jeguccho sunakho paṭikūlo`, `khādanti naṁ suvānā ca, siṅgālā ca vakā kimī`, `soṇo` |
| {648} | `Taruṇassa susu ca` | and `susu` for `taruṇa`, "young" | `daharo tvaṁ, bhikkhu, pabbajito susu kāḷakeso, bhadrena yobbanena samannāgato`, `samaṇo khalu, bho, gotamo daharova samāno yuvā susukāḷakeso`, `susu kāḷakeso`, `taruṇa` |
| {649} | `Yuvassuvassuvuvānunūnā` | `uva`, `uvāna`, `una`, `ūna` for the `uva` of `yuva` | `bhikkhu daharo hoti yuvā susu kāḷakeso`, `yuvā`, `yuvāno`, `yuno` |
| {650} | `Kāle vattamānātīte ṇvādayo` | `ṇu` and the rest in present and past time | `somo yamo candimā vāyu sūriyo`, `yaṁ taṁ jātaṁ bhūtaṁ saṅkhataṁ palokadhammaṁ`, `akāsi, karotīti kāru`, `agacchi, gacchatīti vāyu` |
| {651} | `Bhavissati gamādīhi ṇī ghiṇa` | `ṇī`, `ghiṇ` after `gamu` and the like, in the future sense | `so kuhiṁ gāmī bhavissati`, `āyatiṁ gamituṁ sīlaṁ yassa, so hotīti gāmī`, `āyatiṁ bhajituṁ sīlaṁ yassa, so hotīti bhājī`, `āyatiṁ passavituṁ sīlaṁ yassa, so hotīti passāvi` |
| {652} | `Kriyāyaṁ ṇvu tavo` | `ṇvu`, `tu` when an action is the purpose, in the future sense | `bahuno amhākaṁ anatthassa kārako`, `‘‘karissa’’nti kārako vajati`, `‘‘bhuñjissa’’nti bhottā vajati` |
| {653} | `Bhāvavācimhi catutthī` | the fourth form ④ with a word of action, for the purpose | `pañcasu kāmaguṇesu bhogāya cittaṁ namati`, `pacissate, pacanaṁ vā pāko, pākāya vajati`, `bhuñjissate, bhojanaṁ vā bhogo, bhogāya vajati`, `naccissate, naccanaṁ vā naccaṁ, naccāya vajati` |
| {654} | `Kammani ṇo` | `ṇa` with the object as first member, in the future sense | `nagaraṁ karissati nagarakāro vajati`, `sāliṁ lāvissati sālilāvo vajati`, `dhaññaṁ vapissati dhaññavāpo vajati`, `bhogaṁ dadissati bhogadāyo vajati` |
| {655} | `Sese ssaṁ ntumānānā` | `ssaṁ`, `ntu`, `māna`, `āna` in the remaining [future] sense | `puññāni karonto abhiramassu`, `aparihīno kālaṁ kurumāno`, `na bhuñjamāno sabbaṁ hatthaṁ mukhe pakkhipissāmi`, `yathāpi bhikkhu bhikkhanto` |
| {656} | `Chadādīhi ta traṇa` | `ta`, `traṇ` after `chada` and the like | `chattaṁ nāma tīṇi chattāni`, `nettaṁ yācitumāgato`, `tantaṁ vināti`, `hirī īsā mano yottaṁ` |
| {657} | `Vadādīhi ṇitto gaṇe` | `ṇitta` after `vada` and the like in the sense of a group | `cārittaṁ atha vārittaṁ`, `kulesu cārittaṁ āpajjeyya`, `vāditānaṁ gaṇo vādittaṁ`, `cārittaṁ` |
| {658} | `Midādīhi ttitiyo` | `tti`, `ti` after `mida` and the like | `metti sandhīyate puna`, `cattāro purisā sarahatthā patti`, `yāva ratti vibhāyati`, `pāyantī nāma mātā vā hoti dhāti vā` |
| {659} | `Usu ranja daṁsānaṁ daṁsassa daḍḍhoḍha ṭhā ca` | `daḍḍha` for `daṁsa`; and `ḍha`, `ṭha` after `usu`, `ranja` | `rājāva raṭṭhaṁ vijitaṁ pahāya`, `gāmo agginā daḍḍho hoti`, `usīyate uḍḍho`, `ranjanti etthāti raṭṭhaṁ` |
| {660} | `Sūvusānamūvusānamatothoca` | `at` for the `ū`, `u`, `asa` of `sū`, `vu`, `asa`; and `tha` | `satthaṁ vā āhara, visaṁ vā khāda`, `suddhaṁ vatthaṁ apagatakāḷakaṁ`, `vadeyyātha, bhante, yena attho`, `savati hiṁsati etenāti satthaṁ` |
| {661} | `Ranjudādīhi dha didda kirā kvaci jadalopoca` | `dha`, `da`, `idda`, `ka`, `ira` after `ranja`, `uda` and the like; sometimes `j`, `d` elided | `randhaṁ na passanti samekkhamānā`, `sabbo samuddo asīmo`, `nakkhattānaṁ mukhaṁ cando`, `mando hāsetabbo` |
| {662} | `Paṭito hissa heraṇa hīraṇa` | `heraṇ`, `hīraṇ` for `hi` after `paṭi` | `pāṭiheraṁ daṭṭhukāmā`, `disvāna taṁ pāṭihīraṁ`, `paṭhamaṁ pāṭihāriyaṁ`, `paṭipakkhe madditvā gacchati pavattatīti pāṭiheraṁ` |
| {663} | `Kaḍyādīhi ko` | `ka` after `kaḍi` and the like | `na taṁ kaṇḍaṁ jānāmi yenamhi viddho`, `kuṭṭhaṁ, gaṇḍo, kilāso, soso, apamāro`, `oṭṭho meṇḍo goṇo gadrabho`, `eraṇḍo anto rukkhānaṁ` |
| {664} | `Khādāmagamānaṁ kha ndha’ndha gandhā` | `khandha`, `andha`, `gandha` for `khāda`, `ama`, `gamu` | `yo mahantataro khandho so palujjeyya`, `na, bhikkhave, andho pabbājetabbo`, `na vaṇṇo na gandho na raso paññāyati`, `jātijarāmaraṇādīhi saṁsāradukkhehi khāditabboti khandho` |
| {665} | `Paṭādīhyalaṁ` | `ala` after `paṭa` and the like | `paṭhamaṁ kalalaṁ hoti`, `mahantaṁ ninnaṁ pallalaṁ`, `caṇḍālo veno nesādo rathakāro pukkuso`, `taṁ jaññā vasalo iti` |
| {666} | `Puthassa puthu pathā mo vā` | `puthu`, `patha` for `putha`; in some places `ama` | `nabhato puthavī yathā`, `jātā ca pathavī ajātā ca pathavī`, `ayaṁ, bhikkhave, paṭhamo mahācoro`, `puthujjano tathāgatassa vaṇṇaṁ vadamāno` |
| {667} | `Sasvādīhi tu davo` | `tu`, `du` after `sasu` and the like | `gahitā tena sattunā`, `odano, kummāso, sattu, maccho, maṁsaṁ`, `daddu kacchu ca me natthi`, `aññe satte sasati hiṁsatīti sattu` |
| {668} | `Cyādīhi īvaro` | `īvara` after `ci`, `pā`, `dhā` and the like | `bahuṁ tumhākaṁ cīvaraṁ uppannaṁ`, `cīyatīti cīvaraṁ`, `pivatīti pīvaro`, `pātabbaṁ rakkhitabbanti vā pīvaraṁ` |
| {669} | `Munādīhi ci` | `i` after `muna` and the like, and after nominal lemmas | `bhavasaṅkhāramavassaji muni`, `joti nāma aggi vuccati`, `kavi gāthānamāsayo`, `saddhā, ruci, anussavo` |
| {670} | `Vidādīhyūro` | `ūra` after `vida` and the like | `dūro kho, bhante, maggo`, `majjārasamāgato viya undūro`, `haṁsā koñcā mayūrā ca`, `vidituṁ alanti vidūro` |
| {671} | `Hanādīhi nu ṇutavo` | `nu`, `ṇu`, `tu` after `hana` and the like | `sīlaṁ setu mahesakkho`, `taṁ kissa hetu`, `saṅkhatā ca dhātu asaṅkhatā ca dhātu`, `tuvaṁ satthā ca ketu ca` |
| {672} | `Kuṭādīhi ṭho` | `ṭha` after `kuṭa` and the like | `santi te evarūpā ābādhā — kuṭṭhaṁ, gaṇḍo`, `koṭṭho mayhaṁ na vijjati`, `tattha jātakaṁ kaṭṭhaṁ vā lataṁ vā chindati`, `aṅgamaṅgaṁ kuṭati chindatīti kuṭṭhaṁ` |
| {673} | `Manu pūra suṇādīhi ussa nusisā` | `ussa`, `nusa`, `isa` after `manu`, `pūra`, `suṇa` and the like | `manusso tasmiṁ papatati`, `yaṁ mānuso vissase amānusamhi`, `viññū puriso iti paṭisañcikkhati`, `tasmā hi paṇḍito poso` |
