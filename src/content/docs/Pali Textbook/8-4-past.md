Almost every verb of chapters 3 and 4 is in the past. Kaccāyana has three
sets of past endings: the `ajjatanī` (from `ajja`, "today") {419}, the
`hiyyattanī` (from `hiyyo`, "yesterday") {418} and the `parokkhā` (the
"unseen", unwitnessed past) {417}; the texts use the first for any past event.

Ask three things of a past form: its ending, which gives the person and number
as in [8.1](../8-1-reading-a-verb/); whether an `a` is put before the root,
`a-gamī` {519}; and whether an `s` stands before the ending, `aho-s-i` {491}.

### The endings of the `ajjatanī`

Kaccāyana's active endings are `ī uṁ, o ttha, iṁ mhā`, in the table's order
{428}; on the page the `ī` is nearly always short {517}. `-si` is also the
present "you" ending (`gacchasi` {423}), `-iṁ`, `-uṁ` also noun endings
(`aggiṁ`, `bhikkhuṁ`). [Appendix I](../i-verbs/) tables the rules.

| what you see | person | number | in the texts | rule |
| --- | --- | :-: | --- | :-: |
| `-i` (`-ī`), `-si` | third | ⨀ | `nisīdi`, `udapādi`, `saṅkampi`; `ahosi` | {428}, {517} |
| `-uṁ`, `-iṁsu`, `-aṁsu` | third | ⨂ | `abhinanduṁ`, `saddamanussāvesuṁ`, `agamaṁsu` | {428}, {504}, {517} |
| `-i`, as the third | second | ⨀ | Kaccāyana's `māsu tvaṁ akari pāpaṁ` ("do not do evil") | {428}, {491} |
| `-ittha` | second | ⨂ | `agacchittha`, "you went" (chapter 1) | {428}, {516} |
| `-iṁ` | first | ⨀ | `paccaññāsiṁ`, `paṭilabhiṁ` | {428} |
| `-imha`, `-imhā` | first | ⨂ | `agacchimhā`, "we went" (chapter 1) | {428}, {516} |

### The `a` before the root

An `a` is "sometimes" put before the root in the three pasts, `agamī` {519}:
`avoca`, `ahosi`, `aññāsi` have it, `nisīdi`, `āmantesi`, `abhinanduṁ` do not.
After a prefix it sits between prefix and root: `ud-a-pādi`, `pacc-a-ññāsiṁ`.

### Shapes to know by sight

| what you see | root | what happened | rule |
| --- | :-: | --- | :-: |
| `ahosi`, "was" | `hū` | `a` before, `ū` to `o`, `s` | {519}, {485}, {491} |
| `avoca`, "said" | `vaca` | `a` before, `a` to `o`, `-a` for the ending `ī` | {519}, {477}, {517} |
| `aññāsi`, "understood" | `ñā` | `a` before, `ñ` doubled, `nā` dropped, `s` | {519}, {28}, {509}, {491} |
| `akāsi`, "did", in `manasākāsi` | `kara` | `a` before, `kāsa` for the root | {519}, {491} |
| `āmantesi`, `udānesi`, `desesi` | `manta`, `ana`, `disa` | the class's or causative's `e`, then `s` | {452}, {438}, {491} |

### The rarer pasts

The `-ta` words (`pavattitaṁ`, "set in motion", `abhisambuddhā`) are the
other past, adjectives tabled in [8.8](../8-8-verb-like-forms/).

| set | symbol | endings (same order) | example | rule |
| --- | :-: | --- | --- | :-: |
| `hiyyattanī` | ↩️ | `-ā`, `-ū`, `-o`, `-ttha`, `-aṁ`, `-mhā` | `agamā`, "[he] went"; `ajjhagamaṁ`, "I reached" | {418}, {427}, {519} |
| `parokkhā` | 🔄 | `-a`, `-u`, `-e`, `-ttha`, `-aṁ`, `-mha` | `āha`, "[he] said"; `āhu`, "they said" | {417}, {426}, {475} |

### Examples

> **Idamavoca** bhagavā.

*️⃣🤟🚻⨀②(ima) 🟢⨀🤟⏮(vadati) 🚹⨀①(bhagavant)  
this / said / the Bhagavā  
*This the Bhagavā said.*  
`idaṁ` + `avoca`: the `a` {519}, `o` for `a` {477}, `-a` for the ending {517}.

> Tatra kho bhagavā pañcavaggiye bhikkhū **āmantesi**—

⏏️(tatra) ⏏️(kho) 🚹⨀①(bhagavant) 🚹⨂②🆎(pañca+vaggiya) 🚹⨂②(bhikkhu) 🟢⨀🤟⏮(āmanteti)  
there / indeed / the Bhagavā / of the group of five / bhikkhū / addressed  
*There the Bhagavā addressed the group of five bhikkhū:*  
`āmantesi`: `ā` the prefix, `e` the class's piece {452}, `s` {491}, `-i` {428}.

> Attamanā pañcavaggiyā bhikkhū bhagavato bhāsitaṁ **abhinandunti**.

🚹⨂①🆎(atta+mana) 🚹⨂①🆎(pañca+vaggiya) 🚹⨂①(bhikkhu) 🚹⨀⑥(bhagavant) 🚻⨀②🔽(ta)(bhāsita) 🟢⨂🤟⏮(abhinandati) ⏏️(iti)  
delighted / of the group of five / bhikkhū / of the Bhagavā / what was spoken / rejoiced in / (end quote)  
*The group of five bhikkhū, delighted, rejoiced in the Bhagavā’s words.*  
`abhinanduṁ` + `iti`: `-uṁ` third person plural {428}, its niggahīta `n` before `ti` {40}; no `a`.

> Yato khayaṁ paccayānaṁ **avedī**”ti.

⏏️(yato) 🚹⨀②(khaya) 🚹⨂⑥(paccaya) 🟢⨀🤟⏮(vedeti) ⏏️(iti)  
since / the destruction / of conditions / knew / (end quote)  
*since he has understood the destruction of conditions.”*  
`avedi` + `iti`: the `a` {519}, `-i` {428} long only before `ti` {16}; an `e` class verb without `s`.

### Exercises

Find the past verb in each sentence, name its ending, person and number, say
whether it has the `a` before the root or an `s`, and translate.

:::tip[Hint]
You may find the [Digital Pali Dictionary](https://digitalpalidictionary.github.io/)
useful for looking up words, as well as doing the sandhi and compound word
deconstruction.
:::

1. atha cittaṁ vimucci me”ti.
2. Ñāṇañca pana me dassanaṁ udapādi:
3. Atha kho bhagavā rattiyā paṭhamaṁ yāmaṁ paṭiccasamuppādaṁ anulomapaṭilomaṁ manasākāsi—
4. Iti hidaṁ āyasmato koṇḍaññassa “aññāsikoṇḍañño” tveva nāmaṁ ahosīti.
5. Tassa me tathāgato vā tathāgatasāvako vā dhammaṁ desesi.
6. Tāhaṁ dhammaṁ sutvā tathāgate saddhaṁ paṭilabhiṁ.

### Answers

*Note: please do the exercises first before checking the answers.*

1. > atha cittaṁ vimucci me”ti.

🔼(atha) 🚻①⨀(citta) ⏮🤟⨀(vimucci) 👆⑥⨀(ahaṁ) 🔼(ti)  
then / mind / was liberated / of me / quote  
*Then my mind was liberated.*  
`vimucci`: `-i` third person singular {428}; no `a`.

2. > Ñāṇañca pana me dassanaṁ udapādi:

🚻①⨀(ñāṇa) 🔼(ca pana) 👆⑥⨀(ahaṁ)🚻①⨀(dassana) ⏮🤟⨀(udapādi)  
Having insight / and / moreover / for me realisation / arose  
*Having insight, the realisation arose for me:*  
`udapādi`: `-i` third person singular {428}; the `a` between prefix and root {519}.

3. > Atha kho bhagavā rattiyā paṭhamaṁ yāmaṁ paṭiccasamuppādaṁ anulomapaṭilomaṁ manasākāsi—

⏏️(atha) ⏏️(kho) 🚹⨀①(bhagavant) 🚺⨀⑥(ratti) 🚹⨀②(paṭhama) 🚹⨀②(yāma) 🚹⨀②🆎(paṭicca+samuppāda) 🚹⨀②🆎(anuloma+paṭiloma) 🟢⨀🤟⏮(manasikaroti)  
then / indeed / the Bhagavā / of the night / first / watch / dependent origination / in forward and reverse order / attended to  
*Then the Bhagavā, during the first watch of the night, attended to `paṭiccasamuppādaṁ` (dependent origination) in forward and reverse order —*  
`manasākāsi`: `manasi` + `akāsi`; the `a` {519}, `kāsa` and `s` {491}, `-i` third person singular {428}.

4. > Iti hidaṁ āyasmato koṇḍaññassa “aññāsikoṇḍañño” tveva nāmaṁ ahosīti.

⏏️(iti) ⏏️(hi) *️⃣🤟🚻⨀①(ima) 🚹⨀⑥(āyasmant) 🚹⨀⑥(koṇḍañña) 🚹⨀①(aññāsikoṇḍañña) ⏏️(tu) ⏏️(eva) 🚻⨀①(nāma) 🟢⨀🤟⏮(hoti) ⏏️(iti)  
thus / indeed / this / of the venerable / Koṇḍañña / Aññāsikoṇḍañña / just / so / the name / was / (end quote)  
*Thus it was that `āyasmā` Koṇḍañña came to be known as Aññāsikoṇḍañña (Koṇḍañña who understood).*  
`ahosi` + `iti`: the `a` {519}, `o` for `ū` {485}, `s` {491}, `-i` third person singular {428}, long before `ti` {16}.

5. > Tassa me tathāgato vā tathāgatasāvako vā dhammaṁ desesi.

🚹⑥⨀(ta) 👆②⨀(ahaṁ) 🚹①⨀(tathāgata) 🔼(vā) 🚹①⨀(tathāgatasāvaka) 🔼(vā) 🚹②⨀(dhamma) ⏮🤟⨀(desesi)  
of that / me / Tathāgata / or / disciple of the Buddha / or / Dhamma / taught  
*Because of that, the Tathāgata and his disciples taught the Dhamma to me.*  
`desesi`: `-esi` third person singular {428}, a causative's `e` {438} and `s` {491}.

6. > Tāhaṁ dhammaṁ sutvā tathāgate saddhaṁ paṭilabhiṁ.

🔼(tāhaṁ) 🚹②⨀(dhamma) 🔼(sutvā) 🚹⑦⨀(tathāgata) 🚻①⨀(saddha) ⏮👆⨀(paṭilabhi)  
that I / Dhamma / having heard / in Tathāgata / faith / I acquired  
*Having heard that Dhamma, I acquired faith in the Tathāgata.*  
`paṭilabhiṁ`: `-iṁ` first person singular {428}; the prefix `pati`, no `a`; `sutvā` is an absolutive.
