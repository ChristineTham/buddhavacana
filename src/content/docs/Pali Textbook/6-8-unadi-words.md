The usage examples provided here are not exhaustive, other usages are possible.

A primary derivative (`kitanāma`) is a noun made from a root by a `kita` or an
`uṇādi` affix, with the root's own changes and a `vibhatti` ending of its own.
Section [6.7](../6-7-sadhana/) gave the senses the `uṇādi` affixes carry; this
section covers the rules that form individual words, each affix after a few
named roots: {627}–{634}, {638}, {643}–{649} and {656}–{673}. The root is
written as Kaccāyana names it (`√samu`), the analysis line marks the word 🔽
with the affix as Kaccāyana names it, and where a rule only replaces part of a
word that already exists and names no affix (`suna` → `soṇo`, "a dog"), the
word is shown with its gender, number and ending alone. Appendix
[K](../k-unadi/) tables every `uṇādi` rule with all the words Kaccāyana cites.

### 6.8.1 `mana` after `khi` and the like; `t` for its `m` {627}

* The affix `mana` is added after the roots `khi`, `bhī`, `su`, `ru`, `hu`,
  `vā`, `dhū`, `hi`, `lū`, `pī`, `ada` and the like, and its `m` optionally
  becomes `t`: `khemo` ("safety"), "dangers are destroyed (`khīyanti`) here",
  and `attā` ("the self"), "it devours (`adati`) pleasure and pain", with `t`
  for the `m`. Only the `ma` of the affix remains, its `na` being elided
  {517}.

#### Example

> **khemo**

🚹⨀①🔽(mana)(khemo)  
safety  
*`khemo` ("safety"), "dangers are destroyed (`khīyanti`) here", is `√khi + mana` {627}: the `na` of the affix is elided {517}, the `i` of the root becomes `e` {485}, and the ① ending `si` becomes `o` {104}.*

1. somo
2. dhūmo
3. lomo
4. attā

#### Answers

*Note: please do the exercises first before checking the answers.*

1. > **somo**

🚹⨀①🔽(mana)(somo)  
the moon  
*`somo` ("the moon"), "it pours out (`abhissaveti`) rays", is `√su + mana` {627}: the `na` of the affix is elided {517} and the `u` of the root becomes `o` {485}.*

2. > **dhūmo**

🚹⨀①🔽(mana)(dhūmo)  
smoke  
*`dhūmo` ("smoke"), "it shakes (`dhunāti`), it trembles", is `√dhū + mana` {627}: the `na` of the affix is elided {517}.*

3. > **lomo**

🚹⨀①🔽(mana)(lomo)  
body hair  
*`lomo` ("body hair"), "to be cut (`lunitabbo`)", is `√lū + mana` {627}: the `na` of the affix is elided {517} and the `ū` of the root becomes `o` {485}.*

4. > **attā**

🚹⨀①🔽(mana)(attā)  
the self  
*`attā` ("the self"), "it devours (`adati`) pleasure and pain", is `√ada + mana` {627} with `t` for the `m` of the affix: the final `a` of the root and the `na` of the affix are elided {521}, {517}, the `d` of the root becomes `t` before it {20}, and the ① ending `si` becomes `ā` {189}.*

### 6.8.2 `tha`, `ma` after `samu` and the like {628}

* The affixes `tha` and `ma` are added after the roots `samu`, `damu`, `dara`,
  `raha`, `du`, `hi`, `si`, `bhī`, `dā`, `yā`, `sā`, `ṭhā`, `bhasa` and the
  like: `samatho` ("calm"), "it calms (`sameti`)", with `tha`; `dumo` ("a
  tree"), "it goes (`davati`) upward in growth", with `ma`.

#### Example

> **samatho**

🚹⨀①🔽(tha)(samatho)  
calm  
*`samatho` ("calm, serenity"), "it calms (`sameti`)", is `√samu + tha` {628}, the root appearing as `sama` before the affix, and the ① ending `si` becomes `o` {104}.*

*Note: please do the exercises first before checking the answers.*

1. > **damatho**

🚹⨀①🔽(tha)(damatho)

*`damatho` ("taming, self-control"), "it tames (`damati`)", is `√damu + tha` {628}, formed like `samatho`.*

2. > **ratho**

🚹⨀①🔽(tha)(ratho)  
a chariot  
*`ratho` ("a chariot"), "it holds (`rahati`) the materials", is `√raha + tha` {628}: the final `a` of the root is elided {521} and its `h` is dropped {517}.*

3. > **sīmā**

🚺⨀①🔽(ma)(sīmā)  
a boundary  
*`sīmā` ("a boundary"), "one binds (`bandhati`) here by the formal proclamation", is `√si + ma` {628}, feminine, with the vowel of the root lengthened; the ① ending `si` is elided after `ā` {220}.*

4. > **yāmo**

🚹⨀①🔽(ma)(yāmo)  
a watch of the night  
*`yāmo` ("a watch of the night"), "it goes (`yāti`)", is `√yā + ma` {628}, and the ① ending `si` becomes `o` {104}.*

### 6.8.3 `e` for the penultimate `a` of `gaha` {629}

* The penultimate (`upadhā`) `a` of the root `gaha` ("grasp") optionally
  becomes `e` before the affix: `gehaṁ` ("a house"), "it holds (`gaṇhāti`) the
  building materials", and, with the option not taken, `gahaṁ`.

#### Example

> **gehaṁ**

🚻⨀①🔽(a)(gehaṁ)  
a house  
*`gehaṁ` ("a house") is `√gaha + a`: the final `a` of the root is elided {521}, its penultimate `a` becomes `e` {629}, and the ① ending `si` becomes `aṁ` {219}; without the change the word is `gahaṁ`.*

### 6.8.4 `cchara`, `cchera` for the `su` of `masu` {630}

* The `su` of the nominal base (`pāṭipadika`) `masu` is replaced by `cchara`
  and by `cchera`: `maccharo` ("stingy"), "he is stingy (`maccharati`)", and
  `macchero`.

#### Example

> **maccharo**

🚹⨀①(maccharo)  
stingy  
*`maccharo` ("stingy; stinginess"), "he is stingy (`maccharati`)", is `masu` with its `su` replaced by `cchara` {630}, and the ① ending `si` becomes `o` {104}; `macchero`, with `cchera`, is formed in the same way.*

### 6.8.5 the same for `cara` after `ā` {631}

* The root `cara` preceded by `ā` is replaced by `cchariya`, `cchara` and
  `cchera`, and the `ā` is shortened: `acchariyaṁ` ("a marvel; wonderful"),
  "to be practised intensely (`ābhuso caritabbaṁ`)", `accharaṁ`, `accheraṁ`.
  By the `ca` of the rule the `su` of `masu` is also replaced by `cchariya`:
  `macchariyaṁ` ("stinginess").

#### Example

> **acchariyaṁ**

🚻⨀①🔽(kvi)(acchariyaṁ)  
a marvel  
*`acchariyaṁ` ("a marvel; wonderful"), "to be practised intensely (`ābhuso caritabbaṁ`)", is `ā + √cara + kvi`: the affix `kvi` is elided {639}, `cara` is replaced by `cchariya` and the `ā` shortened {631}, and the ① ending `si` becomes `aṁ` {219}.*

### 6.8.6 `la`, `ya` after `ala`, `kala`, `sala` {632}

* The affixes `la` and `ya` are added after the roots `ala`, `kala` and

  enters (`salati pavisati`)"; and with `ya`, `alyaṁ`, `kalyaṁ`, `salyaṁ`.

> **sallaṁ**

an arrow  
*`sallaṁ` ("an arrow, a dart"), "it goes, it enters (`salati pavisati`)", is `√sala + la` {632}: the final `a` of the root is elided {83} and the ① ending `si` becomes `aṁ` {219}.*

### 6.8.7 `yāṇa`, `lāṇa` {633}

  `sallāṇo`, `paṭisallāṇo`.

🚻⨀①🔽(yāṇa)(kalyāṇaṁ)  
good  
*`kalyāṇaṁ` ("good; goodness, a good deed"), "to be counted, reckoned (`kalitabbaṁ saṅkhyātabbaṁ`)", is `√kala + yāṇa` {633}: the final `a` of the root is elided {521} and the ① ending `si` becomes `aṁ` {219}.*

### 6.8.9 `pabbajjā` and the like from `vaja` and the like {638}

* The words `pabbajjā` ("the going forth"), "to be gone into (`vajitabbā`)
  first of all", and the long list that follows it in the vutti are formed as
  they stand (`nipātana`, "direct statement") from the roots `vaja` and the
  like with their prefixes and affixes, the vutti giving each its gloss:
  `vijjā` ("knowledge"), "knowing (`vijānanaṁ`)", `icchā` ("desire"),
  "seeking (`esanaṁ`)", `saddhā` ("faith"), `saccaṁ` ("truth"), "being so
  (`satanaṁ`)".

#### Example

> **pabbajjā**

🚺⨀①🔽(ṇya)(pabbajjā)  
the going forth  
*`pabbajjā` ("the going forth"), "to be gone into (`vajitabbā`) first of all", is `pa + √vaja + ṇya` {638}: the final `a` of the root is elided {521}, the `ṇ` of the affix is elided {523}, the `v` is doubled after `pa` {28}, `jya` becomes `jjha` {441} and then, by the rule's direct statement, `jjā`, with `bb` for `vv` {638}; the ① ending `si` is elided after `ā` {220}.*

### 6.8.10 `tta` after `bhī` and the roots of thinking, knowing and honouring {643}

* The affix `tta` is added after the roots `bhī` ("fear") and the like, and
  after the roots of the `mati` ("thinking"), `budhi` ("knowing") and `pūjā`
  ("honouring") groups: `bhīto` ("frightened"), "to be afraid (`bhāyitabbo`)",
  `sammato` ("agreed upon, authorized"), `buddho` ("the Awakened One"), "to be
  known, to be understood (`bujjhitabbo ñātabbo`)", `pūjito` ("honoured").

#### Example

> **bhīto**

🚹⨀①🔽(tta)(bhīto)  
frightened  
*`bhīto` ("frightened, afraid"), "to be afraid (`bhāyitabbo`)", is `√bhī + tta` {643}, and the ① ending `si` becomes `o` {104}.*

### 6.8.11 `thu`, `ttima`, `ṇima` after `vepu` and the like {644}

* The affixes `thu`, `ttima` and `ṇima` are added, in the sense of what is
  produced (`nibbatta`), after the roots `vepu`, `sī`, `dava`, `vamu`, `ku`,
  `dā`, `bhū`, `hu` and the like, as each allows: `vepathu` ("a tremor"),
  "trembling (`vepanaṁ`) is `vepa`; what is produced by it", `kuttimaṁ`
  ("made, artificial"), "`kutti` is making; what is produced by it",
  `ohāvimaṁ`, with `ṇima` after `ava + hu`.

> **vamathu**

🚹⨀①🔽(thu)(vamathu)  
vomit  
*`vamathu` ("vomit"), "vomiting (`vamanaṁ`) is `vama`; what is produced by it", is `√vamu + thu` {644}: the `u` of the root is elided {521} and `a` inserted before the affix {517}; the ① ending `si` is elided after `u` {220}.*

### 6.8.12 `āni` in reproach with `na` {645}

* The affix `āni` is added after a root in the sense of reproach (`akkosa`)
  when the negative `na` is present: `agamāni te jamma desaṁ` ("no going for
  you, wretch, to the region"), glossed `na gamitabbaṁ` ("it is not to be gone
  to"); `akarāṇi te jamma kammaṁ` ("no doing for you, wretch, of the deed").
  Without `na` (`vipatti te jamma`, "misfortune to you, wretch!") or without
  reproach (`agati te`, "there is no going for you") the affix is not used.

#### Example

> **agamāni**

🚺⨀①🔽(āni)(agamāni)  
no going  
*`agamāni` ("no going"), "it is not to be gone to (`na gamitabbaṁ`)", is `na + √gamu + āni` {645}: `na` becomes `a` {333}, the final vowel of the root is elided {83}, and the ① ending `si` is elided {220}.*

### 6.8.13 `kkhattuṁ` for `sakiṁ` after the numerals {646}

* After `eka` and the other numerals, `sakiṁ` ("once") is replaced by
  `kkhattuṁ`, making the indeclinable adverbs of repetition: `ekakkhattuṁ`
  ("once"), glossed "one time (`sakiṁ vāraṁ`) of one thing", `dvikkhattuṁ`
  ("twice"), `tikkhattuṁ` ("three times"), and so on to `dasakkhattuṁ` ("ten
  times"); such words are to be applied in the same way.

#### Example

> tiṇṇaṁ padatthānaṁ sakiṁ vāraṁ → **tikkhattuṁ**
