Chapter 1 set out the parts of a Pāḷi sentence and, at its end, the master
table of endings. This chapter is about the inflections of nouns (`nāma`), and noun subclasses such as:

- the pronouns and their relatives (`sabbanāma`),
- the numbers (`saṅkhyā`)

Some words are typically uninflected:

- the prefixes (`upasagga`) and
- the particles (`nipāta`).

Verbs are in chapter 8 and have their own inflection forms and rules. Compounds in chapter 6 and derived words in chapter 10 are generally inflected like nouns. However, the absolutive `-tvā` or an infinitive `-tuṁ` are not inflected.

The following chart summarises all the different categories of words:

```mermaid

  W["a word in a sentence"] --> A["abyaya: uninflected"]
  W --> N["nāma: noun inflections ①…⑦ ⓪, ⨀ or ⨂"]
  W --> V["ākhyāta: verb inflections (chapter 8)"]
  A --> A1["upasagga: a prefix (2.6)"]
  A --> A2["nipāta: a particle (2.7)"]
  A --> A3["an absolutive -tvā or an infinitive -tuṁ (8.8)"]
  N --> N1["a noun or an adjective (2.2, 2.3)"]
  N --> N2["sabbanāma: a pronoun (2.4)"]
  N --> N3["saṅkhyā: a number (2.5)"]
  N --> N4["samāsa: a compound (chapter 6)"]
  N --> N5["taddhita, kita, uṇādi: a derived word (chapter 10)"]
  N --> N6["a participle -ta, -anta, -māna, -tabba (8.8)"]
```
