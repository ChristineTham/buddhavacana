Every noun (`nāma`, "a name") carries an ending that tells what the noun is
doing in its sentence. Once a word is split from its neighbours
([2.1](../2-1-sandhi/)), ask "what is its ending?", then "what is it telling
me?". This section answers the first question with the inflection tables of
the lemma classes that fill the texts, in one view, the way the Bālāvatāra
summarises them; the second question is [chapter 4](../4-0-karaka/)'s. The
master table of endings at the end of [chapter 1](../1-basics/#recognising-a-word-the-endings-at-a-glance)
reads the same tables the other way round, from the ending to the form.

A noun has a gender (`liṅga`) {53}, which the Digital Pāḷi Dictionary writes
as `masc`, `nt` or `fem` after the headword: it is looked up, not guessed from
the lemma's last letter. A noun also has a number, singular (`ekavacana`) ⨀
or plural (`bahuvacana`) ⨂, and one of eight endings {54}: ① to ⑦, the seven
`vibhatti` of chapter 1, and ⓪, the calling form (`ālapana`). Which shape an
ending takes depends on the lemma's last letter and gender, and nine nouns in
ten that a reader meets belong to a handful of classes: by the dictionary's
count of occurrences in the early discourses, masculines and neuters in `-a`
(`purisa`, `citta`) come first by far, then feminines in `-ā` (`kaññā`),
masculines in `-u` (`bhikkhu`), feminines in `-i` (`ratti`, `jāti`),
masculines in `-i` (`aggi`), feminines in `-ī` (`nadī`), and the neuters in
`-i` and `-u` (`aṭṭhi`, `cakkhu`). The few very common words that follow none
of these, `bhagavā`, `satthā`, `rājā`, `attā`, `mano`, are the irregular
inflections of [2.3](../2-3-irregular/).

### The major inflection tables

Each table reads across: the ending, then for each model word its singular
and plural shapes. Where a form has two shapes both are in use; the first is
the commoner in the texts. Kaccāyana's rules for every shape are {55}–{119}
and {215}–{245}, tabled in [Appendix G](../g-vibhatti/).

Masculine nouns: lemmas ending in `-a` (`purisa`, "man"), `-i` (`aggi`,
"fire") and `-u` (`bhikkhu`):

| :-: | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| ① | `puriso` | `purisā` | `aggi` | `aggī`, `aggayo` | `bhikkhu` | `bhikkhū`, `bhikkhavo` |
| ② | `purisaṁ` | `purise` | `aggiṁ` | `aggī`, `aggayo` | `bhikkhuṁ` | `bhikkhū`, `bhikkhavo` |
| ③ | `purisena` | `purisehi` | `agginā` | `aggīhi` | `bhikkhunā` | `bhikkhūhi` |
| ④ | `purisassa`, `purisāya` | `purisānaṁ` | `aggino`, `aggissa` | `aggīnaṁ` | `bhikkhuno`, `bhikkhussa` | `bhikkhūnaṁ` |
| ⑤ | `purisā`, `purisasmā`, `purisamhā` | `purisehi` | `agginā`, `aggismā`, `aggimhā` | `aggīhi` | `bhikkhunā`, `bhikkhusmā`, `bhikkhumhā` | `bhikkhūhi` |
| ⑥ | `purisassa` | `purisānaṁ` | `aggino`, `aggissa` | `aggīnaṁ` | `bhikkhuno`, `bhikkhussa` | `bhikkhūnaṁ` |
| ⑦ | `purise`, `purisasmiṁ`, `purisamhi` | `purisesu` | `aggismiṁ`, `aggimhi` | `aggīsu` | `bhikkhusmiṁ`, `bhikkhumhi` | `bhikkhūsu` |
| ⓪ | `purisa`, `purisā` | `purisā` | `aggi` | `aggī`, `aggayo` | `bhikkhu` | `bhikkhave`, `bhikkhavo` |

Feminine nouns: lemmas ending in `-ā` (`kaññā`, "girl"), `-i` (`ratti`,
"night") and `-ī` (`nadī`, "river"); a lemma in `-u` or `-ū` (`dhenu`, "cow";
`vadhū`, "young wife") goes like `ratti` and `nadī` with `u` for `i`:

| ending | `kaññā` ⨀ | `kaññā` ⨂ | `ratti` ⨀ | `ratti` ⨂ | `nadī` ⨀ | `nadī` ⨂ |
| :-: | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| ① | `kaññā` | `kaññā`, `kaññāyo` | `ratti` | `rattī`, `rattiyo` | `nadī` | `nadī`, `nadiyo` |
| ② | `kaññaṁ` | `kaññā`, `kaññāyo` | `rattiṁ` | `rattī`, `rattiyo` | `nadiṁ` | `nadī`, `nadiyo` |
| ③ | `kaññāya` | `kaññāhi` | `rattiyā` | `rattīhi` | `nadiyā` | `nadīhi` |
| ④ | `kaññāya` | `kaññānaṁ` | `rattiyā` | `rattīnaṁ` | `nadiyā` | `nadīnaṁ` |
| ⑤ | `kaññāya` | `kaññāhi` | `rattiyā` | `rattīhi` | `nadiyā` | `nadīhi` |
| ⑥ | `kaññāya` | `kaññānaṁ` | `rattiyā` | `rattīnaṁ` | `nadiyā` | `nadīnaṁ` |
| ⑦ | `kaññāya`, `kaññāyaṁ` | `kaññāsu` | `rattiyā`, `rattiyaṁ` | `rattīsu` | `nadiyā`, `nadiyaṁ` | `nadīsu` |
| ⓪ | `kaññe` | `kaññā`, `kaññāyo` | `ratti` | `rattī`, `rattiyo` | `nadi` | `nadī`, `nadiyo` |

Neuter nouns: lemmas ending in `-a` (`citta`, "mind"), `-i` (`aṭṭhi`, "bone")
and `-u` (`cakkhu`, "eye"); only ①, ② and ⓪ differ from the masculine of the
same class, so the rest of each column reads as `purisa`, `aggi` and
`bhikkhu`:

| ending | `citta` ⨀ | `citta` ⨂ | `aṭṭhi` ⨀ | `aṭṭhi` ⨂ | `cakkhu` ⨀ | `cakkhu` ⨂ |
| :-: | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| ① | `cittaṁ` | `cittāni` | `aṭṭhi` | `aṭṭhīni`, `aṭṭhī` | `cakkhu` | `cakkhūni`, `cakkhū` |
| ② | `cittaṁ` | `cittāni` | `aṭṭhiṁ` | `aṭṭhīni`, `aṭṭhī` | `cakkhuṁ` | `cakkhūni`, `cakkhū` |
| ③ to ⑦ | as `purisa` | as `purisa` | as `aggi` | as `aggi` | as `bhikkhu` | as `bhikkhu` |
| ⓪ | `citta` | `cittāni` | `aṭṭhi` | `aṭṭhīni` | `cakkhu` | `cakkhūni` |

Two habits of the tables are worth fixing in the eye. The plural ③ to ⑦ of
every class are the same four shapes, `-hi`, `-naṁ`, `-hi`, `-su`, on the
lemma's final vowel, long in `i` and `u` words (`bhikkhūhi`, `rattīnaṁ`,
`aggīsu`) and `e` for `a` (`purisehi`, `purisesu`). And a feminine has one
singular shape for ③ to ⑦, `-āya` after `ā` {111} and `-iyā` after `i` or `ī`
{112}, which the sentence alone sorts out: `taṇhāya` in
[5: 4.3](../5-dhammacakka/#u4-3) is ⑥, "of craving", `rattiyā` in
[3: 2.3](../3-awakening/#u2-3) ⑥, "of the night".

:::note[The same ending, three jobs]
`-ā` is 🚹 ①⨂ (`dhammā`), 🚹 ⑤⨀ (`avijjāpaccayā`), 🚺 ①⨀ (`taṇhā`) and 🚺 ①⨂
(`kaṅkhā`); `-e` is 🚹 ②⨂ (`ante`), 🚹 or 🚻 ⑦⨀ (`migadāye`, `isipatane`) and
🚺 ⓪⨀ (`kaññe`); `-aṁ` is 🚹 ②⨀ (`samayaṁ`), 🚻 ①⨀ or ②⨀ (`viññāṇaṁ`) and 🚺
②⨀ (`mārasenaṁ`). Gender, agreeing words and the verb decide: `pātubhavanti
dhammā` ([3: 6.1](../3-awakening/#u6-1)) has a plural verb, so `dhammā` is ①⨂.
:::

### Adjectives agree

An adjective (`guṇanāma`, "a quality-word") has no gender of its own: it
takes the gender, number and ending of its noun, so the two wear one shape:
`ariyo aṭṭhaṅgiko maggo` ("the noble eightfold path", [5: 3.2](../5-dhammacakka/#u3-2)),
🚹⨀① on all three words; `bhummā devā` ([5: 12.1](../5-dhammacakka/#u12-1)), 🚹⨂①;
`pañcavaggiye bhikkhū` ([5: 1.2](../5-dhammacakka/#u1-2)), 🚹⨂② in two shapes, `-e`
on the `a` lemma and `-ū` on the `u` lemma. An `a` lemma used of a feminine
noun takes the feminine `-ā` {237} and follows `kaññā`: `majjhimā paṭipadā`
("the middle way", [5: 2.4](../5-dhammacakka/#u2-4)), 🚺⨀①.

### Examples

> **Avijjāpaccayā saṅkhārā**,

🚹⨀⑤🆎(avijjā+paccaya) 🚹⨂①(saṅkhāra)  
with ignorance as condition / mental constructions  
*Two `-ā`, two jobs: `avijjāpaccayā` is ⑤⨀, "from" {108}, `saṅkhārā` ①⨂ {107}; [3: 3.1](../3-awakening/#u3-1).*

> **saṁkhittena pañcupādānakkhandhā dukkhā**

🚹⨀③🔽(ta)(saṁkhitta) 🚹⨂①🆎(pañca+upādāna+khandha) 🚹⨂①(dukkha)  
in short / the five collections of fuel / suffering  
*`saṁkhittena`: ③⨀, `-ena` {103}; `pañcupādānakkhandhā`: ①⨂, `-ā` {107}, its adjective `dukkhā` agreeing; [5: 4.1](../5-dhammacakka/#u4-1).*

> jātipi **dukkhā**, jarāpi **dukkhā**, byādhipi **dukkho**, maraṇampi **dukkhaṁ**

🚺⨀①(jāti) ⏏️(api) 🚺⨀①(dukkha) 🚺⨀①(jarā) ⏏️(api) 🚺⨀①(dukkha) 🚹⨀①(byādhi) ⏏️(api) 🚹⨀①(dukkha) 🚻⨀①(maraṇa) ⏏️(api) 🚻⨀①(dukkha)  
birth / too / suffering / aging / too / suffering / illness / too / suffering / death / too / suffering  
*One adjective, three shapes: `dukkhā` with the 🚺 `jāti` and `jarā` {237}, `dukkho` with the 🚹 `byādhi` {104}, `dukkhaṁ` with the 🚻 `maraṇa` {219}; [5: 4.1](../5-dhammacakka/#u4-1).*

> **upasamāya abhiññāya sambodhāya nibbānāya** saṁvattati

🚹⨀④(upasama) 🚺⨀④(abhiññā) 🚹⨀④(sambodha) 🚻⨀④(nibbāna) 🟢⨀🤟▶️(saṁvattati)  
to calm / to direct knowledge / to enlightenment / to extinguishment / leads  
*`-āya` on an `a` lemma is ④⨀, "to" {109}; on the 🚺 `abhiññā` the `-āya` of {111} is ④ too, as its three neighbours; [5: 2.4](../5-dhammacakka/#u2-4).*

> Tatra kho bhagavā pañcavaggiye **bhikkhū** āmantesi—

⏏️(tatra) ⏏️(kho) 🚹⨀①(bhagavant) 🚹⨂②🆎(pañca+vaggiya) 🚹⨂②(bhikkhu) 🟢⨀🤟⏮(āmanteti)  
there / indeed / the Bhagavā / of the group of five / bhikkhū / addressed  
*There the Bhagavā addressed the group of five bhikkhū:*  
*`bhikkhū`: `-ū`, the ② plural, `yo` dropped and the `u` lengthened {118}, {88}, the object of `āmantesi`; `pañcavaggiye` agrees with it; [5: 1.2](../5-dhammacakka/#u1-2).*

> **rattiyā** paṭhamaṁ yāmaṁ

🚺⨀⑥(ratti) 🚹⨀②(paṭhama) 🚹⨀②(yāma)  
of the night / first / watch  
*during the first watch of the night*  
*`rattiyā`: `-iyā`, the feminine singular shape for ③ to ⑦ {112}; here ⑥, "of the night"; [3: 2.3](../3-awakening/#u2-3).*

### Exercises

Each sentence or phrase is from chapter [3](../3-awakening/),
[5](../5-dhammacakka/), [7](../7-anatta/), [9](../9-fire/) or the
[bonus chapter](../11-pbt/), which translates it. Name the gender, number
and ending of every noun or adjective; in the last three the word in bold
is a lemma in `i` or `u`. `bhagavā` belongs to [2.3](../2-3-irregular/).

:::tip[Hint]
You may find the [Digital Pali Dictionary](https://digitalpalidictionary.github.io/)
useful for looking up words, as well as doing the sandhi and compound word
deconstruction.
:::

1. Ekaṁ samayaṁ bhagavā gayāyaṁ viharati gayāsīse saddhiṁ bhikkhusahassena.
2. Evametassa kevalassa dukkhakkhandhassa samudayo hoti.
3. Athassa kaṅkhā vapayanti sabbā,
4. appiyehi sampayogo dukkho, piyehi vippayogo dukkho
5. Yato khayaṁ paccayānaṁ avedī”ti.
6. akuppā me **vimutti**, ayamantimā **jāti**
7. pañcavaggiyānaṁ **bhikkhūnaṁ** anupādāya āsavehi cittāni vimucciṁsūti
8. **Rāgagginā**, dosagginā, mohagginā ādittaṁ, **jātiyā** jarāya maraṇena

### Answers

*Note: please do the exercises first before checking the answers.*

1. > Ekaṁ samayaṁ bhagavā gayāyaṁ viharati gayāsīse saddhiṁ bhikkhusahassena.

*️⃣🤟🚹⨀②(eka) 🚹⨀②(samaya) 🚹⨀①(bhagavant) 🚺⨀⑦(gayā) 🟢⨀🤟▶️(viharati) 🚻⨀⑦(gayāsīsa) ⏏️(saddhiṁ) 🚻⨀③🆎(bhikkhu+sahassa)  
one / time / the Bhagavā / at Gayā / dwells / at Gayāsīsa / together / with a thousand bhikkhū  
*`ekaṁ samayaṁ`: 🚹⨀②, `-aṁ` {83}; `gayāyaṁ`: 🚺⨀⑦, `-āyaṁ` {216}; `gayāsīse`: 🚻⨀⑦, `-e` {108}; `bhikkhusahassena`: 🚻⨀③, `-ena` {103}; [9: 1.1](../9-fire/#u1-1).*

2. > Evametassa kevalassa dukkhakkhandhassa samudayo hoti.

⏏️(evaṁ) *️⃣🤟🚹⨀⑥(eta) 🚹⨀⑥(kevala) 🚹⨀⑥🆎(dukkha+khandha) 🚹⨀①(samudaya) 🟢⨀🤟▶️(hoti)  
thus / of this / of the entire / of the mass of suffering / origin / is  
*`kevalassa dukkhakkhandhassa`: 🚹⨀⑥, `-assa` {61}, "of", adjective and noun alike; `samudayo`: 🚹⨀①, `-o` {104}; [3: 3.12](../3-awakening/#u3-12).*

3. > Athassa kaṅkhā vapayanti sabbā,

⏏️(atha) *️⃣🤟🚹⨀⑥(ima) 🚺⨂①(kaṅkhā) 🟢⨂🤟▶️(vapayāti) *️⃣🤟🚺⨂①(sabba)  
then / his / doubts / vanish / all  
*`kaṅkhā`: 🚺⨂①, `-ā` {118}, with a plural verb; `sabbā` agrees, 🚺⨂①; [3: 6.3](../3-awakening/#u6-3).*

4. > appiyehi sampayogo dukkho, piyehi vippayogo dukkho

🚹⨂③🆎(na+piya) 🚹⨀①(sampayoga) 🚹⨀①(dukkha) 🚹⨂③(piya) 🚹⨀①(vippayoga) 🚹⨀①(dukkha)  
with the unloved / association / suffering / from the loved / separation / suffering  
*`appiyehi`, `piyehi`: 🚹⨂③, `-ehi` {101}; `sampayogo`, `vippayogo` and the agreeing `dukkho`: 🚹⨀①, `-o` {104}; [5: 4.1](../5-dhammacakka/#u4-1).*

5. > Yato khayaṁ paccayānaṁ avedī”ti.

⏏️(yato) 🚹⨀②(khaya) 🚹⨂⑥(paccaya) 🟢⨀🤟⏮(vedeti) ⏏️(iti)  
since / the destruction / of conditions / knew / (end quote)  
*`khayaṁ`: 🚹⨀②, `-aṁ` {83}, the object; `paccayānaṁ`: 🚹⨂⑥, `-ānaṁ` {89}, "of"; [3: 9.4](../3-awakening/#u9-4).*

6. > akuppā me **vimutti**, ayamantimā **jāti**

🚺⨀①🆎(na+kuppa) *️⃣👆⚧⨀⑥(ahaṁ) 🚺⨀①(vimutti) *️⃣🤟🚺⨀①(ima) 🚺⨀①(antima) 🚺⨀①(jāti)  
unshakeable / my / liberation / this / final / birth  
*Unshakeable is my liberation, this is the final birth.*  

7. > pañcavaggiyānaṁ **bhikkhūnaṁ** anupādāya āsavehi cittāni vimucciṁsūti

🚹⨂⑥🆎(pañca+vaggiya) 🚹⨂⑥(bhikkhu) 🔽(ya)(anupādiyati) 🚹⨂⑤(āsava) 🚻⨂①(citta) 🔵⨂🤟⏮(vimuccati) ⏏️(iti)  
of the group of five / bhikkhū / not grasping / from the defilements / minds / were liberated / (end quote)  
*the `cittāni` (minds) of the group of five bhikkhū, `anupādāya` (not grasping), were liberated from the `āsavā` (defilements).*  

8. > **Rāgagginā**, dosagginā, mohagginā ādittaṁ, **jātiyā** jarāya maraṇena

🚹⨀③🆎(rāga+aggi) 🚹⨀③🆎(dosa+aggi) 🚹⨀③🆎(moha+aggi) 🚻⨀①🔽(ta)(āditta) 🚺⨀③(jāti) 🚺⨀③(jarā) 🚻⨀③(maraṇa)  
with the fire of passion / with the fire of hatred / with the fire of delusion / burning / with birth / with aging / with death  
*Burning with the fire of passion (`rāgaggi`), with the fire of hatred (`dosaggi`), with the fire of delusion (`mohaggi`); burning with birth, aging, death*
