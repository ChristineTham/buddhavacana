Every noun (`nāma`, "a name") carries an ending that tells what the noun is
doing in its sentence. Once a word is split from its neighbours
([2.1](../2-1-sandhi/)), ask "what is its ending?", then "what is it telling
me?". This section answers the first question for the lemmas in `-a` and `-ā`
that fill chapters [3](../3-awakening/), [5](../5-dhammacakka/), [7](../7-anatta/)
and [9](../9-fire/), with Kaccāyana's own model words `purisa`
("man"), `citta` ("mind") and `kaññā` ("girl").

A noun has a gender (`liṅga`) {53}, which the Digital Pāḷi Dictionary writes
as `masc`, `nt` or `fem` after the headword: it is looked up, not guessed from
the lemma's last letter. A noun also has a number,
singular (`ekavacana`) ⨀ or plural (`bahuvacana`) ⨂.

| gender | dictionary | the lemma ends in |
| --- | :-: | --- |
| `pulliṅga` 🚹 | `masc` | `-a` (`purisa`); `-i`, `-u` ([2.3](../2-3-vowel-lemmas/)) |
| `napuṁsakaliṅga` 🚻 | `nt` | `-a` (`citta`); `-i`, `-u` |
| `itthiliṅga` 🚺 | `fem` | `-ā` (`kaññā`); `-i`, `-ī`, `-u`, `-ū` |

The ending (`vibhatti`) is one of eight sets {55}, each an answer to "what is
this noun doing?": ① the doer or the thing itself ([4.6](../4-6-agent/),
[4.8](../4-8-nominative/)), ② the object ([4.5](../4-5-accusative/)), ③ "by",
"with" ([4.4](../4-4-instrumental/)), ④ "to", "for" ([4.2](../4-2-dative/)),
⑤ "from" ([4.1](../4-1-ablative/)), ⑥ "of" ([4.7](../4-7-genitive/)), ⑦ "in",
"at", "when" ([4.3](../4-3-locative/)), ⓪ calling someone (`ālapana`) {57}.
[Chapter 4](../4-0-karaka/) gives the answers in full.

### The three inflection tables

Every noun or adjective in `-a` or `-ā` takes one of these shapes, all
Kaccāyana's own; where a cell holds several, all occur. The singular's rules
stand before the semicolon, the plural's after; [Appendix G](../g-vibhatti/)
tables them all.

The inflection table of `purisa` ("man"), 🚹:

| ending | ⨀ | ⨂ | rule |
| :-: | --- | --- | --- |
| ① | `puriso` | `purisā` | {104}; {107} |
| ② | `purisaṁ` | `purise` | {83}; {107} |
| ③ | `purisena` | `purisehi` | {103}; {101} |
| ④ | `purisassa`, `purisāya` | `purisānaṁ` | {61}, {109}; {89} |
| ⑤ | `purisā`, `purisasmā`, `purisamhā` | `purisehi` | {108}, {99}; {101} |
| ⑥ | `purisassa` | `purisānaṁ` | {61}; {89} |
| ⑦ | `purise`, `purisasmiṁ`, `purisamhi` | `purisesu` | {108}, {99}; {101} |
| ⓪ | `purisa`, `purisā` | `purisā` | {220}, {244}; {107} |

The ⓪ singular is the bare lemma, `bho purisa` ("sir, man!") {220}; most
lemmas in `-a` are 🚹 and follow `purisa`.

The inflection table of `citta` ("mind"), 🚻, where it differs from `purisa`:

| ending | ⨀ | ⨂ | rule |
| :-: | --- | --- | --- |
| ① | `cittaṁ` | `cittāni` | {219}; {218} |
| ② | `cittaṁ` | `cittāni` | {83}; {218} |
| ⓪ | `citta` | `cittāni` | {220}; {218} |

The rest is as `purisa`. So `cittaṁ` may be ① or ②, and `-āni` is always a
neuter plural: `kāsāyāni vatthāni` ("ochre robes") in [11: 26, 12.8](../11-pbt/#s26-u12-8),
`cittāni` ("minds") in [7: 12.3](../7-anatta/#u12-3).

The inflection table of `kaññā` ("girl"), 🚺:

| ending | ⨀ | ⨂ | rule |
| :-: | --- | --- | --- |
| ① | `kaññā` | `kaññā`, `kaññāyo` | {220}; {118} |
| ② | `kaññaṁ` | `kaññā`, `kaññāyo` | {83}; {118} |
| ③ | `kaññāya` | `kaññāhi` | {111}; {55} |
| ④ | `kaññāya` | `kaññānaṁ` | {111}; {55} |
| ⑤ | `kaññāya` | `kaññāhi` | {111}; {55} |
| ⑥ | `kaññāya` | `kaññānaṁ` | {111}; {55} |
| ⑦ | `kaññāya`, `kaññāyaṁ` | `kaññāsu` | {111}, {216}; {55} |
| ⓪ | `kaññe` | `kaññā`, `kaññāyo` | {114}; {118} |

One shape, `-āya`, serves the five singular endings ③ to ⑦ {111}: `taṇhāya`
in [5: 4.3](../5-dhammacakka/#u4-3) is ⑥, "of craving", by the sense alone.
The plural ③ to ⑦ add the endings of {55} to the `-ā` unchanged; the ⓪
singular is `-e` {114},
`bhoti kaññe` ("madam, girl!").

:::note[The same ending, three jobs]
`-ā` is 🚹 ①⨂ (`dhammā`), 🚹 ⑤⨀ (`avijjāpaccayā`), 🚺 ①⨀ (`taṇhā`) and 🚺 ①⨂
(`kaṅkhā`); `-e` is 🚹 ②⨂ (`ante`), 🚹 or 🚻 ⑦⨀ (`migadāye`, `isipatane`) and
🚺 ⓪⨀ (`kaññe`); `-aṁ` is 🚹 ②⨀ (`samayaṁ`), 🚻 ①⨀ or ②⨀ (`viññāṇaṁ`) and 🚺
②⨀ (`mārasenaṁ`). Gender, agreeing words and the verb decide: `pātubhavanti
dhammā` ([3: 6.1](../3-awakening/#u6-1)) has a plural verb, so `dhammā` is ①⨂.
:::

### Adjectives agree

An adjective (`guṇanāma`, "a quality-word") has no gender of its own: it
takes the gender, number and ending of its noun, so the two wear one shape:
`ariyo aṭṭhaṅgiko maggo` ("the noble eightfold path", [5: 3.2](../5-dhammacakka/#u3-2)),
🚹⨀① on all three words; `bhummā devā` ([5: 12.1](../5-dhammacakka/#u12-1)), 🚹⨂①;
`pañcavaggiye bhikkhū` ([5: 1.2](../5-dhammacakka/#u1-2)), 🚹⨂② in two shapes, `-e`
on the `a` lemma and `-ū` on the `u` lemma. An `a` lemma used of a feminine
noun takes the feminine `-ā` {237} and follows `kaññā`: `majjhimā paṭipadā`
("the middle way", [5: 2.4](../5-dhammacakka/#u2-4)), 🚺⨀①.

### Examples

> **Avijjāpaccayā saṅkhārā**,

🚹⨀⑤🆎(avijjā+paccaya) 🚹⨂①(saṅkhāra)  
with ignorance as condition / mental constructions  
*Two `-ā`, two jobs: `avijjāpaccayā` is ⑤⨀, "from" {108}, `saṅkhārā` ①⨂ {107}; [3: 3.1](../3-awakening/#u3-1).*

> **saṁkhittena pañcupādānakkhandhā dukkhā**

🚹⨀③🔽(ta)(saṁkhitta) 🚹⨂①🆎(pañca+upādāna+khandha) 🚹⨂①(dukkha)  
in short / the five collections of fuel / suffering  
*`saṁkhittena`: ③⨀, `-ena` {103}; `pañcupādānakkhandhā`: ①⨂, `-ā` {107}, its adjective `dukkhā` agreeing; [5: 4.1](../5-dhammacakka/#u4-1).*

> jātipi **dukkhā**, jarāpi **dukkhā**, byādhipi **dukkho**, maraṇampi **dukkhaṁ**

🚺⨀①(jāti) ⏏️(api) 🚺⨀①(dukkha) 🚺⨀①(jarā) ⏏️(api) 🚺⨀①(dukkha) 🚹⨀①(byādhi) ⏏️(api) 🚹⨀①(dukkha) 🚻⨀①(maraṇa) ⏏️(api) 🚻⨀①(dukkha)  
birth / too / suffering / aging / too / suffering / illness / too / suffering / death / too / suffering  
*One adjective, three shapes: `dukkhā` with the 🚺 `jāti` and `jarā` {237}, `dukkho` with the 🚹 `byādhi` {104}, `dukkhaṁ` with the 🚻 `maraṇa` {219}; [5: 4.1](../5-dhammacakka/#u4-1).*

> **upasamāya abhiññāya sambodhāya nibbānāya** saṁvattati

🚹⨀④(upasama) 🚺⨀④(abhiññā) 🚹⨀④(sambodha) 🚻⨀④(nibbāna) 🟢⨀🤟▶️(saṁvattati)  
to calm / to direct knowledge / to enlightenment / to extinguishment / leads  
*`-āya` on an `a` lemma is ④⨀, "to" {109}; on the 🚺 `abhiññā` the same `-āya` serves ③ to ⑦ {111}; [5: 2.4](../5-dhammacakka/#u2-4).*

### Exercises

Each sentence is from chapter [3](../3-awakening/), [5](../5-dhammacakka/),
[7](../7-anatta/) or [9](../9-fire/), which translates it. Name the gender,
number and ending of every noun or adjective in `-a` or `-ā`;
`bhagavā`, `bhikkhū` and `cakkhuṁ` belong to [2.3](../2-3-vowel-lemmas/) and
[2.4](../2-4-irregular/).

:::tip[Hint]
You may find the [Digital Pali Dictionary](https://digitalpalidictionary.github.io/)
useful for looking up words, as well as doing the sandhi and compound word
deconstruction.
:::

1. Ekaṁ samayaṁ bhagavā gayāyaṁ viharati gayāsīse saddhiṁ bhikkhusahassena.
2. Evametassa kevalassa dukkhakkhandhassa samudayo hoti.
3. Athassa kaṅkhā vapayanti sabbā,
4. pubbe ananussutesu dhammesu cakkhuṁ udapādi
5. appiyehi sampayogo dukkho, piyehi vippayogo dukkho
6. Yato khayaṁ paccayānaṁ avedī”ti.
7. Vidhūpayaṁ tiṭṭhati mārasenaṁ,
8. Vimuttasmiṁ vimuttamiti ñāṇaṁ hoti.

### Answers

*Note: please do the exercises first before checking the answers.*

1. > Ekaṁ samayaṁ bhagavā gayāyaṁ viharati gayāsīse saddhiṁ bhikkhusahassena.

*️⃣🤟🚹⨀②(eka) 🚹⨀②(samaya) 🚹⨀①(bhagavant) 🚺⨀⑦(gayā) 🟢⨀🤟▶️(viharati) 🚻⨀⑦(gayāsīsa) ⏏️(saddhiṁ) 🚻⨀③🆎(bhikkhu+sahassa)  
one / time / the Bhagavā / at Gayā / dwells / at Gayāsīsa / together / with a thousand bhikkhū  
*`ekaṁ samayaṁ`: 🚹⨀②, `-aṁ` {83}; `gayāyaṁ`: 🚺⨀⑦, `-āyaṁ` {216}; `gayāsīse`: 🚻⨀⑦, `-e` {108}; `bhikkhusahassena`: 🚻⨀③, `-ena` {103}; [9: 1.1](../9-fire/#u1-1).*

2. > Evametassa kevalassa dukkhakkhandhassa samudayo hoti.

⏏️(evaṁ) *️⃣🤟🚹⨀⑥(eta) 🚹⨀⑥(kevala) 🚹⨀⑥🆎(dukkha+khandha) 🚹⨀①(samudaya) 🟢⨀🤟▶️(hoti)  
thus / of this / of the entire / of the mass of suffering / origin / is  
*`kevalassa dukkhakkhandhassa`: 🚹⨀⑥, `-assa` {61}, "of", adjective and noun alike; `samudayo`: 🚹⨀①, `-o` {104}; [3: 3.12](../3-awakening/#u3-12).*

3. > Athassa kaṅkhā vapayanti sabbā,

⏏️(atha) *️⃣🤟🚹⨀⑥(ima) 🚺⨂①(kaṅkhā) 🟢⨂🤟▶️(vapayāti) *️⃣🤟🚺⨂①(sabba)  
then / his / doubts / vanish / all  
*`kaṅkhā`: 🚺⨂①, `-ā` {118}, with a plural verb; `sabbā` agrees, 🚺⨂①; [3: 6.3](../3-awakening/#u6-3).*

4. > pubbe ananussutesu dhammesu cakkhuṁ udapādi

⏏️(pubbe) 🚹⨂⑦🔽(ta)🆎(na+anussuta) 🚹⨂⑦(dhamma) 🚻⨀①(cakkhu) 🟢⨀🤟⏮(uppajjati)  
before / not heard / in dhammas / insight / arose  
*`ananussutesu dhammesu`: 🚹⨂⑦, `-esu` {101}, "in", on adjective and noun alike; [5: 5.1](../5-dhammacakka/#u5-1).*

5. > appiyehi sampayogo dukkho, piyehi vippayogo dukkho

🚹⨂③🆎(na+piya) 🚹⨀①(sampayoga) 🚹⨀①(dukkha) 🚹⨂③(piya) 🚹⨀①(vippayoga) 🚹⨀①(dukkha)  
with the unloved / association / suffering / from the loved / separation / suffering  
*`appiyehi`, `piyehi`: 🚹⨂③, `-ehi` {101}, "with"; `sampayogo`, `vippayogo` and the agreeing `dukkho`: 🚹⨀①, `-o` {104}; [5: 4.1](../5-dhammacakka/#u4-1).*

6. > Yato khayaṁ paccayānaṁ avedī”ti.

⏏️(yato) 🚹⨀②(khaya) 🚹⨂⑥(paccaya) 🟢⨀🤟⏮(vedeti) ⏏️(iti)  
since / the destruction / of conditions / knew / (end quote)  
*`khayaṁ`: 🚹⨀②, `-aṁ` {83}, the object; `paccayānaṁ`: 🚹⨂⑥, `-ānaṁ` {89}, "of"; [3: 9.4](../3-awakening/#u9-4).*

7. > Vidhūpayaṁ tiṭṭhati mārasenaṁ,

🚹⨀①🔽(anta)(vidhūpayanta) 🟢⨀🤟▶️(tiṭṭhati) 🚺⨀②🆎(māra+senā)  
scattering / stands / the army of Māra  
*`mārasenaṁ`: 🚺⨀②, the `-aṁ` of a lemma in `-ā` {83}, as `kaññaṁ`; `senā` ("army") is `fem`; [3: 12.3](../3-awakening/#u12-3).*

8. > Vimuttasmiṁ vimuttamiti ñāṇaṁ hoti.

🚻⨀⑦🔽(ta)(vimutta) 🚻⨀①🔽(ta)(vimutta) ⏏️(iti) 🚻⨀①(ñāṇa) 🟢⨀🤟▶️(hoti)  
in liberation / liberated / (end quote) / knowledge / there is  
*`vimuttasmiṁ`: 🚻⨀⑦, `-smiṁ` {55} beside `-e`, "in"; `vimuttaṁ` (in `vimuttamiti`) and `ñāṇaṁ`: 🚻⨀①, `-aṁ` {219}; [7: 11.3](../7-anatta/#u11-3).*
