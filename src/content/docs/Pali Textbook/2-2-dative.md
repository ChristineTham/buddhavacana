The usage examples provided here are not exhaustive, other usages are possible.

### 2.2.1 Recipient {276}

The `sampadāna` ("recipient") is the `kāraka` that receives:

* The one to whom someone wishes to give
* The one to whom something is pleasing
* The one to whom someone owes something

The word `vā` ("or") in the rule shows that these are alternatives: any one of
them makes the word a `sampadāna`.

#### Example

> **samaṇassa** cīvaraṁ dadāti

🚹⨀④(samaṇassa) 🚻⨀②(cīvaraṁ) 🟢⨀🤟▶️(dadāti)
to the ascetic / a robe / [he] gives
*[He] gives a robe to the ascetic.*

### 2.2.2 With various roots and words {277}

The `kāraka` is also termed `sampadāna`, and takes the ④ ending, in the
following constructions:

* With the roots `silāgha` (praising), `hanu` (denying), `ṭhā` (attending on),
  `sapa` (swearing), `dhāra` (owing) and `piha` (longing for)
* With the roots `kudha` (anger), `duha` (hostility) and `issa` (jealousy), and
  with roots in the sense of `usūya` (envy)
* With the roots `rādha` and `ikkha`
* For the agent of the earlier action, with `paṭissuṇāti` ("assents to"),
  `anugiṇāti` and `patigiṇāti` ("approves of")
* In the sense of announcing
* In the sense of purpose (`tadattha`)
* In the sense of an infinitive (`tumattha`)
* In the sense of `alaṁ` ("fit for", "enough")
* With `maññati` ("to think"), in contempt and of an inanimate thing
* For the object of roots in the sense of going
* In the sense of wishing well
* With `sammuti` ("consent") and `bhiyya` ("more")
* In the sense of the ⑦ ending

The word `attha` ("sense") in the rule extends it to many other constructions,
and the word `ca` ("and") to any other word used with a `sampadāna`.

#### 2.2.2.1 `silāgha` (“to praise”)

* The one who is praised, in construction with the root `silāgha`

> **buddhassa** silāghate

🚹⨀④(buddhassa) 🔵⨀🤟▶️(silāghate)
the Buddha / [he] extols
*[He] extols the Buddha.*

#### 2.2.2.2 `hanu` (“to deny”)

* The one to whom something is denied, in construction with the root `hanu`

> hanute **tuyha’meva**

🔵⨀🤟▶️(hanute) *️⃣🤘⨀④(tuyhaṁ) ⏏️(eva)
[he] denies / to you / only
*[He] denies [it] to you alone.*

#### 2.2.2.3 `ṭhā` (“to attend on”)

* The one attended on, in construction with the root `ṭhā` (here `upa` + `ṭhā`,
  "to stand near", "to attend on")

> upatiṭṭheyya **sakyaputtānaṁ** vaḍḍhakī

🟢⨀🤟⏯(upatiṭṭheyya) 🚹⨂④🆎(sakya+putta) 🚹⨀①(vaḍḍhakī)
would attend on / the sons of the Sakyans / the carpenter
*The carpenter would attend on the sons of the Sakyans.*

#### 2.2.2.4 `sapa` (“to swear”)

* The one to whom an oath is sworn, in construction with the root `sapa`

> **tuyhaṁ** sapate

*️⃣🤘⨀④(tuyhaṁ) 🔵⨀🤟▶️(sapate)
to you / [he] swears an oath
*[He] swears an oath to you.*

#### 2.2.2.5 `dhāra` (“to owe”)

* The one to whom something is owed, in construction with the root `dhāra`

> suvaṇṇaṁ **te** dhārayate

🚻⨀②(suvaṇṇaṁ) *️⃣🤘⨀④(te) 🔵⨀🤟▶️(dhārayate)
gold / to you / [he] owes
*[He] owes you gold.*

#### 2.2.2.6 `piha` (“to long for”)

* The one longed for or envied, in construction with the root `piha`

> **buddhassa** aññatitthiyā pihayanti

🚹⨀④(buddhassa) ⨂(🚹①🆎(añña+titthiya) 🟢🤟▶️(pihayanti))
the Buddha / those of other sects / envy
*Those of other sects envy the Buddha.*

#### 2.2.2.7 `kudha`, `duha`, `issa`, `usūya` (anger, hostility, jealousy, envy)

* The one with whom someone is angry, in construction with the root `kudha`
* The one to whom someone is hostile, in construction with the root `duha`
* The one of whom someone is jealous, in construction with the root `issa`
* The one who is envied, in construction with roots in the sense of `usūya`
  ("envy")

> kodhayati **devadattassa**

🟢⨀🤟▶️(kodhayati) 🚹⨀④(devadattassa)
[he] is angry / with Devadatta
*[He] is angry with Devadatta.*

#### 2.2.2.8 `rādha` and `ikkha`

* The one about whom there is an enquiry, who is not stated, in construction with
  the roots `rādha` ("to satisfy", "to offend") and `ikkha` ("to look"), so as
  to make the action known

Can also use with the ② `vibhatti` ending (eg. `rājānaṁ`).

> **ārādho’haṁ** rañño

🚹⨀①(ārādho) *️⃣👆⨀①(ahaṁ) 🚹⨀④(rañño)
winning favour / I / with the king
*I have won the king's favour.*

#### 2.2.2.9 The agent of the earlier action

* The agent of the earlier action, when the next verb is `paṭissuṇāti`
  ("assents to"), `anugiṇāti` or `patigiṇāti` ("approves of")

First there is an action with its agent:

> bhagavā bhikkhū etad’avoca

🚹⨀①(bhagavā) 🚹⨂②(bhikkhū) *️⃣🤟🚻⨀②(etad) 🟢⨀🤟⏮(avoca)
the Blessed One / to the monks / this / said
*The Blessed One said this to the monks.*

Here `bhikkhū` is the object that is not stated, `etaṁ` the object that is
stated, and the agent of the action is `bhagavā`. When the monks assent to what
was said, the agent of that earlier action takes the ④ ending:

> te bhikkhū **bhagavato** paccassosuṁ

🚹⨂①(*️⃣🤟(te) bhikkhū) 🚹⨀④(bhagavato) 🟢⨂🤟⏮(paccassosuṁ)
those / monks / to the Blessed One / assented
*Those monks assented to the Blessed One.*

The rule sums this up in a verse:

> Yo vadeti sa ‘kattā’ti, vuttaṁ ‘kamma’nti vuccati;
> yo paṭiggāhako tassa, ‘sampadānaṁ’ vijāniyā.

*The one who speaks is the `kattā` (agent), what is spoken is called the
`kamma` (object), and the one who receives it, know as the `sampadāna`.*

#### 2.2.2.10 Announcing

* The one to whom something is announced

> ārocayāmi **vo** bhikkhave

🟠⨀👆▶️(ārocayāmi) *️⃣🤘⨂④(vo) 🚹⨂⓪(bhikkhave)
I announce / to you / monks
*I announce to you, monks.*

#### 2.2.2.11 Purpose (`tadattha`)

* The purpose for which something is done (`tadattha`, "for the sake of that")

> ūnassa **pāripūriyā** taṁ cīvaraṁ nikkhipitabbaṁ

🚻⨀⑥(ūnassa) 🚺⨀④(pāripūriyā) 🚻⨀①(*️⃣🤟(taṁ) cīvaraṁ 🔽(tabba)(nikkhipitabbaṁ))
of what is lacking / for the completion / that / robe / is to be laid aside
*That robe is to be laid aside for the completion of what is lacking.*

#### 2.2.2.12 In the sense of an infinitive (`tumattha`)

* A noun with the ④ ending expressing what an infinitive (a verb form in `tuṁ`,
  "in order to …") would express

> **lokānukampāya atthāya hitāya sukhāya** devamanussānaṁ buddho loke uppajjati

🚺⨀④🆎(loka+anukampā) 🚹⨀④(atthāya) 🚻⨀④(hitāya) 🚻⨀④(sukhāya) 🚹⨂⑥🆎(deva+manussa) 🚹⨀①(buddho) 🚹⨀⑦(loke) 🟢⨀🤟▶️(uppajjati)
out of compassion for the world / for the good / for the welfare / for the happiness / of gods and men / a Buddha / in the world / arises
*A Buddha arises in the world out of compassion for the world, for the good, the welfare and the happiness of gods and men.*

#### 2.2.2.13 `alaṁ` (“fit for”, “enough”)

* In construction with `alaṁ`, which has the senses of being fit for
  (`arahati`) and of refusing (`paṭikkhitta`, "enough of")

> **alaṁ me** buddho

⏏️(alaṁ) *️⃣👆⨀④(me) 🚹⨀①(buddho)
is enough, is fit / for me / the Buddha
*The Buddha is enough for me.*

#### 2.2.2.14 `maññati` (“to think”) in contempt, of an inanimate thing

* The inanimate thing to which someone is likened in contempt, in construction
  with `maññati` ("to think", "to consider")

> **kaṭṭhassa** tuvaṁ maññe

🚻⨀④(kaṭṭhassa) *️⃣🤘⨀②(tuvaṁ) 🔵⨀👆▶️(maññe)
a stick / you / I think [to be]
*I take you for a stick.*

Where there is no contempt, or the thing is a living being, the ② ending is used
instead:

> suvaṇṇaṁ viya taṁ maññe

🚻⨀②(suvaṇṇaṁ) ⏏️(viya) *️⃣🤟⨀②(taṁ) 🔵⨀👆▶️(maññe)
gold / like / that / I think [to be]
*I think that [to be] like gold.*

> gadrabhaṁ tuvaṁ maññe

🚹⨀②(gadrabhaṁ) *️⃣🤘⨀②(tuvaṁ) 🔵⨀👆▶️(maññe)
a donkey / you / I think [to be]
*I take you for a donkey.*

#### 2.2.2.15 The object of verbs of going

* The place gone to, the object of a root in the sense of going

Can also use with the ② `vibhatti` ending (eg. `gāmaṁ`).

> **gāmassa** pādena gato

🚹⨀④(gāmassa) 🚹⨀③(pādena) 🚹⨀①🔽(ta)(gato)
to the village / on foot / gone
*Gone to the village on foot.*

#### 2.2.2.16 Wishing well

* The one for whom something good is wished

> **āyasmato** dīghāyuko hotu

🚹⨀④(āyasmato) 🚹⨀①🆎(dīgha+āyuka) 🟢⨀🤟⏹(hotu)
for the venerable one / long-lived / may [he] be
*May the venerable one be long-lived.*

#### 2.2.2.17 `sammuti` (“consent”)

* In construction with `sammuti` ("consent", "permission")

> aññatra saṅghasammutiyā **bhikkhussa** vippavatthuṁ na vaṭṭati

⏏️(aññatra) 🚺⨀③🆎(saṅgha+sammuti) 🚹⨀④(bhikkhussa) 🔽(tuṁ)(vippavatthuṁ) ⏏️(na) 🟢⨀🤟▶️(vaṭṭati)
except / by the consent of the Saṅgha / for a monk / to live apart / not / it is proper
*Except by the consent of the Saṅgha, it is not proper for a monk to live apart.*

#### 2.2.2.18 `bhiyya` (“more”)

* In construction with `bhiyya` ("more")

> bhiyyoso **mattāya**

⏏️(bhiyyoso) 🚺⨀④(mattāya)
still more / to a measure
*To a still greater measure (exceedingly).*

#### 2.2.2.19 In the sense of ⑦

* In the sense of the ⑦ `vibhatti` ending: the person before whom something is
  made plain or appears

> **tuyhañ’c’assa** āvi karomi

*️⃣🤘⨀④(tuyhaṁ) ⏏️(ca) *️⃣🤟🚹⨀④(assa) ⏏️(āvi) 🟢⨀👆▶️(karomi)
to you / and / to him / openly / I make
*I make [it] plain to you and to him.*

#### 2.2.2.20 Other constructions (`attha`)

* By the mention of `attha` ("sense") in the rule, the ④ ending is seen in many
  other constructions of words
* Also in the essential sense (`sārattha`)
* In the remaining constructions too, other usages are to be applied by those
  who are discerning in usage

> upamaṁ **te** karissāmi

🚺⨀②(upamaṁ) *️⃣🤘⨀④(te) 🟢⨀👆⏭(karissāmi)
a simile / for you / I shall make
*I shall make a simile for you.*

#### 2.2.2.21 Other words used with a `sampadāna` (`ca`, `vā`)

* The word `ca` ("and") in the rule draws in the `vā` ("or") of the previous
  rule, so that any other word used with a `sampadāna` that the rule has not
  pointed out is also included, such as `pabhū` ("master over")
* In some places the ④ ending is also used in the senses of the ②, ③, ⑤, ⑥ and
  ⑦ endings

> **bhikkhusaṅghassa** pabhū ayaṁ bhagavā

🚹⨀④🆎(bhikkhu+saṅgha) 🚹⨀①(pabhū) *️⃣🤟🚹⨀①(ayaṁ) 🚹⨀①(bhagavā)
over the community of monks / master / this / Blessed One
*This Blessed One is master over the community of monks.*
