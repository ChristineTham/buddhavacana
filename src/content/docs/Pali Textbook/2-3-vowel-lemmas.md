After `purisa`, `citta` and `kaññā` ([2.2](../2-2-nouns/)), the lemmas a
reader meets most end in `i` or `u`: `bhikkhu`, `jāti` (birth), `cakkhu` (eye,
insight), `sati` (awareness), `dhātu` (element), the long-vowel feminines
`nadī` (river) and `itthī` (woman), and the `-ī` words meaning "one who has
…": `ātāpī` (ardent), `sukhī` (happy). The endings are the `si`, `yo`, `aṁ`,
`nā` … of chapter 1 {55}, but the `a`-rules do not apply, and the vowel itself
lengthens, shortens or becomes `y` as the ending demands. Kaccāyana names these
lemma-endings `jha` and `la` in a masculine or neuter word {58} and `pa` in a
feminine {59}; [Appendix G](../g-vibhatti/) uses the names.

The question is the one of 2.2: which ending, and so which number and which
job? The tables give the shapes that differ from `purisa` and `kaññā`; what is
not listed is as there (`-smiṁ` is added unchanged: `bhikkhusmiṁ`).

### Masculine lemmas in `i` and `u`: `aggi` (fire), `bhikkhu`

| what you see | number | ending | example | rule |
| --- | :-: | --- | --- | :-: |
| `-i`, `-u` | ⨀ | ①: `si` dropped | `aggi`, `bhikkhu` | {220} |
| `-ī`, `-ū` | ⨂ | ① ②: `yo` dropped, the vowel lengthened | `aggī`, `bhikkhū` | {118}, {88} |
| `-ayo`, `-avo` | ⨂ | ① ②: the other shape of `yo` | `aggayo`, `bhikkhavo` | {96}; {119}, {97} |
| `-iṁ`, `-uṁ` | ⨀ | ② | `aggiṁ`, `bhikkhuṁ` | {82} |
| `-inā`, `-unā` | ⨀ | ③, and ⑤ | `agginā`, `bhikkhunā` | {55}; {215} |
| `-ino`, `-uno`; `-issa`, `-ussa` | ⨀ | ④ ⑥ | `aggino`, `aggissa`; `bhikkhuno`, `bhikkhussa` | {117}; {61} |
| `-īhi`/`-ūhi`, `-īnaṁ`/`-ūnaṁ`, `-īsu`/`-ūsu` | ⨂ | ③ ⑤, ④ ⑥, ⑦: the vowel lengthened | `aggīhi`, `bhikkhūnaṁ`, `aggīsu` | {89} |
| `-imhi`, `-umhi` | ⨀ | ⑦, beside `-ismiṁ` | `byādhimhi` | {99} |
| `-i`, `-u` | ⨀ | ⓪: `ga` dropped | `bho aggi` | {220} |
| `-ave`, `-avo` | ⨂ | ⓪ | `bhikkhave`, `bhikkhavo` | {116}, {97} |

The ⓪ plural is the same as the ① plural. A masculine in long `-ī` (`daṇḍī`
"staff-bearer", `ātāpī`, `sukhī`) keeps the long vowel only in the ① singular
{85}; elsewhere it is shortened {84}: `daṇḍino` ① ② plural {225} and ④ ⑥
singular {117}, `daṇḍinaṁ` ② {224}, `daṇḍinā` ③ ⑤ {215}, `daṇḍini` ⑦ {226},
`bho daṇḍi` ⓪ {245}; the plurals `-īnaṁ`, `-ūhi` are as above {89}.

### Feminine lemmas: `ratti` (night), `itthī`, `nadī`, `yāgu` (gruel), `dhenu` (cow), `vadhū` (young wife)

| what you see | number | ending | example | rule |
| --- | :-: | --- | --- | :-: |
| `-i`, `-ī`, `-u`, `-ū` | ⨀ | ①: `si` dropped, a long vowel kept | `ratti`, `itthī`, `nadī`, `yāgu`, `vadhū` | {220}, {85} |
| `-ī`, `-ū` | ⨂ | ① ②: `yo` dropped, the vowel lengthened | `rattī`, `itthī`, `yāgū`, `vadhū` | {118}, {88} |
| `-iyo`, `-uyo` | ⨂ | ① ②: `yo` kept, a long vowel shortened | `rattiyo`, `itthiyo`, `nadiyo`, `yāguyo` | {55}; {84} |
| `-iṁ`, `-uṁ` | ⨀ | ②, a long vowel shortened | `rattiṁ`, `itthiṁ`, `nadiṁ`, `vadhuṁ` | {82}, {84} |
| `-iyā`, `-uyā` | ⨀ | ③ ④ ⑤ ⑥ ⑦: one shape for five jobs | `rattiyā`, `itthiyā`, `nadiyā`, `dhenuyā` | {112} |
| `-iyaṁ`, `-uyaṁ` | ⨀ | ⑦ | `rattiyaṁ`, `itthiyaṁ`, `yāguyaṁ` | {216} |
| `-yā` | ⨀ | `-iyā` with the `i` become `y` | `ratyā`; `najjā` (`nadyā`, the `dy` said `jj`) | {72} |
| `-īhi`/`-ūhi`, `-īnaṁ`/`-ūnaṁ`, `-īsu`/`-ūsu` | ⨂ | ③ ⑤, ④ ⑥, ⑦: the vowel lengthened | `rattīhi`, `itthīnaṁ`, `rattīsu` | {89} |
| `-i`, `-u` | ⨀ | ⓪: a long vowel shortened | `bhoti itthi`, `bhoti vadhu` | {245} |

The ⓪ plural is the same as the ① plural (`dhenuyo`, `yāguyo`); a feminine
in `-ī` and one in `-i` differ only in the ① singular (`itthī` against
`ratti`).

### Neuter lemmas in `i` and `u`: `aṭṭhi` (bone), `cakkhu`, `āyu` (life)

| what you see | number | ending | example | rule |
| --- | :-: | --- | --- | :-: |
| `-i`, `-u` | ⨀ | ① | `aṭṭhi`, `cakkhu`, `āyu` | {220} |
| `-iṁ`, `-uṁ` | ⨀ | ② | `dāruṁ` (wood), `cakkhuṁ` | {82} |
| `-īni`, `-ūni` | ⨂ | ① ②: `yo` becomes `ni`, the vowel lengthened | `aṭṭhīni`, `āyūni` | {217}, {88} |
| `-ī`, `-ū` | ⨂ | ① ②: `yo` dropped | `aṭṭhī`, `āyū` | {118}, {88} |

The other endings are those of `aggi` and `bhikkhu`. In the texts the neuter
`-uṁ` also serves as ①, as the `-aṁ` of a neuter in `a` does {219}: `cakkhuṁ
udapādi`, "insight arose" ([5: 5.1](../5-dhammacakka/#u5-1)).

:::note[The same ending, more than one job]
`-ī` or `-ū` ending a word is the ① or ② plural of an `i`/`u` lemma
(`bhikkhū`), but the ① singular of a lemma that ends in the long vowel
(`itthī`, `daṇḍī`, `viññū` "wise"): the dictionary form decides. `-ino` is ④
or ⑥ singular (`aggino`, `ātāpino`), but after a long-`ī` lemma also ① or ②
plural (`sukhino`). `-iyā` is any of ③ to ⑦ singular of a feminine
(`rattiyā`): the sentence decides, as [chapter 4](../4-0-karaka/) explains.
`-iṁ`/`-uṁ` is ② singular (`bhikkhuṁ`), but for a neuter ① too (`cakkhuṁ`).
:::

### Examples

> Tatra kho bhagavā pañcavaggiye **bhikkhū** āmantesi—

⏏️(tatra) ⏏️(kho) 🚹⨀①(bhagavant) 🚹⨂②🆎(pañca+vaggiya) 🚹⨂②(bhikkhu) 🟢⨀🤟⏮(āmanteti)  
there / indeed / the Bhagavā / of the group of five / bhikkhū / addressed  
*There the Bhagavā addressed the group of five bhikkhū:*  
*`bhikkhū`: `-ū`, the ② plural, `yo` dropped and the `u` lengthened {118}, {88}, the object of `āmantesi`; `pañcavaggiye` agrees with it; [5: 1.2](../5-dhammacakka/#u1-2).*

> **jātipi** dukkhā, jarāpi dukkhā, **byādhipi** dukkho

🚺⨀①(jāti) ⏏️(api) 🚺⨀①(dukkha) 🚺⨀①(jarā) ⏏️(api) 🚺⨀①(dukkha) 🚹⨀①(byādhi) ⏏️(api) 🚹⨀①(dukkha)  
birth / too / suffering / aging / too / suffering / illness / too / suffering  
*Birth is `dukkha` (suffering), aging is `dukkha`, illness is `dukkha`.*  
*`jāti`, `byādhi`: `-i`, the ① singular {220}, with `pi` (`api`) joined on; `dukkhā` and `dukkho` show `jāti` feminine, `byādhi` masculine; [5: 4.1](../5-dhammacakka/#u4-1).*

> **rattiyā** paṭhamaṁ yāmaṁ

🚺⨀⑥(ratti) 🚹⨀②(paṭhama) 🚹⨀②(yāma)  
of the night / first / watch  
*during the first watch of the night*  
*`rattiyā`: `-iyā`, the feminine singular shape for ③ to ⑦ {112}; here ⑥, "of the night"; [3: 2.3](../3-awakening/#u2-3).*

> Dveme, **bhikkhave**, antā pabbajitena na sevitabbā.

⚧⨂①(dvi) *️⃣🤟🚹⨂①(ima) 🚹⨂⓪(bhikkhu) 🚹⨂①(anta) 🚹⨀③🔽(ta)(pabbajita) ⏏️(na) 🚹⨂①🔽(tabba)(sevitabba)  
two / these / bhikkhave / extremes / by one gone forth / not / should be cultivated  
*"These two extremes, bhikkhave, should not be cultivated by one who has gone forth.*  
*`bhikkhave`: `-ave`, the ⓪ plural {116}, the `u` become `a` before `ve` {97}: "bhikkhus!"; [5: 2.1](../5-dhammacakka/#u2-1).*

### Exercises

For the word in bold (from chapters [5](../5-dhammacakka/), [7](../7-anatta/),
[9](../9-fire/) and [11](../11-pbt/)) name the lemma and its gender, the
ending, the number and the job, and cite the rule.

useful for looking up words, as well as doing the sandhi and compound word

3. Attamanā pañcavaggiyā **bhikkhū** bhagavato bhāsitaṁ abhinandunti.

### Answers

*Note: please do the exercises first before checking the answers.*

1. > akuppā me **vimutti**, ayamantimā **jāti**

🚺⨀①🆎(na+kuppa) *️⃣👆⚧⨀⑥(ahaṁ) 🚺⨀①(vimutti) *️⃣🤟🚺⨀①(ima) 🚺⨀①(antima) 🚺⨀①(jāti)  
unshakeable / my / liberation / this / final / birth  
*Unshakeable is my liberation, this is the final birth.*  
*`vimutti`, `jāti`: feminine, `-i`, the ① singular {220}; `akuppā` and `antimā` agree in the feminine `-ā`; [5: 10.2](../5-dhammacakka/#u10-2).*

2. > anuttaraṁ **sammāsambodhiṁ** abhisambuddho

🚺⨀②🆎(na+uttara) 🚺⨀②🆎(sammā+sambodhi) 🚹⨀①🔽(ta)(abhisambuddha)  
unsurpassed / perfect understanding / fully awakened  
*woken up to perfect understanding unsurpassed by anything*  
*`sammāsambodhiṁ`: feminine `sammāsambodhi`, `-iṁ`, the ② singular {82}, the object of `abhisambuddho`; [5: 10.1](../5-dhammacakka/#u10-1).*

3. > Attamanā pañcavaggiyā **bhikkhū** bhagavato bhāsitaṁ abhinandunti.

🚹⨂①🆎(atta+mana) 🚹⨂①🆎(pañca+vaggiya) 🚹⨂①(bhikkhu) 🚹⨀⑥(bhagavant) 🚻⨀②🔽(ta)(bhāsita) 🟢⨂🤟⏮(abhinandati) ⏏️(iti)  
delighted / of the group of five / bhikkhū / of the Bhagavā / what was spoken / rejoiced in / (end quote)  
*The group of five bhikkhū, delighted, rejoiced in the Bhagavā's words.*  
*`bhikkhū`: masculine, `-ū`, here the ① plural {118}, {88}, the subject of `abhinanduṁ`; in the first example the same shape was ②; [5: 10.4](../5-dhammacakka/#u10-4).*

4. > Adhigatamidaṁ **bahūhi**,

🚻⨀①(adhigata) 🚻⨀①(ima) 🚹⨂③(bahu)  
achieved / this / by many  
*This is achieved by many,*  
*`bahūhi`: masculine `bahu` (many), `-ūhi`, the ③ plural, the `u` lengthened before `hi` {89}, "by"; [11: 49, 66.1](../11-pbt/#s49-u66-1).*

5. > Evaṁ khandhā ca **dhātuyo**,

🔼(evaṁ) 🚹⨂①(khandha) 🔼(ca) 🚺⨂①(dhātu)  
thus / collections / and / elements  
*Thus, the collections and elements,*  
*`dhātuyo`: feminine `dhātu`, `-uyo`, the ① plural, `yo` kept {55}, as `khandhā` beside it; [11: 20, 6.1](../11-pbt/#s20-u6-1).*

6. > pañcavaggiyānaṁ **bhikkhūnaṁ** anupādāya āsavehi cittāni vimucciṁsūti

🚹⨂⑥🆎(pañca+vaggiya) 🚹⨂⑥(bhikkhu) 🔽(ya)(anupādiyati) 🚹⨂⑤(āsava) 🚻⨂①(citta) 🔵⨂🤟⏮(vimuccati) ⏏️(iti)  
of the group of five / bhikkhū / not grasping / from the defilements / minds / were liberated / (end quote)  
*the `cittāni` (minds) of the group of five bhikkhū, `anupādāya` (not grasping), were liberated from the `āsavā` (defilements).*  
*`bhikkhūnaṁ`: masculine `bhikkhu`, `-ūnaṁ`, the ⑥ plural, the `u` lengthened before `naṁ` {89}; `pañcavaggiyānaṁ` agrees with it; [7: 12.3](../7-anatta/#u12-3).*

7. > **Rāgagginā**, dosagginā, mohagginā ādittaṁ, **jātiyā** jarāya maraṇena

🚹⨀③🆎(rāga+aggi) 🚹⨀③🆎(dosa+aggi) 🚹⨀③🆎(moha+aggi) 🚻⨀①🔽(ta)(āditta) 🚺⨀③(jāti) 🚺⨀③(jarā) 🚻⨀③(maraṇa)  
with the fire of passion / with the fire of hatred / with the fire of delusion / burning / with birth / with aging / with death  
*Burning with the fire of passion (`rāgaggi`), with the fire of hatred (`dosaggi`), with the fire of delusion (`mohaggi`); burning with birth, aging, death*  
*`rāgagginā`: masculine `aggi` ending the compound, `-inā`, the ③ singular {55}; `jātiyā`: feminine `jāti`, `-iyā` {112}, here ③, "with birth"; [9: 2.6](../9-fire/#u2-6).*

8. > Tañce, āvuso, **bhikkhuṁ** pare aniṭṭhehi akantehi amanāpehi samudācaranti—

🔼(tañce) 🔼(āvuso) 🚹⨀②(bhikkhu) 🚹⨂①(para) 🚹⨂③(aniṭṭha) 🚹⨂③(akanta) 🚹⨂③(amanāpa) ▶️🤟⨂(samudācarati)  
if that / friend / bhikkhu / others / with unpleasant / disagreeable / dislikeable / behave  
*Friend, if others behave with unpleasantness, disagreeableness, dislikeableness to that bhikkhu -*  
*`bhikkhuṁ`: masculine, `-uṁ`, the ② singular {82}; `taṁ` in `tañce` agrees with it; [11: 45, 9.1](../11-pbt/#s45-u9-1).*
