The affixes of section [10.7](../10-7-sadhana/) make words by the hundred. Those
here each go after a few named roots, and Kaccāyana lists the words one by
one: `tha` after the root `samu` gives `samatho` ("calm") {628}, `īvara` after
`ci` gives `cīvaraṁ` ("a robe") {668}, `tu` after `hi` gives `hetu` ("a
cause") {671}. Nothing in the shape of `hetu` says it is `√hi` + `tu`: there
is no pattern to learn, only words to know, many of them on every page of the
texts.

When you meet one, the question is not "which affix?" but "is this a word to
look up?". The dictionary gives the root and the affix, sometimes cut
differently from Kaccāyana (`attho` is `√ar` + `tha` there), and the rule
number leads to Kaccāyana's account, usually a short sentence on the root's
meaning: `samatho`, "it calms".

### Words worth knowing

Mostly from the texts of chapters [3](../3-awakening/) and [5](../11-pbt/).

| word | meaning | root + affix | rule |
| --- | --- | --- | :-: |
| `attā` | the self | `√ada` + `mana`, `t` for `m` | {627} |
| `khemo` | safety | `√khi` + `mana` | {627} |
| `samatho` | calm | `√samu` + `tha` | {628} |
| `gehaṁ` | a house | `√gaha` + `a`, `a` → `e` | {629} |
| `saddhā` | faith | `saṁ` + `√dhā` + `a` | {638} |
| `vijjā` | knowledge | `√vida` + `ṇya` | {638} |
| `saccaṁ` | truth | `√sata` + `ya` | {638} |
| `buddho` | the Awakened One | `√budha` + `tta` | {643} |
| `cittaṁ` | the mind | `√citi` + `ta` | {656} |
| `ratti` | night | `√ranja` + `tti` | {658} |
| `attho` | meaning | `√asa` + `tha`, `asa` → `at` | {660} |
| `khandho` | an aggregate | `√khāda` → `khandha` | {664} |
| `kusalaṁ` | wholesome | `√kusa` + `ala` | {665} |
| `cīvaraṁ` | a robe | `√ci` + `īvara` | {668} |
| `hetu` | a cause | `√hi` + `tu` | {671} |
| `dhātu` | an element | `√dhā` + `tu` | {671} |
| `dhammo` | the teaching | `√dhara` + `ramma` | {531} |
| `bhikkhu` | a monk | `√bhikkha` + `rū` | {535} |

`dhammo` and `bhikkhu`, the commonest nouns of all, come from the `kita`
chapter ([Appendix J](../j-kita/)); `loko` and `maggo` have no row, since
Kaccāyana names neither.

### The rest of the group

cites.
