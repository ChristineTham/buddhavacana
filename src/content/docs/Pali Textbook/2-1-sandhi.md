Pāḷi was spoken before it was written, and the texts print it as it was said:
when a word ending in a vowel or in `ṁ` meets the next word, the two run
together and the seam changes shape. The joined form is a `sandhi` (a join),
and the texts of chapters [3](../3-awakening/), [5](../5-dhammacakka/),
[7](../7-anatta/), [9](../9-fire/) and the [bonus chapter](../11-pbt/) have one in almost
every line: `yo cāyaṁ` is `yo ca ayaṁ`, `natthi` is `na atthi`, `kiñci` is
`kiṁ ci`, and the `”ti` that closes every quotation is `iti` with its vowel
gone.

So the first question to ask of a word the dictionary does not have is: is it
two words? The table lists the joins the reader meets most, what each was
before it was joined, and the rule that makes it; the rule number links to
Kaccāyana's statement with its examples, and [Appendix E](../e-sandhi/) tables
all the rules {12}–{51}. Chapter 1 showed the joining once (`tatrāyamādi`);
here the direction is reversed.

### The joins to recognise

| what you see | what it was | examples from the texts | rule |
| --- | --- | --- | :-: |
| the first word's final vowel missing | `pana idaṁ` | `panidaṁ`, `natthi` (`na atthi`), `vivicceva` | {12} |
| a long vowel for two short ones: the first dropped, the second lengthened | `ca ayaṁ` | `cāyaṁ`, `tāvāhaṁ` (`tāva ahaṁ`), `yannūnāhaṁ` | {12}, {15} |
| `o’ti`, `ova`: `iti`, `iva` after `o`, the `i` dropped | `abhisambuddho iti` | `abhisambuddho’ti`, `sūriyova` | {13} |
| `ī”ti`: `iti` after `i`, the first `i` dropped, the second lengthened | `hoti iti` | `hotī”ti`, `kiñcī’ti` | {12}, {15} |
| `n”ti`, `mpi`: `iti`, `api` after `ṁ`, the first vowel dropped, the `ṁ` turned into `n` or `m` (the word's own ending is `-aṁ`, never `-an`) | `nirodhadhammaṁ iti`, `maraṇaṁ api` | `nirodhadhamman”ti`, `maraṇampi`, `yampicchaṁ` (`yaṁ api icchaṁ`) | {40}, {31} |
| `ṅ`, `ñ`, `ṇ`, `n`, `m` before `k`, `c`, `ṭ`, `t`, `p`: a `ṁ` turned into the nasal of that group | `kiṁ ci` | `kiñci`, `imasmiñca`, `saṅkhittena` ([5: 4.1](../5-dhammacakka/#u4-1) keeps the `ṁ`: `saṁkhittena` {30}) | {31} |
| `m` or `d` between a word in `ṁ` and a vowel | `evaṁ etassa` | `evametassa`, `ayamantimā`, `yadidaṁ` (`yaṁ idaṁ`) | {34} |
| a `y` (or `v`, `m`, `d`, `n`, `t`, `r`, `ḷ`) between two vowels, neither dropped | `tassā eva` | `tassāyeva`, `nayidaṁ` (`na idaṁ`) | {35} |
| a doubled consonant after a short prefix or first word | `vi payogo` | `vippayogo`, `appaṭivattiyaṁ` | {28} |
| `kkh`, `cch`, `ṭṭh`, `tth`, `pph`: an aspirate doubled with the first letter of its group (`jjh`, `ddh`, `bbh` with the third) | `dukkha khandha` | `dukkhakkhandhassa`, `pañcupādānakkhandhā` | {29} |
| `pacc-`: the prefix `pati` before a vowel | `pati aññāsiṁ` | `paccaññāsiṁ` | {19} |

### How to split a word

1. Find the seam: a long vowel before a short tail (`cāyaṁ`), an apostrophe
   or quotation mark, an `m`, `d`, `n`, `ñ` or `ṅ` no dictionary form has
   (`evametassa`, `kiñci`), a doubled consonant after a one-syllable prefix
   (`appaṭivattiyaṁ`), a `y` between two vowels (`tassāyeva`). Then undo what
   the rule did: put the dropped vowel back, shorten the lengthened one, turn
   the nasal back into `ṁ`, take the `y` out, make the double consonant
   single.
2. Try the small words first: `ca`, `eva`, `api`, `iti`, `pana`, `na`, `ayaṁ`,
   `idaṁ`, `ahaṁ` or a pronoun in `ṁ` (`yaṁ`, `taṁ`, `evaṁ`) is on one side of
   nearly every join.
3. Look both halves up: the Digital Pāḷi Dictionary lists the common joins as
   headwords (`cāyaṁ`: `ca + ayaṁ`) and splits the rest. If no split gives two
   dictionary words, it is one word, a compound ([chapter 6](../6-0-samasa/))
   or a prefixed word ([2.6](../2-6-upasagga/)) with the join inside it:
   `dukkhakkhandha`, `vippayoga`.

### Examples

> Yo **cāyaṁ** kāmesu kāmasukhallikānuyogo

*️⃣🤟🚹⨀①(ya) ⏏️(ca) *️⃣🤟🚹⨀①(ima) 🚹⨂⑦(kāma) 🚹⨀①🆎(kāma+sukhallikā+anuyoga)  
which / and / this / in sensual pleasures / pursuit of sensual happiness  
*`cāyaṁ` = `ca ayaṁ`: the `a` of `ca` dropped {12}, the next `a` lengthened {15}; [5: 2.3](../5-dhammacakka/#u2-3).*

> Taṁ kho **panidaṁ** dukkhaṁ ariyasaccaṁ **pariññeyyan’ti**

*️⃣🤟🚻⨀①(ta) ⏏️(kho) ⏏️(pana) *️⃣🤟🚻⨀①(ima) 🚻⨀①(dukkha) 🚻⨀①🆎(ariya+sacca) 🚻⨀①🔽(ṇya)(pariññeyya) ⏏️(iti)  
that / indeed / now / this / suffering / realisation / to be fully understood / thus  
*`panidaṁ` = `pana idaṁ`: the `a` of `pana` dropped {12}; `pariññeyyan’ti` = `pariññeyyaṁ iti`: the `i` of `iti` dropped {40}, `ṁ` becomes `n` {31}; [5: 5.2](../5-dhammacakka/#u5-2).*

> **Evametassa** kevalassa **dukkhakkhandhassa** nirodho hotī”ti

⏏️(evaṁ) *️⃣🤟🚹⨀⑥(eta) 🚹⨀⑥(kevala) 🚹⨀⑥🆎(dukkha+khandha) 🚹⨀①(nirodha) 🟢⨀🤟▶️(hoti) ⏏️(iti)  
thus / of this / of the entire / of the mass of suffering / cessation / is / (end quote)  
*`evametassa` = `evaṁ etassa`: `ṁ` becomes `m` before a vowel {34}; `dukkhakkhandhassa` = `dukkha` + `khandha`: `kh` doubled as `kkh` {29}; [3: 4.12](../3-awakening/#u4-12).*

> yo **tassāyeva** taṇhāya asesavirāganirodho

*️⃣🤟🚹⨀①(ya) *️⃣🤟🚺⨀⑥(ta) ⏏️(eva) 🚺⨀⑥(taṇhā) 🚹⨀①🆎(asesa+virāga+nirodha)  
which / of that / very / craving / remainderless fading away and cessation  
*`tassāyeva` = `tassā eva`: neither vowel dropped; a `y` put between them {35}; [5: 4.3](../5-dhammacakka/#u4-3).*

### Exercises

Each phrase, from chapters [5](../5-dhammacakka/), [9](../9-fire/) and
the [bonus chapter](../11-pbt/), has a join in bold: split it into its parts, say what the
join dropped, lengthened, changed or doubled, and name the rule.

:::tip[Hint]
You may find the [Digital Pali Dictionary](https://digitalpalidictionary.github.io/)
useful for looking up words, as well as doing the sandhi and compound word
deconstruction.
:::

1. **natthi** dāni punabbhavo
2. **Imasmiñca** pana veyyākaraṇasmiṁ bhaññamāne
3. **Yampidaṁ** cakkhusamphassapaccayā uppajjati vedayitaṁ
4. yaṁ **kiñci** samudayadhammaṁ sabbaṁ taṁ **nirodhadhamman”ti**
5. ‘anuttaraṁ sammāsambodhiṁ **abhisambuddho’ti**
6. So **vivicceva** kāmehi
7. piyehi **vippayogo** dukkho

### Answers

*Note: please do the exercises first before checking the answers.*

1. > **natthi** dāni punabbhavo

⏏️(na) 🟢⨀🤟▶️(atthi) ⏏️(dāni) 🚹⨀①🆎(puna+bhava)  
not / there is / now / appearing again  
*`natthi` = `na atthi`: the `a` of `na` dropped {12}, nothing lengthened; [5: 10.2](../5-dhammacakka/#u10-2).*

2. > **Imasmiñca** pana veyyākaraṇasmiṁ bhaññamāne

*️⃣🤟🚻⨀⑦(ima) ⏏️(ca) ⏏️(pana) 🚻⨀⑦(veyyākaraṇa) 🚻⨀⑦🔽(māna)(bhaññamāna)  
in this / and / moreover / detailed exposition / being spoken  
*`imasmiñca` = `imasmiṁ ca`: the `ṁ` of the ⑦ ending becomes `ñ` before `c` {31}; [5: 11.1](../5-dhammacakka/#u11-1).*

3. > **Yampidaṁ** cakkhusamphassapaccayā uppajjati vedayitaṁ

*️⃣🤟🚻⨀①(ya) ⏏️(api) *️⃣🤟🚻⨀①(ima) 🚹⨀⑤🆎(cakkhu+samphassa+paccaya) 🟢⨀🤟▶️(uppajjati) 🚻⨀①🔽(ta)(vedayita)  
what / too / this / with eye-contact as cause / arises / feeling  
*`yampidaṁ` = `yaṁ api idaṁ`: the `a` of `api` dropped {40}, `ṁ` becomes `m` before `p` {31}, the `i` of `pi` dropped {12}; [9: 2.4](../9-fire/#u2-4).*

4. > yaṁ **kiñci** samudayadhammaṁ sabbaṁ taṁ **nirodhadhamman”ti**

*️⃣🤟🚻⨀①(ya) *️⃣🤟🚻⨀①(kaci) 🚻⨀①🆎(samudaya+dhamma) *️⃣🤟🚻⨀①(sabba) *️⃣🤟🚻⨀①(ta) 🚻⨀①🆎(nirodha+dhamma) ⏏️(iti)  
whatever / anything / subject to origination / all / that / subject to cessation / (end quote)  
*`kiñci` = `kiṁ ci`: `ṁ` becomes `ñ` before `c` {31}. `nirodhadhamman”ti` = `nirodhadhammaṁ iti`: the `i` of `iti` dropped {40}, `ṁ` becomes `n` {31}; [5: 11.1](../5-dhammacakka/#u11-1).*

5. > ‘anuttaraṁ sammāsambodhiṁ **abhisambuddho’ti**

🚺⨀②🆎(na+uttara) 🚺⨀②🆎(sammā+sambodhi) 🚹⨀①🔽(ta)(abhisambuddha) ⏏️(iti)  
unsurpassed / perfect awakening / fully awakened / thus  
*`abhisambuddho’ti` = `abhisambuddho iti`: after `o` the `i` of `iti` is dropped {13}; [5: 9.1](../5-dhammacakka/#u9-1).*

6. > So **vivicceva** kāmehi

*️⃣🤟🚹⨀①(ta) 🔽(ya)(viviccati) ⏏️(eva) 🚹⨂⑤(kāma)  
that / secluding oneself from / just / sensual pleasures  
*`vivicceva` = `vivicca eva`: the `a` of `vivicca` dropped {12}; [Bonus: 38, 10.1](../11-pbt/#s38-u10-1).*

7. > piyehi **vippayogo** dukkho

🚹⨂⑤(piya) 🚹⨀①(vippayoga) 🚹⨀①(dukkha)  
from the loved / separation / suffering  
*`vippayogo` = `vi` + `payogo`: the `p` doubled after the prefix `vi` {28}; [5: 4.1](../5-dhammacakka/#u4-1).*
