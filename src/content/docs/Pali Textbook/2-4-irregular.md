Most nouns follow [2.2](../2-2-nouns/) and [2.3](../2-3-vowel-lemmas/). A few
dozen very common words do not. Their endings do the same jobs; what differs
is the shape, and the lemma never shows itself: no text has `rāja`, only
`rājā`, `rañño`.

So the question of 2.2, "which ending, and so which job?", gets one step in
front of it: "which of this word's few shapes is this?" The shapes recur:
`-ā` is ① (`rājā`, `bhagavā`), `-ānaṁ` is ② (`attānaṁ`), `-no` and `-to` are
④⑥ (`rañño`, `bhagavato`), `-nā` and `-tā` are ③ (`brahmunā`, `bhagavatā`),
`-ni` and `-i` are ⑦ (`attani`, `manasi`). The texts' symbol lines use the
dictionary's lemmas, `bhagavant`, `satthar`, `pitar`, `manas`; Kaccāyana's
are `bhagavantu`, `satthu`, `pitu`, `mana`. [Appendix G](../g-vibhatti/)
tables every rule cited below.

### `satthā`, `pitā`, `mātā`

The lemmas `satthu`, `pitu`, `mātu` 🚺 put `-ār-` or `-ar-` before most

### `bhagavā`, `āyasmā`, `arahaṁ` and the participles in `-ant`

`bhagavā` and `āyasmā` end in the affix `ntu` ("having"); the participles in
`-ant` and `mahanta` ("great") share these endings in every form but ① {187}.

| lemma | what you see | ending | rule |
| --- | --- | :-: | :-: |
| `bhagavantu`, `āyasmantu` (words in `ntu`) | `bhagavā`, `āyasmā` | ①⨀, ⓪ | {124}, {126} |
| | `bhagavantaṁ`, `āyasmantaṁ`, `mahantaṁ` | ②⨀ | {92} |
| | `bhagavatā`; `bhagavato`, `āyasmato`, `jānato`; `bhagavati` | ③⨀; ④⑥⨀; ⑦⨀ | {127}, {187} |
| | `guṇavanto`, `arahanto`; `guṇavantā`, `jānantā` | ①⨂ | {122}; {92} |
| | `sīlavataṁ`; `guṇavantehi`; `guṇavantesu` | ④⑥⨂; ③⑤⨂; ⑦⨂ | {128}; {92} |
| `arahanta`, `gacchanta`, `mahanta` | `arahaṁ`, `gacchaṁ`, `gacchanto` | ①⨀ | {186} |
| `santa` (being) | `santo`; `sataṁ`; `sabbhi` | ①⨀, ①⨂; ④⑥⨂; ③⨂ | {186}; {185} |
| `bhavanta` (sir) | `bho`, `bhante`; `bhavaṁ`; `bhoto` | ⓪; ①⨀; ④⑥⨀ | {243} |

:::note[`-ā`, `-ato`, `-ānaṁ`]
Here `-ā` is ① singular (`rājā`, `bhagavā`), on `purisa` ① plural or ⑤;
`-ato` is ④⑥ of a `ntu` or `-ant` word, but `-to` on a pronoun is "from"
(`tato`) {248}; `-ānaṁ` is ② singular {188} and ④⑥ plural {89}: the sentence
decides.
:::

### `kamma`, the `mano` group and `go`

The `s` of `manasā` is inserted before a vowel ending {184}.

| lemma | what you see | ending | rule |
| --- | --- | :-: | :-: |
| `kamma` 🚻 (action) | `kammunā`, `kammanā`, `kammena`; `kammani`, `kamme`; the rest as `citta` | ③⨀; ⑦⨀ | {160}; {197}, {108} |
| `mana` (mind), `ceta`, `vaca`, `tapa`, `teja`, `yasa`, `sira`: the `mano` group | `mano`, `ceto`, `tejo`; `mano-` in a compound | ①⨀ | {104}; {183} |
| | `manasā`, `cetasā`, `vacasā`; `manaso`, `cetaso`; `manasi`, `vacasi` | ③⨀; ④⑥⨀; ⑦⨀ | {181}, {184}; {182} |
| `go` (cow) | `go`; `gāvo`; `gāvaṁ`, `gavaṁ`; `gāvena`; `gāvassa`; `gunnaṁ`; `gosu` | ①⨀; ①②⨂; ②⨀; ③⨀; ④⑥⨀; ④⑥⨂; ⑦⨂ | {220}; {74}; {75}; {73}; {81} |

### Examples

> bhikkhū **bhagavato** bhāsitaṁ abhinandunti

🚹⨂①(bhikkhu) 🚹⨀⑥(bhagavant) 🚻⨀②🔽(ta)(bhāsita) 🟢⨂🤟⏮(abhinandati) ⏏️(iti)  
bhikkhū / of the Bhagavā / what was spoken / rejoiced in / (end quote)  
*"Rejoiced in the Bhagavā’s words": `bhagavato`, `-ato`, ④⑥ singular of a word in `ntu` {127}, here ⑥; chapter 5, 10.4.*

> **satthā** no garu

🚹⨀①(satthar) *️⃣👆⚧⨂⑥(ahaṁ) 🚹⨀①(garu)  
teacher / of us / respected  
*"Our teacher is respected": `satthā`, ① singular of `satthu` {199}, the `u` become `ā`; chapter 11, 24.5.*

> mettāsahagatena **cetasā**

🚹⨀③(mettāsahagata) 🚹⨀③(cetas)  
filled with goodwill/friendliness / with intent  
*"With intent": `cetasā`, ③ singular of the `mano` group, `ā` for `nā` with `s` inserted {181}, {184}; chapter 11, 1.11.52.*

### Exercises

Name the lemma, ending, number and rule of each word in bold.

3. aññaṁ **satthāraṁ** uddiseyyāthā”ti

### Answers

*Note: please do the exercises first before checking the answers.*

1. > Pavattite ca pana **bhagavatā** dhammacakke

🚻⨀⑦🔽(ta)(pavattita) ⏏️(ca) ⏏️(pana) 🚹⨀③(bhagavant) 🚻⨀⑦🆎(dhamma+cakka)  
set in motion / and / now / by the Bhagavā / the wheel of the Dhamma  
*"Set in motion by the Bhagavā": `bhagavatā`, `-atā`, ③ singular of `bhagavantu` {127}; chapter 5, 12.1.*

2. > mārena vā **brahmunā** vā kenaci vā

🚹⨀③(māra) ⏏️(vā) 🚹⨀③(brahma) ⏏️(vā) *️⃣🤟🚹⨀③(kaci) ⏏️(vā)  
by Māra / or / by Brahmā / or / by anyone / or  
*"Māra or Brahmā or by anyone": `brahmunā`, ③ singular of `brahma`, `a` become `u` before `nā` {198}; chapter 5, 12.1.*

3. > aññaṁ **satthāraṁ** uddiseyyāthā”ti

🚹⨀②(añña) 🚹⨀②(satthar) ⏯🤘⨂(uddisati)  
another / teacher / appoint  
*"Will you appoint another teacher?": `satthāraṁ`, ② singular of `satthu`, with `-ār-` {200}; chapter 11, 24.10.*

4. > **jānato passato** na cetanāya karaṇīyaṁ

🚻⨀④(jānanta) 🚹⨀④(passanta) 🔼(no) 🚺⨀③(cetana) 🚻⨀①(karaṇīya)  
for knowing / for seeing / no / with intention / ought to be done  
*"One who sees and know reality as it is": `jānato`, `passato`, ④⑥ singular of the participles {187}, {127}; chapter 11, 1.22.*

5. > So kho ahaṁ, bhikkhave, **attanā** jātidhammo samāno

🚹⨀①(ta) 🔼(kho) *️⃣👆⚧⨀①(ahaṁ) 🚹⨂⓪(bhikkhu) 🚹⨀⑤(atta) 🚹⨀①(jātidhamma) 🚹⨀①(samāna)  
that / indeed / I / Bhikkhave / from self / liable to birth / being  
*"From my own doing I was liable to birth": `attanā`, ⑤ singular of `atta`, `nā` for `smā` {214}, also ③; chapter 11, 18.1.*

6. > Yāvajīvaṁ **arahanto** abrahmacariyaṁ pahāya

🚻⨀①(yāvajīva) 🚹⨂①(arahant) 🚻⨀①(abrahmacariya) 🔼(pahāya)  
life-long / liberated ones / incelibacy / giving up  
*"Life long arahants (liberated ones) give up incelibacy": `arahanto`, ① plural of `arahanta` {122}, singular `arahaṁ` {186}; chapter 11, 21.1.*
