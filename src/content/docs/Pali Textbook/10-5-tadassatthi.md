`bhagavā`, "the Blessed One", the word you meet on every page of the texts, is
literally "one who has good fortune": `bhaga` ("good fortune") with the affix
`vantu` {368}. A whole family of words is built this way: a noun names a
thing, the affix adds "having that" (`tadassatthi`, "he has that"), and the
new word names whoever has it. `sīlavā` is one who has `sīla` (ethical
conduct), virtuous; `satimā` one who has `sati` (mindfulness), mindful;
`āyasmā` one who has `āyu` (life), venerable. Kaccāyana explains each by the
phrase `X yassa atthi` ("of whom there is X") and lists nine affixes
{364}–{371}. When you meet such a word, ask what noun is left when the `-vā`,
`-mā` or `-ī` is taken away, and who has it.

One more affix means "made of that" (`tappakati`): `sovaṇṇamayaṁ` is "made of
gold" {372}.

### How to recognise a word meaning "having …"

| affix | what you see | example | meaning | rule |
| :-: | --- | --- | --- | :-: |
| `vantu` | `-vā` on a word in `a` or `ā` | `guṇavā`, `bhagavā` | has `guṇa` (virtue), `bhaga` (good fortune): virtuous, the Blessed One | {368} |
| `mantu` | `-mā` on a word in `i` or `u`; `āyu` becomes `āyas-` before it | `satimā`, `āyasmā` | has `sati` (mindfulness), `āyu` (life): mindful, venerable | {369}, {371} |
| `ī`, `ika` | `-ī` or `-iko` in place of the final vowel | `daṇḍī`, `daṇḍiko` | has a `daṇḍa` (staff) | {366} |
| `vī` | `-vī` on a word in `ā`; the rule's `ca` adds `sa`, `-so` | `medhāvī`, `sumedhaso` | has `medhā` (wisdom): wise | {364} |
| `sī` | `-ssī`, the `s` doubled | `tapassī` | has `tapa` (austerity): an ascetic | {365} |
| `ra` | `-ro` | `madhuro` | has `madhu` (honey): sweet | {367} |
| `ṇa` | nothing you can see: the noun with `-o`; `ddh`, `ññ` keep the first vowel short ([10.1](../10-1-apacca/)) | `saddho`, `pañño` | has `saddhā` (faith), `paññā` (wisdom): faithful, wise | {370} |
| `maya` | `-maya` after the name of a material | `sovaṇṇamayaṁ` | made of `suvaṇṇa` (gold) | {372} |

The vutti gives every word in the masculine ①; the word takes the gender of
the noun it describes. Every rule number links to the full rule;
[Appendix H](../h-taddhita/) tables them all.

### The shapes of a `-vantu` or `-mantu` word

Only the ① shows `-vā` or `-mā`. Before the other endings you see `-vant-` or
`-vat-`, `-mant-` or `-mat-`, and the dictionary lists the word under the
lemma `bhagavant`, `āyasmant`, as the symbol lines of chapters 3, 5 and 11 do:

| ending | what you see | example | rule |
| :-: | --- | --- | :-: |
| ① | `-vā`, `-mā` | `bhagavā`, `āyasmā`, `satimā` (chapters 3, 5 and 11) | {124} |
| ② | `-vantaṁ`, `-mantaṁ` | `bhagavantaṁ`, `āyasmantaṁ` (chapter 11) | |
| ③ | `-vatā`, `-matā` | `bhagavatā` (chapter 11) | {127} |
| ④ ⑥ | `-vato`, `-mato` | `sīlavato` (chapter 11) | {127} |
| ① ⨂ | `-vanto`, `-manto` | `guṇavanto` | {122} |

Rule {127} is optional: `guṇavantassa`, `guṇavantena`, `guṇavantasmiṁ` occur
beside `guṇavato`, `guṇavatā`, `guṇavati`.

### Examples

> guṇo yassa atthi → **guṇavā**

🚹⨀①(guṇo) *️⃣🤟🚹⨀⑥(yassa) 🟢⨀🤟▶️(atthi) → 🚹⨀①🔽(vantu)(guṇavā)  
virtue / of whom / there is → one who has virtue, virtuous  
*`vantu` {368}: `guṇa` + `-vā`; the lemma is `guṇavant`, and the ① ends in `-vā` {124}.*

> mati yassa atthi → **matimā**

🚺⨀①(mati) *️⃣🤟🚹⨀⑥(yassa) 🟢⨀🤟▶️(atthi) → 🚹⨀①🔽(mantu)(matimā)  
intelligence / of whom / there is → one who has intelligence, wise  
*`mantu` {369}: `mati` + `-mā`; the `mantu` words of the rule all end in `i` or `u`.*

> daṇḍo yassa atthi → **daṇḍī**

🚹⨀①(daṇḍo) *️⃣🤟🚹⨀⑥(yassa) 🟢⨀🤟▶️(atthi) → 🚹⨀①🔽(ī)(daṇḍī)  
a staff / of whom / there is → one who has a staff  
*`ī` {366}: the final `a` of `daṇḍa` gives way to `-ī`; `daṇḍiko`, with `ika`, means the same.*

> suvaṇṇena pakataṁ kammaṁ → **sovaṇṇamayaṁ**

🚻⨀③(suvaṇṇena) 🚻⨀①🔽(ta)(pakataṁ) 🚻⨀①(kammaṁ) → 🚻⨀①🔽(maya)(sovaṇṇamayaṁ)  
with gold / made / a work → made of gold  
*`maya` {372}: `-maya` after the material; `suvaṇṇa` has the form `sovaṇṇa` here {391}.*

### Answers

*Note: please do the exercises first before checking the answers.*

1. > bhagaṁ yassa atthi → **bhagavā**

🚻⨀①(bhagaṁ) *️⃣🤟🚹⨀⑥(yassa) 🟢⨀🤟▶️(atthi) → 🚹⨀①🔽(vantu)(bhagavā)  
good fortune / of whom / there is → one who has good fortune, the Blessed One  
*`vantu` {368}: `bhaga` + `-vā`; chapters 3, 5 and 11 write the lemma `bhagavant`.*

2. > āyu assa atthi → **āyasmā**

🚻⨀①(āyu) *️⃣🤟🚹⨀⑥(assa) 🟢⨀🤟▶️(atthi) → 🚹⨀①🔽(mantu)(āyasmā)  
life / his / there is → one who has life, venerable  
*`mantu` {369}: `āyu` + `-mā`, the `u` of `āyu` becoming `asa` before the affix {371}.*

3. > sati yassa atthi → **satimā**

🚺⨀①(sati) *️⃣🤟🚹⨀⑥(yassa) 🟢⨀🤟▶️(atthi) → 🚹⨀①🔽(mantu)(satimā)  
mindfulness / of whom / there is → one who has mindfulness, mindful  
*`mantu` {369}: `sati` + `-mā`; chapter 11 has it in `upekkhako satimā sukhavihārī`.*

4. > sīlaṁ yassa atthi → **sīlavā**

🚻⨀①(sīlaṁ) *️⃣🤟🚹⨀⑥(yassa) 🟢⨀🤟▶️(atthi) → 🚹⨀①🔽(vantu)(sīlavā)  
ethical conduct / of whom / there is → one who has ethical conduct, virtuous  
*`vantu` {368}: `sīla` + `-vā`; chapter 11 has its ④, `sīlavato`, "for whoever is moral" {127}.*

5. > paññā yassa atthi → **paññavā**

🚺⨀①(paññā) *️⃣🤟🚹⨀⑥(yassa) 🟢⨀🤟▶️(atthi) → 🚹⨀①🔽(vantu)(paññavā)  
wisdom / of whom / there is → one who has wisdom, wise  
*`vantu` {368}: `paññā` + `-vā`, the final `ā` shortened before the affix {403}.*

6. > medhā yassa atthi → **medhāvī**

🚺⨀①(medhā) *️⃣🤟🚹⨀⑥(yassa) 🟢⨀🤟▶️(atthi) → 🚹⨀①🔽(vī)(medhāvī)  
wisdom / of whom / there is → one who has wisdom, wise  
*`vī` {364}: `medhā` + `-vī`; so too `māyāvī` ("deceitful") from `māyā`.*

7. > mālā yassa atthi → **mālī**

🚺⨀①(mālā) *️⃣🤟🚹⨀⑥(yassa) 🟢⨀🤟▶️(atthi) → 🚹⨀①🔽(ī)(mālī)  
a garland / of whom / there is → one who has a garland, garlanded  
*`ī` {366}: the final `ā` of `mālā` gives way to `-ī`; `māliko`, with `ika`, means the same.*

8. > dārunā pakataṁ kammaṁ → **dārumayaṁ**

🚻⨀③(dārunā) 🚻⨀①🔽(ta)(pakataṁ) 🚻⨀①(kammaṁ) → 🚻⨀①🔽(maya)(dārumayaṁ)  
with wood / made / a work → made of wood, wooden  
*`maya` {372}: `dāru` + `-maya`; neuter (🚻), agreeing with `kammaṁ`, like all the vutti's `maya` words.*
