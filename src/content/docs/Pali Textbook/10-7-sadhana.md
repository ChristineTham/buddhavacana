Many of the commonest nouns in the texts are built on the root (`dhātu`) of a
verb: `satthā` ("the teacher") is the root `sāsa` (instruct) with an affix
(`paccaya`) meaning "the one who does it", `sāsanaṁ` ("the teaching") the
same root with an affix meaning "the act". Kaccāyana calls such affixes
`kita`, and the long tail of them that each form a word or two `uṇādi`
("`u` and the rest"). When you meet a word of this kind, ask which verb is
inside it and which of four senses its affix gives: the doer, the act, what
is to be done, the purpose.

### How to recognise the sense

| what you see | what it means | affix | example | rule |
| --- | --- | :-: | --- | :-: |
| `-aka`, first vowel lengthened | the doer | `ṇvu` | `kārako` "a doer", `dāyako` "a giver" | {624}, {527}, {622} |
| `-tā` in ① (the affix is `-tu`) | the doer | `tu` | `kattā` "a doer", `satthā` "the teacher" | {624}, {527}, {566} |
| `-ī`, first vowel lengthened | one whose habit, duty or future it is to do it | `ṇī` | `brahmacārī` "living the holy life", `kārī` "bound to do", `gāmī` "about to go" | {532}, {636}, {651} |
| `-ana`, `-aṇa`, neuter | the act, its means or its place | `yu` | `ñāṇaṁ` "knowing", `ṭhānaṁ` "a place" | {547}, {548}, {549}, {622}, {641} |
| `-anā`, `-ti`, feminine | the act | `yu`, `ti` | `vedanā` "feeling", `jāti` "birth" | {553}, {585} |
| `-tabba`, `-anīya` | to be done; must, should, may be done | `tabba`, `anīya` | `kattabbaṁ`, `karaṇīyaṁ` "to be done" | {540}, {625}, {635} |
| `-iya`, `-eyya`, `-ya`, first vowel lengthened | the same | `ṇya` | `kāriyaṁ` "to be done", `neyyaṁ` "to be led" | {541}, {636} |
| `-tuṁ`, no ending after it | to do: the purpose, beside "wish", "fit", "able" | `tuṁ` | `sotuṁ` "to hear", `vattuṁ` "to speak" | {561}, {562}, {637} |
| `-ta` | done, or the thing done; the doer, when the thing done keeps ② | `tta` | `pacito` "cooked"; `bhāsitaṁ` "what was spoken" ([9: 10.2](../9-fire/#u10-2)); `dānaṁ dinno devadatto` "Devadatta has given a gift" | {625}, {626} |

A word in `-tabba`, `-anīya` or `-ya` agrees with the thing to be done, and
the one who must do it stands in ③: `bhottabbo odano bhavatā`, "the rice is
to be eaten by you"; in the neuter it names the act itself, `sayitabbaṁ
bhavatā`, "lying down is to be done by you", as does `sussayo` ("easy to lie
on") with the affix `kha` {625}. Every such word takes a noun's endings
{601}; a word in `-tuṁ` takes none {221}. [Appendix K](../k-unadi/) tables
every `uṇādi` rule, [Appendix J](../j-kita/) every `kita` rule.

### The other senses Kaccāyana lists

| what you see | what it means | example | rule |
| --- | --- | --- | :-: |
| a bare root after a word, its last consonant gone | the doer: the affix `kvi` leaves no trace | `sayambhū` "self-become", `bhujago` "a snake" | {639}, {615} |
| `k`, `g` for the root's `c`, `j`, vowel lengthened | the act, masculine in `-o` | `pāko` "cooking", `rāgo` "passion" | {640} |
| `-ana`, `-aka` after `nuda`, `su`, `ñā`, `samu` and the like | the doer, the act or the means | `samaṇo` "an ascetic", `sāvako` "a hearer" | {641} |
| a lengthened pronoun before `-disa`, `-risa`, `-dikkha`, `-dī` | "such as …" | `īdiso` "such as this", `kīdiso` "of what kind?" | {642} |
| `-u`, `-ana`, `-ta` | the doer or the act, now or before | `kāru` "a craftsman", `bhūtaṁ` "what has come to be" | {650} |
| `-ī`, `-i` after `gamu`, `bhaja`, `su`, `ṭhā` | the one who will do it | `gāmī` "about to go", `passāvi` "about to flow forth" | {651} |
| `-aka`, `-tā`, or `-ssaṁ`, `-nto`, `-māno`, `-āno` on the verb's present shape (`karonto`, `kurumāno`, `bhuñjanto`), beside a verb of going | going in order to do it | `kārako vajati` "the doer goes [to do it]", `kammaṁ karonto vajati` "he goes to do the work" | {652}, {655}, {451}, {511}, {512}, {446} |
| the act in ④, or the thing done as first member, beside a verb of going | the purpose of the going | `pākāya vajati` "he goes to cook", `nagarakāro vajati` "he goes to build the city" | {653}, {654} |

### The changes you will see in the root

| what you see | what happened | rule |
| --- | --- | :-: |
| first vowel lengthened before `-aka`, `-ī`, `-iya`, `-a` (`kārako`, `kārī`, `pāko`); a bare vowel for `kha`, `ghiṇ` (`sussayo`, `passāvi`) | the marker letters `ṇ`, `kh`, `gh` are not sounds: `ṇ` lengthens the vowel, then all three drop | {621}, {483}, {523}, {517} |
| `e`, `o` for `i`, `u` (`bhottabbo`, `sotuṁ`); `ay`, `av`, `āy`, `āv` before a vowel (`bhavituṁ`, `sāvako`) | the vowel strengthened before the affix, split before a vowel | {485}, {513}, {514}, {515} |
| `kat-` for `kara` (`kattā`); `gan-`, `han-` for `gamu`, `hana` (`gantuṁ`); `bhutt-`, `bhott-` for `bhuja` (`bhutto`); `dinn-` for `dā` (`dinno`) | the root reshaped before `-ta`, `-tu`, `-tabba`, `-tuṁ` | {619}, {620}, {596}, {578}, {582} |
| an `i` between root and affix (`pacito`, `sayitabbaṁ`, `karissaṁ`) | the insertion of `i` | {605}, {516} |
| `-āy-` ending a root in `ā` (`dāyako` "a giver", `bhogadāyo`) | `āya` for `ā` before a `ṇ` affix | {593} |
| `kārīsi`, `passāvi`, `paṭṭhāyi`, `saṅgo`, `ajjhayitabbaṁ` | the usual joins of sandhi (`kārī asi`, `pa sāvi`, `pa ṭhāyi`, `saṁ ga`, `adhi aya`); a root's last vowel drops before the affix | {13}, {28}, {29}, {31}, {45}, {83} |
| `kattā`, `kāru`, `kārī`, `ñāṇaṁ`, `pākāya`, `karonto` | the new word's endings: `-tu` as `-tā` in ①; `-u`, `-ī` with none; neuter `-aṁ`; ④ `-āya`; `-nto` from `ntu` | {199}, {220}, {219}, {109}, {94} |

### Examples

> karoti → **kārako**

🟢⨀🤟▶️(karoti) → 🚹⨀①🔽(ṇvu)(kārako)  
he does → a doer  
*`ṇvu` {624}: `-aka` on `√kara` (do), first vowel lengthened: the one who does.*

> karoti → **kattā**

🟢⨀🤟▶️(karoti) → 🚹⨀①🔽(tu)(kattā)  
he does → a doer  
*`tu` {624}: `-tu` on `√kara`, shown as `kat-` before it {619}; ① ends in `-tā` {199}.*

> **ñāṇaṁ**

🚻⨀①🔽(yu)(ñāṇaṁ)  
knowing, knowledge  
*`yu` {641}: `-ana` on `√ñā` (know), neuter: the act of knowing, `ñāṇaṁ hoti` ([7: 11.3](../7-anatta/#u11-3)).*

> vedayati → **vedanā**

🟢⨀🤟▶️(vedayati) → 🚺⨀①🔽(yu)(vedanā)  
one feels → feeling  
*`yu` {553}: `-anā`, the feminine of `-ana`, on `√vida` (feel): the act of feeling; `vedanā anattā` ([7: 3.1](../7-anatta/#u3-1)).*

> **bhottabbo** odano bhavatā

🚹⨀①🔽(tabba)(bhottabbo) 🚹⨀①(odano) 🚹⨀③(bhavatā)  
is to be eaten / the rice / by you  
*`tabba` {625}: "to be eaten"; it agrees with `odano`, and the one who must eat stands in ③.*

> saddhammaṁ **sotuṁ** icchati

🚹⨀②(saddhamma) 🔽(tuṁ)(suṇāti) 🟢⨀🤟▶️(icchati)  
the true dhamma / to hear / he wishes  
*`tuṁ` {561}: `-tuṁ` on `√su` (hear), the `u` as `o` {485}: to hear, after `icchati` "he wishes".*

### Exercises

Each of the following you met in the texts, in the form it has there, in the
phrase given; the link leads to its sentence. Say which affix it carries,
which root it is built on and which of the four senses it has; look the word
up in a dictionary and check the root against the changes above.

You may find the [Digital Pali Dictionary](https://digitalpalidictionary.github.io/)

:::

2. sāvako (the last member of `ariyasāvako`, [11: 33, 21.17](../11-pbt/#s33-u21-17), [7: 11.1](../7-anatta/#u11-1), [9: 8.1](../9-fire/#u8-1))
3. sāsanaṁ ([11: 25, 4](../11-pbt/#s25-u4): `etaṁ buddhāna sāsanaṁ`)

### Answers

*Note: please do the exercises first before checking the answers.*

1. > **satthā**

🚹⨀①🔽(tu)(satthā)  
teacher  
*`tu` {566}: `-tu` on `√sāsa` (instruct), the root shaped `satth-` before it: the one who instructs.*

2. > **sāvako**

🚹⨀①🔽(ṇvu)(sāvako)  
a hearer, a disciple  
*`ṇvu` {641}: `-aka` on `√su` (hear), the `u` as `āva` {515}: the one who hears.*

3. > **sāsanaṁ**

🚻⨀①🔽(yu)(sāsanaṁ)  
teaching  
*`yu` {547}: `-ana` on `√sāsa` (instruct), neuter: the act of instructing, what `satthā` does.*

4. > kataṁ **karaṇīyaṁ**

🚻⨀①🔽(ta)(kata) 🚻⨀①🔽(anīya)(karaṇīya)  
done / what had to be done  
*`anīya` {540}: `-aṇīya` on `√kara` (do), its `n` as `ṇ` after `r` {549}: what is to be done.*

5. > **jāti**

🚺⨀①🔽(ti)(jāti)  
birth  
*`ti` {553}: `-ti` on `√jana` (be born), its end `ā` before it {585}: the act of being born.*
