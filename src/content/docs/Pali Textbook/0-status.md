This is a textbook meant for those who wish to learn Pali as quickly as possible, without prior knowledge of linguistics or grammar terminology. It is based on **Kaccāyana Pali Vyākaraṇam**, the oldest extant Pali grammar book, and aims to provide a comprehensive understanding of Pali grammar and syntax.

## Contents

The chapters alternate between grammar and texts: each grammar chapter is
followed by a discourse, read word by word, that uses what the chapter taught.
The grammar chapters together cover all eight chapters of Kaccāyana; the texts
run from the Buddha's awakening through the three discourses that made the
first arahants to a selection of the core teachings.

## How to read it

Chapter 1 is the whole language in one chapter, and it is enough to start
reading the texts with; the rest of the book is optional, and deepens what
chapter 1 introduces. After it, take the chapters in order, a grammar chapter
and the text after it, or go straight to a text and come back to the grammar
chapters as questions arise. Each grammar chapter opens with a reading path
naming the sections a first pass needs; the rest of the chapter is for looking
a form up when it comes. Every rule number in braces, `{104}`, links to Kaccāyana's own
rule on [kaccayana.github.io](https://kaccayana.github.io), for the reader who
wants to know why a form is what it is.

The texts are read with a dictionary open: the
[Digital Pāḷi Dictionary](https://dpdict.net/) gives every form's lemma and
analysis, and the exercises are built on it.
