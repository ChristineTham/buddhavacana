A noun that something is given to, done for or said to is the `sampadāna`
(the recipient), and it takes the ④ ending {293}: `samaṇassa cīvaraṁ dadāti`,
"[he] gives a robe to the ascetic". Kaccāyana defines the recipient as the one
someone wishes to give to, the one something is pleasing to and the one
something is owed to {276}, then lists some twenty other words that go with it
{277}, and adds `namo` (homage) and its like {294}. The question to ask of a
word with this ending is "to whom, or for what?".

What you see is most often `-ssa`, `-āya` or `-no` in the singular
(`samaṇassa`, `atthāya`, `bhikkhuno`), `-naṁ` in the plural (`bhikkhūnaṁ`),
and `te`, `me`, `vo`, `no` ("to you", "to me", "to you all", "to us")
{147}–{148}. Only `-āya` on a masculine or neuter word (`atthāya`, `hitāya`,
and `ābādhāya`, "to affliction", which [chapter 7](../7-anatta/) repeats for
each of the five collections, [7: 2.3](../7-anatta/#u2-3)) is ④ alone {109};
the other forms also mean "of", the ⑥ of section 4.7, so the sentence decides:
is there a giving, a saying, a purpose, a wish? The texts sometimes use the ⑦
ending in its place {311}; [Appendix G](../g-vibhatti/) tables the endings.

### The uses of the ④ ending

| what the noun is doing | what you see | example | rule |
| --- | --- | --- | :-: |
| the one given to | a verb of giving: `dadāti`, `deti` | `samaṇassa cīvaraṁ dadāti` | {276}, {293} |
| the one something is pleasing to | `rocati` | `samaṇassa rocate saccaṁ` | {276} |
| the one something is owed to | `dhāreti` | `suvaṇṇaṁ te dhārayate` | {276}, {277} |
| the one praised | `silāghati` | `buddhassa silāghate` | {277} |
| the one something is denied to | the root `hanu` | `hanute tuyha'meva` | {277} |
| the one attended on | `upatiṭṭhati` | `upatiṭṭheyya sakyaputtānaṁ` | {277} |
| the one an oath is sworn to | `sapati` | `tuyhaṁ sapate` | {277} |
| the one longed for or envied | `pihayati` | `samiddhānaṁ pihayanti daliddā` | {277} |
| the one someone is angry with, hostile to or jealous of | `kujjhati`, `issati`, `usūyati` | `kodhayati devadattassa` | {277} |
| the one whose favour is won or who is wronged (② too) | `ārādho`, `aparajjhati` | `ārādho'haṁ rañño` | {277} |
| the speaker assented to | `paṭissuṇāti`, `anugiṇāti`, `patigiṇāti` | `bhagavato paccassosuṁ` ([7: 1.3](../7-anatta/#u1-3)) | {277} |
| the one announced to | `āroceti`, `āmanteti`, `paṭivedeti` | `ārocayāmi vo bhikkhave` | {277} |
| the purpose, "for the sake of" (`tadattha`) | `atthāya` or a noun of purpose | `buddhassa atthāya` | {277} |
| the aim, "in order to" (`tumattha`) | a noun of action in `-āya` | `bhikkhūnaṁ phāsuvihārāya` | {277} |
| the one something is enough or fit for | `alaṁ`, `arahati` | `alaṁ me buddho` | {277} |
| a lifeless thing someone is likened to in contempt | `maññati` | `kaṭṭhassa tuvaṁ maññe` | {277} |
| the place gone to (② too) | a verb of going | `appo saggāya gacchati` | {277} |
| the one wished well | `hotu` with `bhaddaṁ`, `sukhaṁ` | `āyasmato dīghāyuko hotu` | {277} |
| the one a consent concerns | `sammuti` | `sādhu sammuti me` | {277} |
| the measure exceeded | `bhiyyoso` | `bhiyyoso mattāya` | {277} |
| the one something appears or is made plain to (a ⑦ meaning) | `pātubhavati`, `āvi karoti` | `tassa me sakko pāturahosi` | {277} |
| the one something is taught to, needed by, or done for (`attha`, and many more) | `deseti`, `attho`, `hoti` | `dhammaṁ vo desessāmi`, `tassa phāsuvihārāya hoti` | {277} |
| the one ruled over, and any other word with a recipient; ④ at times for ②–⑦ | `pabhū` | `desassa pabhū` | {277} |
| the one homage, well-being or welcome is wished to | `namo`, `sotthi`, `svāgataṁ` | `namo te buddha` | {294} |
| the recipient with a ⑦ ending instead | `-e`, `-esu` meaning "to" | `saṅghe dinnaṁ mahapphalaṁ` | {311} |

### Examples

The one given to {276}, {293}:

> **samaṇassa** cīvaraṁ dadāti

🚹⨀④(samaṇassa) 🚻⨀②(cīvaraṁ) 🟢⨀🤟▶️(dadāti)  
to the ascetic / a robe / [he] gives  
*[He] gives a robe to the ascetic.*

The purpose, "for the sake of" {277}:

> ūnassa **pāripūriyā** taṁ cīvaraṁ nikkhipitabbaṁ

🚻⨀⑥(ūnassa) 🚺⨀④(pāripūriyā) 🚻⨀①(*️⃣🤟(taṁ) cīvaraṁ 🔽(tabba)(nikkhipitabbaṁ))  
of what is lacking / for the completion / that / robe / is to be laid aside  
*That robe is to be laid aside for the completion of what is lacking.*

The one honoured, with `namo` {294}:

> namo **te** buddha vīr’atthu

⏏️(namo) *️⃣🤘⚧⨀④(te) 🚹⨀⓪(buddha) 🚹⨀⓪(vīra) 🟢⨀🤟⏹(atthu)  
homage / to you / O Buddha / O hero / let there be  
*Homage be to you, O Buddha, O hero.*

### Exercises

Translate the following from Pāḷi to English and say, for each word with the ④
ending, which use of the table it shows. Look up the words in a dictionary, do
the necessary sandhi and compound word deconstruction, and let the endings
guide the meaning. Sentences 1–5 and 7 are from the texts of chapters 3, 7, 9 and
11, linked to their place there; 6 is Kaccāyana's own.

:::tip[Hint]
You may find the [Digital Pali Dictionary](https://digitalpalidictionary.github.io/)
useful for looking up words, as well as doing the sandhi and compound word
deconstruction.
:::

1. “Bhadante”ti te bhikkhū bhagavato paccassosuṁ. ([7: 1.3](../7-anatta/#u1-3))
2. Dhammatā esā, bhikkhave, yaṁ pamuditassa pīti uppajjati. ([11: 18, 1.9](../11-pbt/#s18-u1-9))
3. Sīlavato, bhikkhave, sīlasampannassa na cetanāya karaṇīyaṁ. ([11: 18, 1.1](../11-pbt/#s18-u1-1))
4. uppannānaṁ pāpakānaṁ akusalānaṁ dhammānaṁ pahānāya chandaṁ janeti ([11: 35, 21.21](../11-pbt/#s35-u21-21))
5. Yadā have pātubhavanti dhammā, ātāpino jhāyato brāhmaṇassa ([3: 6.1–6.2](../3-awakening/#u6-1))
6. saṅghe gotami dehi
7. ‘Khīṇā jāti, vusitaṁ brahmacariyaṁ, kataṁ karaṇīyaṁ, nāparaṁ itthattāyā’ti pajānātī”ti. ([9: 9.2](../9-fire/#u9-2))
