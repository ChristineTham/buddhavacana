A verb that says what is still to come carries `ssa` before the ending that
gives its person and number: `bhavissati`, `gacchissanti`. Kaccāyana names
these endings `bhavissantī` (the rule spells it `bhavissantī`), the future, ⏭:
one rule says when they are used, in time to come {421}, and one lists the
twelve {429}. The same `ssa` with a past-looking tail and an `a-` before the
root is the `kālātipatti` ("the lapse of time"), 🔀, an action that never came
about: "if he had got the vehicle, he would have gone" {422}, {430}.

So ask what follows the `ssa`: `-ti`, `-nti`, `-si`, `-tha`, `-mi`, `-ma` make
the future, `-ā` and `-aṁsu` with the `a-` in front the conditional; person
and number as in [8.1](../8-1-reading-a-verb/), the rules in
[Appendix I](../i-verbs/). Kaccāyana gives the name `sabbadhātuka` to the
present, the command, the wish and the `hiyyattanī` {431}; the other four
sets, these two among them, take an `i` between the root and the ending {516},
so what you see is nearly always `-issa-`: `gam-i-ssati`, `kar-i-ssati`. That
`i` is the surest sign; each rule's `attanopada` six, rare in the texts, are in
[chapter 1](../1-basics/#verb-terminations-423-430).

### The future: `bhavissantī` ⏭

| what you see | person | number | example | rule |
| --- | :-: | :-: | --- | :-: |
| `-ssati` | third 🤟 | ⨀ | `bhavissati`, `gacchissati`, `karissati` | {429} |
| `-ssanti` | third 🤟 | ⨂ | `gacchissanti`, `karissanti`, `bhavissantī` | {429} |
| `-ssasi`, `-ssatha` | second 🤘 | ⨀, ⨂ | none in the rules or the texts | {429} |
| `-ssāmi` | first 👆 | ⨀ | `āharissāmi`, `karissāmi` | {429} |
| `-ssāma` | first 👆 | ⨂ | `karissāma`, `gamissāma` | {429} |

### Shapes before the ending

| what you see | what it is | example | rule |
| --- | --- | --- | :-: |
| `-issa-` | the `i` before the ending; a root ending in a vowel does without it | `gamissati`, `labhissati`, `pacissati` | {516} |
| `bhavissa-` | `bhū` for `asa`, the root of `atthi` | `bhavissati`, `bhavissantī` | {507} |
| `hessa-`, `hehi-`, `hohi-` | the future of `hoti`, its `ssa` often dropped | `hessati`, `hohiti` | {480} |
| `kāha-` | `karoti`'s future: `kāha` for `kara` and the `ssa` | `kāhati`, `kāhasi` ("you will do"), `kāhāmi` | {481} |

### The conditional: `kālātipatti` 🔀

| what you see | person | number | example | rule |
| --- | :-: | :-: | --- | :-: |
| `a-` … `-ssā`, `-ssa` | third 🤟 | ⨀ | `agacchissā` ("he would have gone"), `alabhissā`; the texts' `abhavissa` ([7: 2.2](../7-anatta/#u2-2)) | {430}, {519} |
| `a-` … `-ssaṁsu` | third 🤟 | ⨂ | `agacchissaṁsu`, `alabhissaṁsu`; the texts' `abhavissaṁsu` ([7: 4.5](../7-anatta/#u4-5)) | {430}, {519} |
| `-sse`, `-ssatha`, `-ssaṁ`, `-ssāmhā` | second 🤘, first 👆 | ⨀, ⨂ | none in the rules or the texts | {430} |

The `a-` is the past's, from [8.4](../8-4-past/), and its rule names the
`kālātipatti` too {519}. In the texts the final vowel is short, `abhavissa`,
and the sentence carries `ce` ("if") or `no ce` ("if not"), or, as in
[chapter 7](../7-anatta/), no "if" at all; its other half is a conditional or
a `sattamī` (`paññāyetha`, "would be evident", `saṁvatteyya`, "would lead").

:::note[Look-alikes]
`-ssatha` belongs to both sets: the `a-` before the root and a `ce` mark the
conditional. A noun's `-assa` (④ or ⑥, `jātassa`) has no `i` before it and
sits on a lemma; `passati` ("sees") is a present: no `i`.
:::

### Examples

> Āraddhaṁ kho pana me vīriyaṁ **bhavissati** asallīnaṁ

🚻⨀①🔽(ta)(āraddha) ⏏️(kho) ⏏️(pana) *️⃣👆⚧⨀⑥(ahaṁ) 🚻⨀①(vīriya) 🟢⨀🤟⏭(bhavati) 🚻⨀①🔽(ta)🆎(na+sallīna)  
applied / indeed / now / my / determination / will become / active  
*`bhavissati`, "will become": `-ssati`, the future, third person singular {429}, with the `i` before `ssa` {516}; [Bonus: 45, 9.7](../11-pbt/#s45-u1061-7).*

> te **gacchissanti**, **karissanti**

*️⃣🤟🚹⨂①(ta) 🟢⨂🤟⏭(gacchati) 🟢⨂🤟⏭(karoti)  
they / will go / will do  
*`-ssanti`, the future, third person plural {429}: `gacchanti` with `-issa-` before its `-nti` {516}; Kaccāyana's own {421}.*

> No cetaṁ, bhikkhave, **abhavissa** ajātaṁ abhūtaṁ akataṁ asaṅkhataṁ

⏏️(no) ⏏️(ce) *️⃣🤟🚻⨀①(eta) 🚹⨂⓪(bhikkhu) 🟢⨀🤟🔀(bhavati) 🚻⨀①🔽(ta)🆎(na+jāta) 🚻⨀①🔽(ta)🆎(na+bhūta) 🚻⨀①🔽(ta)🆎(na+kata) 🚻⨀①🔽(ta)🆎(na+saṅkhata)  
surely? / if / this / Bhikkhave / could have been / not born / not become / not made / not formed  
*`abhavissa`, "could have been": the conditional, third person singular, `a-` {519}, `-ssa` for `-ssā` {430}, after `no ce` ("if not"); [Bonus: 51, 3.2](../11-pbt/#s51-u671-2).*

> Saṅkhārā ca hidaṁ, bhikkhave, attā **abhavissaṁsu**

🚹⨂①(saṅkhāra) ⏏️(ca) ⏏️(hi) *️⃣🤟🚻⨀①(ima) 🚹⨂⓪(bhikkhu) 🚹⨀①(atta) 🟢⨂🤟🔀(bhavati)  
mental constructions / and / indeed / this / Bhikkhave / self / would be  
*`abhavissaṁsu`, "would be": `-ssaṁsu`, the conditional, third person plural {430}, with the `a-` {519} and no "if" spoken; [7: 4.5](../7-anatta/#u4-5).*

### Exercises

The first is from [chapter 7](../7-anatta/); the rest are Kaccāyana's own,
since the texts hold no future form but `bhavissati` and no conditional but
`abhavissa` and its plural. Name the verb's set, person and number, and what
stands before its ending; then look it up.

:::tip[Hint]
You may find the [Digital Pali Dictionary](https://digitalpalidictionary.github.io/)
useful for looking up words, as well as doing the sandhi and compound word
deconstruction.
:::

1. Rūpañca hidaṁ, bhikkhave, attā abhavissa
2. kilanto addhānaṁ gamissati
3. so ce taṁ yānaṁ alabhissā, agacchissā
4. pabbajjā vā hohiti, maraṇaṁ vā me
5. kāhāmi kusalaṁ bahuṁ

### Answers

*Note: please do the exercises first before checking the answers.*

1. > Rūpañca hidaṁ, bhikkhave, attā **abhavissa**

🚻⨀①(rūpa) ⏏️(ca) ⏏️(hi) *️⃣🤟🚻⨀①(ima) 🚹⨂⓪(bhikkhu) 🚹⨀①(atta) 🟢⨀🤟🔀(bhavati)  
form / and / indeed / this / Bhikkhave / self / would be  
*`abhavissa`, "would be": the conditional, third person singular, `a-` {519}, `-ssa` for `-ssā` {430}, with no "if" spoken; [7: 2.2](../7-anatta/#u2-2).*

2. > kilanto addhānaṁ **gamissati**

🚹⨀①🔽(ta)(kilanta) 🚻⨀②(addhāna) 🟢⨀🤟⏭(gacchati)  
tired / the road / he will travel  
*`gamissati`, "he will travel": `-ssati`, the future, third person singular {429}, on the root `gam` with the `i` before `ssa` {516}.*

3. > so ce taṁ yānaṁ **alabhissā**, **agacchissā**

*️⃣🤟🚹⨀①(ta) ⏏️(ce) *️⃣🤟🚻⨀②(ta) 🚻⨀②(yāna) 🟢⨀🤟🔀(labhati) 🟢⨀🤟🔀(gacchati)  
he / if / that / vehicle / had got / he would have gone  
*`-ssā`, the conditional, third person singular {430}, with the `a-` {519}; `ce` ("if") marks the failed condition {422}.*

4. > pabbajjā vā **hohiti**, maraṇaṁ vā me

🚺⨀①(pabbajjā) ⏏️(vā) 🟢⨀🤟⏭(hoti) 🚻⨀①(maraṇa) ⏏️(vā) *️⃣👆⚧⨀⑥(ahaṁ)  
going forth / either / will be / death / or / mine  
*`hohiti`, "will be": the future of `hoti`, third person singular, its `ssa` dropped {480}.*

5. > **kāhāmi** kusalaṁ bahuṁ

🟢⨀👆⏭(karoti) 🚻⨀②(kusala) 🚻⨀②(bahu)  
I will do / good / much  
*`kāhāmi`, "I will do": the future, first person singular; `kāha` for `kara` and its `ssa` {481}.*
