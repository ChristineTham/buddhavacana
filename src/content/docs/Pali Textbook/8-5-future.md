A verb that says what is still to come carries `ssa` before the ending that
gives its person and number: `bhavissati`, `gacchissanti`. Kaccāyana names
these endings `bhavissanti` (the rule spells it `bhavissantī`), the future, ⏭:
one rule says when they are used, in time to come {421}, and one lists the
twelve {429}. The same `ssa` with a past-looking tail and an `a-` before the
root is the `kālātipatti` ("the lapse of time"), 🔀, an action that never came
about: "if he had got the vehicle, he would have gone" {422}, {430}.

So ask what follows the `ssa`: `-ti`, `-nti`, `-si`, `-tha`, `-mi`, `-ma` make
the future, `-ā` and `-aṁsu` with the `a-` in front the conditional; person
and number as in [8.1](../8-1-reading-a-verb/), the rules in
[Appendix I](../i-verbs/). Kaccāyana gives the name `sabbadhātuka` to the
present, the command, the wish and the `hiyyattanī` {431}; the other four
sets, these two among them, take an `i` between the root and the ending {516},
so what you see is nearly always `-issa-`: `gam-i-ssati`, `kar-i-ssati`. That
`i` is the surest sign; each rule's `attanopada` six, rare in the texts, are in
[chapter 1](../1-basics/#verb-terminations-423-430).

### The future: `bhavissanti` ⏭

| what you see | person | number | example | rule |
| --- | :-: | :-: | --- | :-: |
| `-ssati` | third 🤟 | ⨀ | `bhavissati`, `gacchissati`, `karissati` | {429} |
| `-ssanti` | third 🤟 | ⨂ | `gacchissanti`, `karissanti`, `bhavissanti` | {429} |
| `-ssasi`, `-ssatha` | second 🤘 | ⨀, ⨂ | none in the rules or the texts | {429} |
| `-ssāmi` | first 👆 | ⨀ | `āharissāmi`, `karissāmi` | {429} |
| `-ssāma` | first 👆 | ⨂ | `karissāma`, `gamissāma` | {429} |

### Shapes before the ending

| what you see | what it is | example | rule |
| --- | --- | --- | :-: |
| `-issa-` | the `i` before the ending; a root ending in a vowel does without it | `gamissati`, `labhissati`, `pacissati` | {516} |
| `bhavissa-` | `bhū` for `asa`, the root of `atthi` | `bhavissati`, `bhavissanti` | {507} |
| `hessa-`, `hehi-`, `hohi-` | the future of `hoti`, its `ssa` often dropped | `hessati`, `hohiti` | {480} |
| `kāha-` | `karoti`'s future: `kāha` for `kara` and the `ssa` | `kāhati`, `kāhasi` ("you will do"), `kāhāmi` | {481} |

### The conditional: `kālātipatti` 🔀

| what you see | person | number | example | rule |
| --- | :-: | :-: | --- | :-: |
| `a-` … `-ssā`, `-ssa` | third 🤟 | ⨀ | `agacchissā` ("he would have gone"), `alabhissā`; the texts' `abhavissa`, `nābhavissa` | {430}, {519} |
| `a-` … `-ssaṁsu` | third 🤟 | ⨂ | `agacchissaṁsu`, `alabhissaṁsu` | {430}, {519} |
| `-sse`, `-ssatha`, `-ssaṁ`, `-ssāmhā` | second 🤘, first 👆 | ⨀, ⨂ | none in the rules or the texts | {430} |

The `a-` is the past's, from [8.4](../8-4-past/), and its rule names the
`kālātipatti` too {519}. In the texts the final vowel is short, `abhavissa`,
and the sentence carries `ce` ("if") or `no ce` ("if not"), its other half a
conditional or a `sattamī` (`paññāyetha`, "would be evident").

:::note[Look-alikes]
`-ssatha` belongs to both sets: the `a-` before the root and a `ce` mark the
conditional. A noun's `-assa` (④ or ⑥, `jātassa`) has no `i` before it and
sits on a lemma; `passati` ("sees") is a present: no `i`.
:::

### Examples

> Āraddhaṁ kho pana me vīriyaṁ **bhavissati** asallīnaṁ, upaṭṭhitā sati asammuṭṭhā, passaddho kāyo asāraddho, samāhitaṁ cittaṁ ekaggaṁ.

🚻①⨀(āraddha) 🔼(kho pana) 👆③⨀(ahaṁ) 🚻①⨀(vīriya) ⏭🤟⨀(bhavati) 🚻①⨀(asallīna) 🚹①⨂(upaṭṭhita) 🚺①⨀(sati) 🚹①⨂(asammuṭṭha) 🚹①⨀(passaddha) 🚹①⨀(kāya) 🚹①⨀(asāraddha) 🚻①⨀(samāhita) 🚻①⨀(citta) 🚻①⨀(ekagga)  
applied / and now /  with me /  determination / will become / active / presence / awareness / unmuddled / tranquility / body / unagitated / composed / mind / focused  
*`bhavissati`, "will become": `-ssati`, the future, third person singular {429}; `bhū` for `asa` {507}.*

> te **gacchissanti**, **karissanti**

*️⃣🤟🚹⨂①(ta) 🟢⨂🤟⏭(gacchati) 🟢⨂🤟⏭(karoti)  
they / will go / will do  
*`-ssanti`, the future, third person plural {429}: `gacchanti` with `ssa` before its `-nti` {421}.*

> No cetaṁ, bhikkhave, **abhavissa** ajātaṁ abhūtaṁ akataṁ asaṅkhataṁ, nayidha jātassa bhūtassa katassa saṅkhatassa nissaraṇaṁ paññāyetha.

🔼(no) 🔼(cetaṁ) 🚹⓪⨂(bhikkhu) 🔀🤟⨀(abhavissa) 🚻①⨀(ajāta) 🚻①⨀(abhūta) 🚻①⨀(akata) 🚻①⨀(asaṅkhata) 🔼(nayidha) 🚹⑥⨀(jāta) 🚹⑥⨀(bhūta) 🚹⑥⨀(kata) 🚹⑥⨀(saṅkhata) 🚻①⨀(nissaraṇa) 🔵⏯🤟⨀(paññāyati)  
surely? / if this / Bhikkhave / could have been / not born / not become / not made / not formed / wouldn't be here / of born / of becoming / of made / of formed / way out of / will be clearly known  
*`abhavissa`, "could have been": the conditional, third person singular, `a-` {519}, `-ssa` for `-ssā` {430}, after `no ce`.*

> te ce taṁ yānaṁ **alabhissaṁsu**, **agacchissaṁsu**

*️⃣🤟🚹⨂①(ta) ⏏️(ce) *️⃣🤟🚻⨀②(ta) 🚻⨀②(yāna) 🟢⨂🤟🔀(alabhissa) 🟢⨂🤟🔀(agacchissa)  
they / if / that / vehicle / had got / they would have gone  
*`-ssaṁsu`, the conditional, third person plural {430}, with the `a-` {519}; Kaccāyana's sentence at {422}.*

### Exercises

The first is from [chapter 11](../11-pbt/), the rest Kaccāyana's own, since the
texts hold only three such forms. Name the verb's set, person and number, and
what stands before its ending; then look it up.

:::tip[Hint]
You may find the [Digital Pali Dictionary](https://digitalpalidictionary.github.io/)
useful for looking up words, as well as doing the sandhi and compound word
deconstruction.
:::

1. “Kāmadhātuvepakkañca, ānanda, kammaṁ nābhavissa, api nu kho kāmabhavo paññāyethā”ti?
2. so gacchissati, karissati
3. kilanto addhānaṁ gamissati
4. so ce taṁ yānaṁ alabhissā, agacchissā
5. pabbajjā vā hohiti, maraṇaṁ vā me
6. kāhāmi kusalaṁ bahuṁ

### Answers

*Note: please do the exercises first before checking the answers.*

1. > “Kāmadhātuvepakkañca, ānanda, kammaṁ **nābhavissa**, api nu kho kāmabhavo paññāyethā”ti?

🚻①⨀(kāmadhātuvepakka) 🔼(ca) 🚹⓪⨀(ānanda) 🚻①⨀(kamma) 🔀🤟⨀(nābhavissa) 🔼(apinu kho) 🚹①⨀(kāmabhava) 🔵⏯🤟⨀(paññāyati) 🔼(ti)  
ripening in the realm of sensual desire / Ānanda / volitional action / would not exist / what / indeed / desire for becoming / is evident / quote  
*`nābhavissa` = `na` + `abhavissa`, "would not exist": the conditional, third person singular, `a-` {519}, `-ssa` {430}.*

2. > so **gacchissati**, **karissati**

*️⃣🤟🚹⨀①(ta) 🟢⨀🤟⏭(gacchati) 🟢⨀🤟⏭(karoti)  
he / will go / will do  
*`-ssati`, the future, third person singular {429}, the `i` {516}; Kaccāyana's sentence at {421}.*

3. > kilanto addhānaṁ **gamissati**

🚹⨀①🔽(ta)(kilanta) 🚻⨀②(addhāna) 🟢⨀🤟⏭(gacchati)  
tired / the road / he will travel  
*`gamissati`: `-ssati` {429} on the bare root `gam` with the `i` {516}; `gacchissati` adds `cch` {476}.*

4. > so ce taṁ yānaṁ **alabhissā**, **agacchissā**

*️⃣🤟🚹⨀①(ta) ⏏️(ce) *️⃣🤟🚻⨀②(ta) 🚻⨀②(yāna) 🟢⨀🤟🔀(alabhissa) 🟢⨀🤟🔀(agacchissa)  
he / if / that / vehicle / had got / he would have gone  
*`-ssā`, the conditional, third person singular {430}, with the `a-` {519}; `ce` ("if") marks the failed condition {422}.*

5. > pabbajjā vā **hohiti**, maraṇaṁ vā me

🚺⨀①(pabbajjā) ⏏️(vā) 🟢⨀🤟⏭(hoti) 🚻⨀①(maraṇa) ⏏️(vā) *️⃣👆⚧⨀⑥(ahaṁ)  
going forth / either / will be / death / or / mine  
*`hohiti`, "will be": the future of `hoti`, third person singular, its `ssa` dropped {480}.*

6. > **kāhāmi** kusalaṁ bahuṁ

🟢⨀👆⏭(karoti) 🚻⨀②(kusala) 🚻⨀②(bahu)  
I will do / good / much  
*`kāhāmi`, "I will do": the future, first person singular; `kāha` for `kara` and its `ssa` {481}.*
