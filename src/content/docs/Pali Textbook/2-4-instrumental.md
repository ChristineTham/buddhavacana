 `karaṇa` words refer to the instrument by which an action is done and are

 to as "instrumental" in English Pali grammar books.

### 2.4.1 The instrument {279}

* The thing by which an action is done
* The thing by which one sees
* The thing by which one hears

A word in any of these roles is a `karaṇa` ("instrument"). The `vibhatti`
ending it takes is given by the next rule, {286}.

#### Example

> **dattena** vīhiṁ lunāti

🚻⨀③(dattena) 🚹⨀②(vīhiṁ) 🟢⨀🤟▶️(lunāti)  
with a sickle / the rice / [he] reaps  
*[He] reaps the rice with a sickle.*

#### Exercises

Translate the following from Pali to English. Look up the words in a dictionary,
perform necessary sandhi and compound word deconstruction, and take note of
the `vibhatti` endings to deduce the meaning of the sentence.

:::tip[Hint]
You may find the [Digital Pali Dictionary](https://digitalpalidictionary.github.io/)
useful for looking up words, as well as doing the sandhi and compound word
deconstruction.
:::

1. vāsiyā kaṭṭhaṁ tacchati
2. pharasunā rukkhaṁ chindati
3. kudālena pathaviṁ khaṇati
4. satthena kammaṁ karoti
5. cakkhunā rūpaṁ passati
6. sotena saddaṁ suṇāti

#### Answers

*Note: please do the exercises first before checking the answers.*

1. > vāsiyā kaṭṭhaṁ tacchati

🚺⨀③(vāsiyā) 🚻⨀②(kaṭṭhaṁ) 🟢⨀🤟▶️(tacchati)  
with an adze / the wood / [he] planes  
*[He] planes the wood with an adze.*

2. > pharasunā rukkhaṁ chindati

🚹⨀③(pharasunā) 🚹⨀②(rukkhaṁ) 🟢⨀🤟▶️(chindati)  
with an axe / the tree / [he] cuts down  
*[He] cuts down the tree with an axe.*

3. > kudālena pathaviṁ khaṇati

🚹⨀③(kudālena) 🚺⨀②(pathaviṁ) 🟢⨀🤟▶️(khaṇati)  
with a spade / the earth / [he] digs  
*[He] digs the earth with a spade.*

4. > satthena kammaṁ karoti

🚻⨀③(satthena) 🚻⨀②(kammaṁ) 🟢⨀🤟▶️(karoti)  
with a knife / the work / [he] does  
*[He] does the work with a knife.*

5. > cakkhunā rūpaṁ passati

🚻⨀③(cakkhunā) 🚻⨀②(rūpaṁ) 🟢⨀🤟▶️(passati)  
with the eye / a form / [he] sees  
*[He] sees a form with the eye.*

6. > sotena saddaṁ suṇāti

🚻⨀③(sotena) 🚹⨀②(saddaṁ) 🟢⨀🤟▶️(suṇāti)  
with the ear / a sound / [he] hears  
*[He] hears a sound with the ear.*

### 2.4.2 The ③ ending for the instrument {286}

* The `vibhatti` ending ③ is used for the `karaṇa` ("instrument") `kāraka`.

Sometimes the ② ending is used in the sense of ③ instead {307}; this is
treated in section 2.5.

The ③ ending used with `maṇḍita` ("gladdened") and `ussukka` ("zealous")  
{315}, which also allows the ⑦ ending, is treated in section 2.3.

#### Example

> **agginā** kuṭiṁ jhāpeti

🚹⨀③(agginā) 🚺⨀②(kuṭiṁ) 🟠⨀🤟▶️(jhāpeti)  
with fire / the hut / [he] burns (causes to burn)  
*[He] burns the hut with fire.*

#### Exercises

1. manasā ce paduṭṭhena
2. manasā ce pasannena
3. kāyena kammaṁ karoti

#### Answers

1. > manasā ce paduṭṭhena

🚹⨀③(manasā ⏏️(ce) 🔽(ta)(paduṭṭhena))  
with mind / if / corrupted  
*If with a corrupted mind …*

2. > manasā ce pasannena

🚹⨀③(manasā ⏏️(ce) 🔽(ta)(pasannena))  
with mind / if / pure  
*If with a pure mind …*

These two phrases open the first two verses of the Dhammapada.

3. > kāyena kammaṁ karoti

🚹⨀③(kāyena) 🚻⨀②(kammaṁ) 🟢⨀🤟▶️(karoti)  
with the body / a deed / [he] does  
*[He] does a deed with the body.*

### 2.4.3 With `saha` and similar words {287}

* In construction with `saha` ("together with") and words like it.

Kaccāyana's examples show `saha` ("together with"), `vinā` ("without"),  
`saddhiṁ` ("together with") and `samaṁ` ("equal to"). The word with the ③  
ending is the one that these words go with.

#### Example

> sahā’pi **gaggena** saṅgho uposathaṁ kareyya

⏏️(saha) ⏏️(api) 🚹⨀③(gaggena) ⨀(🚹①(saṅgho) 🚹②(uposathaṁ) 🟢🤟⏯(kareyya))  
together with / even / Gagga / the Saṅgha / the uposatha (observance day ceremony) / may perform  
*The Saṅgha may perform the uposatha even together with Gagga.*

#### Exercises

1. vināpi gaggena
2. mahatā bhikkhusaṅghena saddhiṁ
3. sahassena samaṁ mitā

#### Answers

1. > vinā’pi gaggena

⏏️(vinā) ⏏️(api) 🚹⨀③(gaggena)  
without / even / Gagga  
*Even without Gagga.*

2. > mahatā bhikkhusaṅghena saddhiṁ

🚹⨀③(mahatā) 🚹⨀③🆎(bhikkhu+saṅgha) ⏏️(saddhiṁ)  
with a great / community of monks / together  
*Together with a great community of monks.*

3. > sahassena samaṁ mitā

🚻⨀③(sahassena) ⏏️(samaṁ) 🚺⨀①🔽(ta)(mitā)  
with a thousand / equal / measured  
*Measured as equal to a thousand.*

In the Tipiṭaka this is said of a gift (`dakkhiṇā`, feminine), which is why
`mitā` is feminine.

### 2.4.4 The agent of a passive verb {288}

* The `kattā` ("agent"), the one who does the action, when the verb is
  passive; in Kaccāyana's examples the verb is a past participle (`hato`,
  "killed").

With an active verb the `kattā` takes the ① ending instead {284}.

Sometimes the ⑥ ending is used for this agent, as in `kato me kalyāṇo` ("good  
has been done by me") {308}; this is treated in section 2.7.

#### Example

> **raññā** hato poso

🚹⨀③(raññā) ⨀🚹①(🔽(ta)(hato) poso)  
by the king / killed / the man  
*The man [was] killed by the king.*

#### Exercises

1. yakkhena dinno varo
2. ahinā daṭṭho naro

#### Answers

1. > yakkhena dinno varo

🚹⨀③(yakkhena) ⨀🚹①(🔽(ta)(dinno) varo)  
by the yakkha (spirit) / given / the boon (wish)  
*The boon [was] given by the yakkha.*

2. > ahinā daṭṭho naro

🚹⨀③(ahinā) ⨀🚹①(🔽(ta)(daṭṭho) naro)  
by a snake / bitten / the man  
*The man [was] bitten by a snake.*

### 2.4.5 Cause or reason {289}

* In the sense of `hetu` ("cause"), the reason for which something is done.

#### Example

> **annena** vasati

🚻⨀③(annena) 🟢⨀🤟▶️(vasati)  
because of food / [he] stays  
*[He] stays because of food.*

#### Exercises

1. dhammena vasati
2. vijjāya vasati
3. sakkārena vasati

#### Answers

1. > dhammena vasati

🚹⨀③(dhammena) 🟢⨀🤟▶️(vasati)  
because of the Dhamma / [he] stays  
*[He] stays because of the Dhamma.*

2. > vijjāya vasati

🚺⨀③(vijjāya) 🟢⨀🤟▶️(vasati)  
because of knowledge / [he] stays  
*[He] stays because of knowledge.*

3. > sakkārena vasati

🚹⨀③(sakkārena) 🟢⨀🤟▶️(vasati)  
because of honour / [he] stays  
*[He] stays because of honour.*

### 2.4.6 In the sense of the ⑦ ending {290}

* In the sense of the `vibhatti` ending ⑦, which normally marks the place or
  time at which something happens.

Kaccāyana's examples are all expressions of time ("at that time").

#### Example

> **tena kālena**

🚹⨀③(*️⃣🤟(tena) kālena)  
at that / time  
*At that time.*

#### Exercises

1. tena samayena
2. yena kālena
3. yena samayena
4. tena kho pana samayena

#### Answers

1. > tena samayena

🚹⨀③(*️⃣🤟(tena) samayena)  
at that / time  
*At that time.*

2. > yena kālena

🚹⨀③(*️⃣🤟(yena) kālena)  
at which / time  
*At which time.*

3. > yena samayena

🚹⨀③(*️⃣🤟(yena) samayena)  
at which / time  
*At which time.*

4. > tena kho pana samayena

🚹⨀③(*️⃣🤟(tena) ⏏️(kho) ⏏️(pana) samayena)  
at that / indeed / now / time  
*Now at that time.*

`tena kho pana samayena` opens a great many passages of the Tipiṭaka.

### 2.4.7 The limb marked by a defect {291}

* The diseased limb by which a defect of the one who has the limb is marked
  (the eye of a one-eyed man, the hand of a cripple).

#### Example

> **akkhinā** kāṇo

🚻⨀③(akkhinā) 🚹⨀①(kāṇo)  
in the eye / one-eyed  
*[He is] one-eyed in the eye* (i.e. blind in one eye).

#### Exercises

1. hatthena kuṇī
2. kāṇaṁ passati nettena
3. pādena khañjo
4. piṭṭhiyā khujjo

#### Answers

1. > hatthena kuṇī

🚹⨀③(hatthena) 🚹⨀①(kuṇī)  
in the hand / crippled  
*[He is] crippled in the hand.*

2. > kāṇaṁ passati nettena

🚻⨀②(kāṇaṁ) 🟢⨀🤟▶️(passati) 🚻⨀③(nettena)  
one-eyed / [he] sees / with the eye  
*One-eyed, [he] sees with the eye.*

Here `kāṇaṁ` is used as an adverb; the eye (`nettena`) is the limb that
marks him as one-eyed.

3. > pādena khañjo

🚹⨀③(pādena) 🚹⨀①(khañjo)  
in the foot / lame  
*[He is] lame in the foot.*

4. > piṭṭhiyā khujjo

🚺⨀③(piṭṭhiyā) 🚹⨀①(khujjo)  
in the back / hunchbacked  
*[He is] hunchbacked in the back.*

### 2.4.8 Distinguishing attribute {292}

* In the sense of a `visesana` ("distinguishing attribute"), the quality by
  which someone or something is told apart, such as clan or austerity.

#### Example

> **gottena** gotamo nātho

🚻⨀③(gottena) ⨀🚹①(gotamo nātho)  
by clan / Gotama / the Protector  
*The Protector [is] a Gotama by clan.*

#### Exercises

1. suvaṇṇena abhirūpo
2. tapasā uttamo

#### Answers

1. > suvaṇṇena abhirūpo

🚻⨀③(suvaṇṇena) 🚹⨀①(abhirūpo)  
by [his] golden complexion / handsome  
*[He is] handsome by [his] golden complexion.*

2. > tapasā uttamo

🚹⨀③(tapasā) 🚹⨀①(uttamo)  
by austerity / supreme  
*[He is] supreme by [his] austerity.*
