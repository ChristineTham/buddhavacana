This appendix summarises Kaccāyana's Nāmakappa {52}–{270}, the chapter that derives the inflected forms of nouns from their lemmas (`liṅga`). Each row gives the sutta number `{N}`, the sutta's own words, a one-line summary and the example words Kaccāyana cites; after "not" come the counter-examples, where the rule does not apply. The summaries use the book's symbols: ①–⑦ for the inflection forms, ⨀ singular, ⨂ plural, 🚹🚺🚻 for the genders. `jha`, `la`, `pa`, `gha` and `ga` are Kaccāyana's own technical names: `jha` and `la` are masculine or neuter lemma-endings in `i`/`ī` and `u`/`ū` {58}, `pa` the feminine endings in those vowels {59}, `gha` the feminine ending `ā` {60}, and `ga` the vocative `si` {57}. The full rules, with Kaccāyana's vutti and the derivation of every form, are at <https://kaccayana.github.io/kaccayana/2-namakappa>.

## Paṭhamakaṇḍa: definitions and the lemmas in `a`, `i`, `u`, `ā` {52}–{119}

| {68} | `Amā pato smiṁsmānaṁ vā` | `aṁ`, `ā` from `smiṁ`, `smā` [inflections] of `pa` [words] | `matyaṁ`, `matiyaṁ`, `matyā`, `matiyā` |

| {71} | `Yavakārā ca` | and [`jha`, `la` become] `y`, `v` before a vowel | `agyāgāraṁ`, `cakkhvāyatanaṁ`, `svāgataṁ, te mahāvīra` |
| {72} | `Pasaññassa ca` | And for `pa` | `puthabyā`, `ratyā`, `matyā` — not `puthaviyaṁ` |
| {73} | `Gāva se` | `go` to `gāva` before `s` | `gāvassa` |
| {74} | `Yosu ca` | And in `yo` inflections | `gāvo gacchanti`, `gāvo passanti`, `gāvī gacchanti`, `gāvī passanti` — not `gāvena`, `gāvā` |
| {75} | `Avamhi ca` | And for `aṁ` inflections | `gāvaṁ`, `gavaṁ`, `gavassa`, `gavo` |
| {76} | `Āvassu vā` | Optionally, from `āva` to `u` | `gāvuṁ`, `gāvaṁ` — not `gāvo` |
| {77} | `Tato namaṁ patimhā lutte ca samāse` | From that, `naṁ` becomes `aṁ` before `pati` when inflections are not elided in a compound | `gavaṁpati` — not `gopati`, `gavaṁ` |
| {78} | `O sare ca` | And `o` before vowel | `gavassakaṁ`, `gaveḷakaṁ`, `gavājinaṁ` — not `bhuvi`, `pasavo` |
| {79} | `Tabbiparītūpapade byañjane ca` | And inverse of that in `upapada` [auxiliary word] before consonant | `uggate sūriye`, `uggacchati`, `uggahetvā` — not `avasāne`, `avakiraṇe` |
| {80} | `Goṇa naṁmhi vā` | Optionally, `goṇa` before `naṁ` inflections | `goṇānaṁ sattannaṁ` — not `goṇabhūtānaṁ` |
| {81} | `Suhināsu ca` | And in `su`, `hi`, `nā` [inflections] | `goṇesu`, `goṇehi`, `goṇebhi`, `goṇena` — not `gosu`, `gohi` |
| {82} | `Aṁmo niggahitaṁ jhalapehi` | `aṁ`, `ma` become `ṁ` for `jha`, `la`, `pa` | `aggiṁ`, `isiṁ`, `gahapatiṁ`, `daṇḍiṁ` — not `agginā`, `pāṇinā` |
| {83} | `Saralopo’ mādesa paccayādimhi saralope tu pakati` | the vowel is elided before `aṁ`, a replacement or a suffix; but after the elision, the natural state | `purisaṁ`, `purise`, `pāpaṁ`, `pāpe` — not `Appamādo amataṁ padaṁ`, `amataṁ` |
| {84} | `Agho rassamekavacanayosvapi ca` | And not `gha` [vowels become] short in singular `yo` [inflections] also | `itthiṁ`, `itthiyo`, `itthiyā`, `vadhuṁ` — not `kaññaṁ`, `kaññāyo` |
| {85} | `Na sismimanapuṁsakāni` | No [shortening] for non-neuter words before `si` | `itthī`, `bhikkhunī`, `vadhū`, `daṇḍī` — not `bhoti itthi`, `bhoti vadhu` |
| {86} | `Ubhādito naminnaṁ` | `ubha` etc.: `naṁ` become `innaṁ` | `ubhinnaṁ`, `duvinnaṁ` — not `ubhayesaṁ` |
| {87} | `Iṇṇamiṇṇannaṁ tīhi saṅkhyāhi` | From the number `ti` (3) there are `iṇṇaṁ` and `iṇṇannaṁ` [replacements] | `tiṇṇaṁ`, `tiṇṇannaṁ` — not `dvinnaṁ` |
| {88} | `Yosu katanikāralopesu dīghaṁ` | In `yo` [inflections] replaced with `ni` or elided, [end vowel] is lengthened | `aggī`, `bhikkhū`, `rattī`, `yāgū` — not `aggi`, `bhikkhu` |
| {89} | `Sunaṁhisu ca` | And in `su`, `naṁ`, `hi` | `aggīsu`, `aggīnaṁ`, `aggīhi`, `rattīsu` — not `agginā`, `pāṇinā` |
| {90} | `Pañcādīnamattaṁ` | 5 etc. becomes `a` | `pañcasu`, `pañcannaṁ`, `pañcahi`, `chasu` — not `dvīsu`, `dvinnaṁ` |
| {91} | `Patissinīmhi` | For `pati` with `inī` | `gahapatānī` — not `gahapati` |
| {92} | `Ntussanto yosuca` | And the final of `ntu` before `yo` and the rest | `guṇavantesu`, `guṇavantānaṁ`, `guṇavantehi`, `guṇavantā` — not `isīnaṁ`, `guṇavā` |
| {93} | `Sabbassa vā aṁsesu` | Optionally for all in `aṁ`, `sa` | `satimaṁ bhikkhuṁ`, `satimantaṁ bhikkhuṁ`, `bandhumaṁ rājānaṁ`, `bandhumantaṁ rājānaṁ` — not `satimā bhikkhu`, `bandhumā rājā` |
| {94} | `Simhi vā` | Optionally in `si` | `himavanto pabbato` — not `himavā pabbato` |
| {95} | `Aggissini` | `aggi` to `ini` | `purato aggini`, `pacchato aggini`, `dakkhiṇato aggini`, `vāmato aggini` — not `aggi` |
| {96} | `Yosvakatarasso jho` | For `yo`, `jha` non-short becomes `a` | `aggayo`, `munayo`, `isayo`, `gahapatayo` — not `aggīsu`, `daṇḍino` |
| {97} | `Vevosu lo ca` | In `ve`, `vo`, `la`-words too | `bhikkhave`, `bhikkhavo`, `hetave`, `hetavo` — not `sayambhuvo`, `vessabhuvo` |
| {98} | `Mātulādīnamānattamīkāre` | `mātula` etc. endings become `āna` before `ī` | `mātulānī`, `ayyakānī`, `varuṇānī` — not `bhikkhunī`, `rājinī` |
| {99} | `Smāhismiṁnaṁ mhābhimhi vā` | Optionally, `smā`, `hi`, `smiṁ` become `mhā`, `bhi`, `mhi` | `purisamhā`, `purisasmā`, `purisebhi`, `purisehi` |
| {100} | `Na timehi katākārehi` | Not for `ta`, `ima` that have become `a` | `asmā`, `asmiṁ` — not `tamhā`, `tamhi` |
| {101} | `Suhisvakāro e` | In `su`, `hi`, `a` becomes `e` | `sabbesu`, `yesu`, `tesu`, `kesu` |
| {102} | `Sabbanāmānaṁ naṁmhi ca` | And pronouns in `naṁ` | `sabbesaṁ`, `sabbesānaṁ`, `yesaṁ`, `yesānaṁ` — not `amūsaṁ`, `amūsānaṁ` |
| {103} | `Ato nena` | From `a`, `nā` becomes `ena` | `sabbena`, `yena`, `tena`, `kena` — not `muninā`, `amunā` |
| {104} | `So` | `si` to `o` | `sabbo`, `yo`, `so`, `ko` — not `purisānaṁ`, `sayambhū` |
| {105} | `So vā` | Optionally, `so` | `akkharaso`, `suttaso`, `padaso`, `yasaso` |
| {106} | `Dīghorehi` | With `dīgha`, `ora` | `dīghaso`, `oraso`, `dīghamhā`, `oramhā` — not `saramhā`, `vacanamhā` |
| {107} | `Sabbayonīnamāe` | For both `yo`: `ā`, `e` | `purisā`, `purise`, `rūpā`, `rūpe` — not `aggayo`, `munayo` |
| {108} | `Smāsmiṁnaṁ vā` | Optionally, for `smā`, `smiṁ` | `purisā`, `purisasmā`, `purise`, `purisasmiṁ` — not `daṇḍinā`, `daṇḍismiṁ` |
| {109} | `Āya catutthekavacanassatu` | `āya` for ④⨀ inflections | not `isissa`, `purisassa mukhaṁ` |
| {110} | `Tayo neva ca sabbanāmehi` | And not the 3 [replacements] with pronouns ending in `a` | `sabbasmā`, `sabbasmiṁ`, `sabbassa`, `yasmā` — not `pāpā`, `pāpe` |
| {111} | `Ghato nādīnaṁ` | From `gha` in `nā` etc. | not `rattiyā`, `itthiyā` |
| {112} | `Pato yā` | From `pa` to `yā` | `rattiyā`, `itthiyā`, `deviyā`, `dhenuyā` — not `rattī`, `rattiṁ` |
| {113} | `Sakhato gasse vā` | Optionally, from `sakha` in `ga` | `bho sakha`, `bho sakhā`, `bho sakhi`, `bho sakhī` |
| {114} | `Ghate ca` | And from `gha` | `bhoti ayye`, `bhoti kaññe`, `bhoti kharādiye` |
| {115} | `Na ammādito` | Not for `amma` etc. | `bhoti ammā`, `bhoti annā`, `bhoti ambā`, `bhoti tātā` — not `bhoti kaññe` |
| {116} | `Akatarassā lato yvālapanassa vevo` | From `la`-words not made short `yo` vocative inflections become `ve`, `vo` | `bhikkhave`, `bhikkhavo`, `hetave`, `hetavo` — not `sayambhuvo`, `nāgiyo` |
| {117} | `Jhalato sassano vā` | `jha`, `la`: `sa` to `no` | `aggino`, `aggissa`, `sakhino`, `sakhissa` — not `isinā`, `bhikkhunā` |
| {118} | `Ghapato ca yonaṁ lopo` | And `gha`, `pa`: elision of `yo` | `kaññā`, `kaññāyo`, `rattī`, `rattiyo` |
| {119} | `Lato vokāro ca` | And from `la`: becomes `vo` | `bhikkhavo`, `bhikkhū`, `sayambhuvo`, `sayambhū` — not `jantuno` |

## Dutiyakaṇḍa: `ntu`, `tumha` and `amha`, the numerals, `rāja` and the rest {120}–{160}

| sutta | rule | summary | examples |
| :-: | --- | --- | --- |
| {120} | `Amhassa mamaṁ savibhattissa se` | For `amha` to `mamaṁ` in `sa` inflections |  |
| {121} | `Mayaṁ yomhi paṭhame` | `mayaṁ` before `yo` of the first form | `mayaṁ gacchāma`, `mayaṁ dema` — not `purisā tiṭṭhanti`, `ahaṁ gacchāmi` |
| {122} | `Ntussa nto` | `ntu` becomes `nto` before `yo` of the first form | `guṇavanto tiṭṭhanti` — not `sabbe sattā gacchanti`, `guṇavante passanti janā` |
| {123} | `Ntassa se vā` | Optionally `ntassa` for `ntu` before `sa` | `sīlavantassa jhāyino`, `sīlavato jhāyino` — not `sīlavā tiṭṭhati` |
| {124} | `Ā simhi` | `ā` for `ntu` before `si` | `sīlavā hoti`, `kāme pahāya satimā sampajāno`, `guṇavā`, `paññavā` — not `puriso tiṭṭhati`, `sīlavanto tiṭṭhanti` |
| {125} | `Aṁ napuṁsake` | `aṁ` for `ntu` before `si` in the neuter | `guṇavaṁ cittaṁ tiṭṭhati`, `rucimaṁ pupphaṁ virocati` — not `vaṇṇavantaṁ agandhakaṁ virūḷhapupphaṁ passasi tvaṁ`, `yathāpi ruciraṁ pupphaṁ, vaṇṇavantaṁ agandhakaṁ` |
| {126} | `Avaṇṇā ca ge` | `aṁ`, `a`, `ā` for `ntu` before `ga` | `bho guṇavaṁ`, `bho guṇava`, `bho guṇavā` |
| {127} | `To titā sasmiṁnāsu` | `to`, `ti`, `tā` for `ntu` before `sa`, `smiṁ`, `nā` | `satimato vipassissa`, `guṇavato`, `guṇavantassa`, `guṇavati` — not `guṇavā`, `satimā` |
| {128} | `Naṁmhi taṁ vā` | Optionally `taṁ` for `ntu` before `naṁ` | `dassanakāmo sīlavataṁ`, `guṇavataṁ`, `guṇavantānaṁ`, `satimataṁ` — not `guṇavanto tiṭṭhanti`, `satimanto tiṭṭhanti` |
| {129} | `Imassidamaṁsisu napuṁsake` | `idaṁ` for neuter `ima` before `aṁ`, `si` | `idaṁ cittaṁ passasi`, `idaṁ cittaṁ tiṭṭhati`, `imaṁ cittaṁ passasi`, `imaṁ cittaṁ tiṭṭhati` — not `imaṁ purisaṁ passasi`, `ayaṁ puriso tiṭṭhati` |
| {130} | `Amussāduṁ` | `aduṁ` for neuter `amu` before `aṁ`, `si` | `aduṁ pupphaṁ passasi`, `aduṁ pupphaṁ virocati`, `aduṁ cassa hoti tapassitāya` — not `amuṁ rājānaṁ passasi`, `asu rājā tiṭṭhati` |
| {131} | `Itthipumanapuṁsakasaṅkhyaṁ` | A numeral in the feminine, masculine and neuter: a governing heading |  |
| {132} | `Yosu dvinnaṁ dve ca` | `dve` for `dvi` before `yo`; and `duve`, `dvaya`, `ubha`, `ubhaya`, `duvi` | `dve itthiyo dvinnaṁ itthīnaṁ`, `dve itthiyo`, `dve dhammā`, `dve rūpāni` — not `dvīsu` |
| {133} | `Ti catunnaṁ tisso catasso tayo cattāro tīṇi cattāri` | forms of `ti`, `catu` before `yo`, by gender | `tisso vedanā`, `cattāri ariyasaccāni`, `catasso disā`, `tayo janā` — not `tīsu`, `catūsu` |
| {134} | `Pañcādīnamakāro` | final of `pañca` etc. with `yo` becomes `a` | `pañca dhammā sammadakkhātā`, `pañca`, `cha`, `satta` — not `dve`, `tayo` |
| {135} | `Rājassa rañño rājino se` | `rañño`, `rājino` for `rāja` before `sa` | `rañño māgadhassa seniyassa bimbisārassa nivesanaṁ`, `pacārā tassa rājino`, `rañño`, `rājino` — not `raññā` |
| {136} | `Raññaṁ naṁ mhi vā` | Optionally `raññaṁ` for `rāja` before `naṁ` | `raññaṁ idaṁ raṭṭhaṁ`, `rājūnaṁ idaṁ raṭṭhaṁ`, `raññaṁ, rājamahāmattānaṁ, khattiyānaṁ, brāhmaṇānaṁ`, `rājūnaṁ ādinnadaṇḍānaṁ ādinnasatthānaṁ` |
| {137} | `Nāmhi raññā vā` | Optionally `raññā` for `rāja` before `nā` | `tena raññā kataṁ`, `rājena vā kataṁ`, `na dāni tena raññā ciraṁ jīvitabbaṁ hoti` — not `rañño santakaṁ` |
| {138} | `Smiṁmhi raññe rājini` | `raññe`, `rājini` for `rāja` before `smiṁ` | `raññe sīlaṁ tiṭṭhati`, `rājini sīlaṁ tiṭṭhati`, `acchariyā abbhutā dhammā raññe cakkavattimhi` |
| {139} | `Tumhamhākaṁ tayi mayi` | `tayi`, `mayi` for `tumha`, `amha` before `smiṁ` | `tayi gedhitacittosmi`, `atthi ca ete dhammā mayi`, `tayi`, `mayi` — not `tvaṁ bhavasi`, `ahaṁ bhavāmi` |
| {140} | `Tvamahaṁ simhi ca` | `tvaṁ`, `ahaṁ` for `tumha`, `amha` before `si`; and `tuvaṁ` | `tvaṁ`, `ahaṁ` — not `tayi`, `mayi` |
| {141} | `Tava mama se` | `tava`, `mama` for `tumha`, `amha` before `sa` | `brahmacariyaṁ carissāma, bhagavā tava santike`, `idha, aggivessana, mama sāvako`, `tava`, `mama` — not `tayi`, `mayi` |
| {142} | `Tuyhaṁ mayhañca` | And `tuyhaṁ`, `mayhaṁ` for `tumha`, `amha` before `sa` | `tuyhaṁ dhanaṁ dīyate`, `mayhaṁ dhanaṁ dīyate`, `natthi tuyhaṁ sugati`, `āma, mayhaṁ kammaṁ` — not `tayā`, `mayā` |
| {143} | `Taṁ mamaṁmhi` | `taṁ`, `maṁ` before `aṁ` | `taṁ`, `maṁ` — not `janinda nāññatra tayā mayā vā`, `tayā` |
| {144} | `Tavaṁ mamañca navā` | `tavaṁ`, `mamaṁ` too, in some places | `caturaṅginiṁ senaṁ sannayhitvā mamaṁ abbhuyyāto`, `tavaṁ ārādhayitvāna jātiyā parimuccare`, `tavaṁ passati`, `mamaṁ passati` — not `taṁ passati`, `maṁ passati` |
| {145} | `Nāmhi tayā mayā` | `tayā`, `mayā` before `nā` | `sace tayā kataṁ`, `ekaṁ leṇaṁ mayā kataṁ`, `tayā kataṁ`, `mayā kataṁ` — not `tumhehi`, `amhehi` |
| {146} | `Tumhassa tuvaṁ tvamaṁmhi` | `tuvaṁ`, `tvaṁ` for `tumha` before `aṁ` | `kaliṅgarassa tuvaṁ maññe`, `kaṭṭhassa tvaṁ maññe` |
| {147} | `Padato dutiyā catutthī chaṭṭhīsu vo no` | after a word, `vo`, `no` in the plural of ②, ④, ⑥ | `pahāya vo gamissāmi`, `mā no ajja vikantiṁsu, rañño sūdā mahānase`, `dhammaṁ vo, bhikkhave, desessāmi`, `saṁvibhajetha no rajjena` — not `yo amhākaṁ satthā`, `eso amhākaṁ satthā` |
| {148} | `Temekavacanesu ca` | and `te`, `me` in the singular [of ④ and ⑥] | `dadāmi te gāmavarāni pañca`, `idaṁ te raṭṭhaṁ sadhanaṁ sayoggaṁ`, `dadāhi me gāmavaraṁ`, `idaṁ te raṭṭhaṁ` — not `tava ñāti`, `mama ñāti` |
| {149} | `Na aṁmhi` | not before `aṁ` | `passeyya taṁ vassasataṁ arogaṁ`, `passāsi maṁ vassasataṁ arogaṁ`, `so maṁ bravīti` |
| {150} | `Vā tatiye ca` | and optionally in the third [singular] | `kataṁ me saraṇamattano`, `kataṁ te pāpaṁ`, `kataṁ me pāpaṁ`, `kataṁ tayā pāpaṁ` — not `tayā kataṁ`, `mayā kataṁ` |
| {151} | `Bahuvacanesu vo no` | `vo`, `no` in the plural [of the third] | `kataṁ vo kammaṁ`, `kataṁ no kammaṁ`, `gāmaṁ vo gaccheyyātha` — not `tumhehi kataṁ`, `amhehi kataṁ` |

| {247} | `Tvādayo vibhattisaññāyo` | `to` and the rest are named `vibhatti` | `sabbato`, `yato`, `tato`, `kuto` |
| {248} | `Kvaci to pañcamyatthe` | Sometimes `to` in the sense of the fifth form | `viññāṇaṁ anidassanaṁ anantaṁ sabbato pabhaṁ`, `sabbato`, `yato`, `tato` — not `sabbasmā`, `imasmā` |
| {249} | `Tratha sattamiyā sabbanāmehi` | `tra`, `tha` after pronouns in the sense of the seventh form | `tatra sudaṁ āyasmā sudinno vesāliyaṁ viharati`, `sabbatra`, `sabbattha`, `yatra` |
| {250} | `Sabbato dhi` | `dhi` after `sabba`, sometimes | `sucetaso sabbadhi vippamutto`, `sabbadhi` — not `sabbasmiṁ` |
| {251} | `Kiṁ smā vo` | `va` after `kiṁ` | `kva gatosi tvaṁ devānaṁpiyatissa`, `tvaṁ pana, bhante, kva ahosi` |
| {252} | `Hiṁhaṁhiñcanaṁ` | `hiṁ`, `haṁ`, `hiñcanaṁ` after `kiṁ` | `rattiṁ kuhiṁ gacchasi kissa hetu`, `kuhiṁ`, `kuhaṁ`, `kuhiñcanaṁ` |
| {253} | `Tamhā ca` | And after `ta`: `hiṁ`, `haṁ` | `santhatāni tahaṁ tahaṁ ujjhitāni`, `tahiṁ`, `tahaṁ` |
| {254} | `Imasmā hadhā ca` | And after `ima`: `ha`, `dha` | `idha, bhikkhave, ekacco puggalo acelako hoti`, `iha`, `idha` |
| {255} | `Yato hiṁ` | `hiṁ` after `ya` | `yattha vā brahmā yena vā brahmā yahiṁ vā brahmā`, `yahiṁ` |
| {256} | `Kāle` | `kāle` "in the sense of time": a governing heading |  |
| {257} | `Kiṁsabbaññekayakuhi dādācanaṁ` | `dā`, `dācanaṁ` after `kiṁ`, `sabba`, `añña`, `eka`, `ya`, `ku` | `kadā te adhigataṁ`, `sabbadā ve sukhaṁ seti, brāhmaṇo parinibbuto`, `manāpameva bhāseyya, nāmanāpaṁ kudācanaṁ`, `kadā` |
| {258} | `Tamhā dāni ca` | And after `ta`: `dāni`, `dā` | `yadā sakkosi tadā`, `tadāni`, `tadā` |
| {259} | `Imasmā rahidhunādāni ca` | And after `ima`: `rahi`, `dhunā`, `dāni` | `etarahi kho vajjī dubbhikkhā`, `adhunā kālaṅkato`, `etarahi`, `adhunā` |
| {260} | `Sabbassa so dāmhi vā` | Optionally `sa` for `sabba` before `dā` | `upasantassa sadā satīmato`, `sadā`, `sabbadā` |
| {261} | `Avaṇṇo ye lopañca` | `a`/`ā` elided before the suffix `ya` | `kiṁ paṇḍiccaṁ karissati`, `vuḍḍhiṁ viruḷhiṁ vepullaṁ āpajjissanti`, `bāhussaccaṁ`, `paṇḍiccaṁ` |
| {262} | `Vuḍḍhassa jo iyiṭṭhesu` | `ja` for `vuḍḍha` before `iya`, `iṭṭha` | `addasa kho, bhikkhave, jeṭṭho isi kaniṭṭhaṁ isiṁ`, `jeyyo`, `jeṭṭho` |
| {263} | `Pasatthassa so ca` | `sa`, and `ja`, for `pasattha` | `mataṁ te jīvitā seyyo`, `svāhaṁ, brāhmaṇa, jeṭṭho seṭṭho lokassa`, `seyyo`, `seṭṭho` |
| {264} | `Antikassa nedo` | `neda` for `antika` | `nediyo`, `nediṭṭho` |
| {265} | `Bāḷhassa sādho` | `sādha` for `bāḷha` | `sādhiyo`, `sādhiṭṭho` |
| {266} | `Appassa kaṇa` | `kaṇa` for `appa` | `kaṇiyo`, `kaṇiṭṭho` |
| {267} | `Yuvānañca` | And [`kaṇa`] for `yuva` | `yena kaniṭṭho isi tenupasaṅkami`, `kaniyo`, `kaniṭṭho` |
| {268} | `Vantu mantu vīnañca lopo` | `vantu`, `mantu`, `vī` elided before `iya`, `iṭṭha` | `guṇiyo`, `guṇiṭṭho`, `satiyo`, `satiṭṭho` |
| {269} | `Yavataṁ ta la ṇa dakārānaṁ byañjanāni ca la ña jakārattaṁ` | `t`, `l`, `ṇ`, `d` with `y` become `c`, `l`, `ñ`, `j` | `paṇḍiccaṁ kosallaṁ nepuññaṁ`, `bāhussaccaṁ`, `paṇḍiccaṁ`, `vepullaṁ` — not `tiṇadalaṁ`, `ālasyaṁ anuṭṭhānaṁ bhogānaṁ paripantho` |
| {270} | `Amha tumha ntu rāja brahmatta sakha satthu pitādīhi smā nāva` | After `amha`, `tumha`, `ntu`, `rāja`, `brahma`, `atta`, `sakha`, `satthu`, `pitu` etc., `smā` has the form of `nā` | `mā kisittho mayā vinā`, `mayā`, `tayā`, `guṇavatā` — not `purisā` |
