## International Phonetic Alphabet for Pāḷi

As part of creating the World Tipiṭaka Edition in Roman script, 2007, the Dhamma Society's World Tipiṭaka Project proposed the following tables showing Siam/Roman Script Transliteration & Pāḷi Transcription in International Phonetic Alphabet (IPA Pāḷi)

This tables are based on the recitation of Pāḷi sound recording of the Pāḷi & Tipiṭaka Master, Mr Siri Petchai, Chairman of Dhamma Society Fund, under the supervision of Emeritus Professor Kanchana Ngowransri and was analysed by Emeritus Professor Vichin Phanupong.
