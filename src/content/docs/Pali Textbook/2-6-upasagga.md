Most verbs and many nouns in the texts begin with one of twenty short words
that Kaccāyana calls `upasagga` (prefixes, 🔼): `viharati` ("dwells", [9: 1.1](../9-fire/#u1-1)) is `vi`
before the root `√har`, `nirodho` ("cessation", [3: 4.12](../3-awakening/#u4-12)) `ni` before `√rudh`
("obstruct"), `abhisambuddho` ("fully awakened", [5: 9.1](../5-dhammacakka/#u9-1)) `abhi` and `saṁ` before
`√budh` ("wake"). A prefix carries no ending, since {221} drops every ending
after a prefix or a particle, and keeps its place in every form of the
word. [Chapter 1](../1-basics/) sorts the prefixes into those that
follow the root's meaning, those that reverse it (`gacchati` "goes",
`āgacchati` "comes") and those that sharpen it (`jānāti` "knows", `pajānāti`
"knows clearly").

Ask of such a word: what is left when the prefix is taken off, and what did it
add? The Digital Pali Dictionary's `construction` field answers the first
(`vi + hara + ti`), the first table below the second. A prefix may change
shape at the join, and two or three may stand together (`abhisambuddho`); the
second table gives the shapes. The texts' analysis lines and the dictionary treat a prefixed word as
one lemma, `🟢⨀🤟▶️(viharati)`: the prefix is to be seen through, not looked
up.

### The twenty prefixes

In Kaccāyana's order from {221}, with the commonest meaning
([Appendix F](../f-upasagga/) has every sense), one example and the rule that
shapes it at the join, or {221} where nothing changes. The examples are the
texts', each linked to its sentence, except `parābhavo` (Kaccāyana's own) and `dukkaraṁ`, `pidahati`,
`apagacchati` (Appendix F).

| prefix | meaning | example | rule |
| :-: | --- | --- | :-: |
| `pa` | forth; clearly | `pajānāti`, knows clearly ([3: 6.4](../3-awakening/#u6-4)) | {221} |
| `parā` | away; the reverse | `parābhavo`, ruin | {221} |
| `ni` | down, into | `nirodho`, cessation ([3: 4.12](../3-awakening/#u4-12)) | {221} |
| `nī` | out | `nissaraṇaṁ`, the way out ([Bonus: 50, 3.3](../11-pbt/#s50-u3-3)) | {28} |
| `u` | up | `udapādi`, arose ([5: 5.1](../5-dhammacakka/#u5-1)) | {35} |
| `du` | badly; hard to | `dukkaraṁ`, hard to do | {28} |
| `saṁ` | together; fully | `sambodhi`, full awakening ([5: 2.4](../5-dhammacakka/#u2-4)) | {31} |
| `vi` | apart; specially | `vimutti`, liberation ([5: 10.2](../5-dhammacakka/#u10-2)) | {221} |
| `ava` | down, off | `obhāso`, radiance ([5: 13.2](../5-dhammacakka/#u13-2)) | {50} |
| `anu` | along, after | `ananussutesu`, not heard before ([5: 5.1](../5-dhammacakka/#u5-1)) | {28} |
| `pari` | all round; fully | `pariññeyyaṁ`, to be fully understood ([5: 5.2](../5-dhammacakka/#u5-2)) | {28} |
| `adhi` | over; in | `ajjhattaṁ`, internal ([7: 7.1](../7-anatta/#u7-1)) | {45} |
| `abhi` | towards; fully | `abhiññā`, experiential understanding ([5: 2.4](../5-dhammacakka/#u2-4)) | {221} |
| `pati` | back, against | `paṭipadā`, the way ([5: 2.4](../5-dhammacakka/#u2-4)) | {48} |
| `su` | well | `suvisuddhaṁ`, totally purified ([5: 9.1](../5-dhammacakka/#u9-1)) | {221} |
| `ā` | to; the reverse | `āmantesi`, addressed ([9: 1.2](../9-fire/#u1-2)); `ādittaṁ`, burning ([9: 2.1](../9-fire/#u2-1)) | {221} |
| `ati` | beyond | `atikkamma`, surpassing ([5: 13.2](../5-dhammacakka/#u13-2)) | {28} |
| `api` | over, on | `pidahati`, closes | {221} |
| `apa` | away | `apagacchati`, goes away | {221} |
| `upa` | near | `upasamāya`, to calm ([5: 2.4](../5-dhammacakka/#u2-4)) | {221} |

The dictionary writes `u` as `ud`, `du` as `dur` and sometimes `ni` as `nī`
(`nibbāna`, `nī + √vā`), their shapes before a vowel or a doubled consonant;
`api` loses its `a` in `pidahati`.

### What happens at the join

The Sandhikappa ([Appendix E](../e-sandhi/)) says what happens where prefix
and root meet; each rule number links to the full rule.

| what you see | what it was | example | rule |
| --- | --- | --- | :-: |
| `sam`, `saṅ`, `sañ`, `san` | `saṁ`, with the nasal of the next consonant's group; kept before `v`, `s`, `h` | `sambodhi` ([5: 2.4](../5-dhammacakka/#u2-4)), `cakkhusamphasso` ([9: 2.3](../9-fire/#u2-3)), `saṅkhārā` ([7: 4.4](../7-anatta/#u4-4)), `saññā` ([7: 4.1](../7-anatta/#u4-1)), `sandiṭṭhiko`; `saṁvattati` ([7: 2.3](../7-anatta/#u2-3)) | {31}, {30} |
| `sam` before a vowel | `saṁ` | `samudayo` ([3: 3.12](../3-awakening/#u3-12)), `samanupassituṁ` ([7: 6.5](../7-anatta/#u6-5)) | {34} |
| a doubled consonant after `vi`, `u`, `pa`, `du`, `anu`, `pari`, `nī`, `ati` | the next consonant doubled | `vippayogo` ([5: 4.1](../5-dhammacakka/#u4-1)), `uppajjati` ([9: 2.4](../9-fire/#u2-4)), `nissaraṇaṁ` ([Bonus: 50, 3.3](../11-pbt/#s50-u3-3)), `atikkamma` ([5: 13.2](../5-dhammacakka/#u13-2)) | {28} |
| `ud` before a vowel | `u` with a `d` inserted | `udapādi` ([5: 5.1](../5-dhammacakka/#u5-1)), `udānesi` ([3: 5.1](../3-awakening/#u5-1)) | {35} |
| a final vowel gone | a vowel dropped before a vowel | `upādāna` (`upa` + `ādāna`, [3: 3.8](../3-awakening/#u3-8)), `upāyāsehi` (`upa` + `āyāsa`, [9: 2.6](../9-fire/#u2-6)) | {12} |
| `abbh` before a vowel | `abhi` | `abbhuggacchi` ([5: 13.1](../5-dhammacakka/#u13-1)) | {44} |
| `ajjh` before a vowel | `adhi` | `ajjhattaṁ` ([7: 7.1](../7-anatta/#u7-1)), `ajjhagamaṁ` ([Bonus: 46, 18.1](../11-pbt/#s46-u18-1)) | {45} |
| `pacc` before a vowel | `pati`, the `ti` become `cc` | `paccaññāsiṁ` ([5: 10.1](../5-dhammacakka/#u10-1)), `paccassosuṁ` ([7: 1.3](../7-anatta/#u1-3)), `paccayā` ([9: 2.4](../9-fire/#u2-4)) | {19} |
| `paṭi` | `pati`, in most words | `paṭipadā` ([5: 2.4](../5-dhammacakka/#u2-4)), `paṭicca` ([Bonus: 14, 8.1](../11-pbt/#s14-u8-1)), `paṭilabhiṁ` ([Bonus: 26, 12.4](../11-pbt/#s26-u12-4)) | {48} |
| `o` before a consonant | `ava` | `obhāso` ([5: 13.2](../5-dhammacakka/#u13-2)), `ohāretvā` ([Bonus: 26, 12.8](../11-pbt/#s26-u12-8)) | {50} |

:::note[`anu`, or `an` + `u`?]
Not every `anu-` is the prefix `anu`: the negative `an-` can stand before a
prefix beginning with `u`. `anupagamma` ("not approaching", [5: 2.4](../5-dhammacakka/#u2-4)) is
`an` + `upa` + `√gam` and `anuttaraṁ` ("unsurpassed", [5: 9.1](../5-dhammacakka/#u9-1)) `an` + `u` + `tara`,
while `ananussutesu` ([5: 5.1](../5-dhammacakka/#u5-1)) is `an` + `anu` + `√su`; the `construction` field
settles it.
:::

### Examples

> Tatra kho bhagavā pañcavaggiye bhikkhū **āmantesi**—

⏏️(tatra) ⏏️(kho) 🚹⨀①(bhagavant) 🚹⨂②🆎(pañca+vaggiya) 🚹⨂②(bhikkhu) 🟢⨀🤟⏮(āmanteti)  
there / indeed / the Bhagavā / of the group of five / bhikkhū / addressed  
*`ā` ("to") + `√mant` ("counsel"): counsel turned towards someone; unchanged before a consonant, and the `-esi` is the verb's ending, not the prefix's {221} ([5: 1.2](../5-dhammacakka/#u1-2)).*

> appiyehi **sampayogo** dukkho, piyehi **vippayogo** dukkho

🚹⨂③🆎(na+piya) 🚹⨀①(sampayoga) 🚹⨀①(dukkha) 🚹⨂③(piya) 🚹⨀①(vippayoga) 🚹⨀①(dukkha)  
with the unloved / association / suffering / from the loved / separation / suffering  
*`pa` + `√yuj` ("yoke") under two prefixes: `saṁ` ("together"), `sam` before `p` {31}, gives association, and `vi` ("apart"), the `p` doubled after it {28}, separation ([5: 4.1](../5-dhammacakka/#u4-1)).*

> **sambodhāya** nibbānāya **saṁvattati**

🚹⨀④(sambodha) 🚻⨀④(nibbāna) 🟢⨀🤟▶️(saṁvattati)  
to full awakening / to extinguishment / leads  
*`saṁ` twice: `sam` before `b` {31} in `sambodhāya` (`saṁ` + `√budh`, "wake"), `saṁ` kept before `v` {30} in `saṁvattati` (`saṁ` + `√vatt`, "proceed") ([5: 2.4](../5-dhammacakka/#u2-4)).*

> cakkhuṁ **udapādi**

🚻⨀①(cakkhu) 🟢⨀🤟⏮(uppajjati)  
insight / arose  
*`udapādi` is `u` ("up") + `√pad` ("go"), "went up": a `d` is inserted after `u` before a vowel {35}, and the `a` after it is the `a` put before the root in the past {519} ([5: 5.1](../5-dhammacakka/#u5-1)).*

### Answers

*Note: please do the exercises first before checking the answers.*

1. > **pajānāti**

🟢⨀🤟▶️(pajānāti)  
knows  
*`pa` + `√ñā` ("know"): `pa` sharpens the root, "knows clearly"; nothing changes at the join ([3: 6.4](../3-awakening/#u6-4)).*

2. > **abhisambuddho**

🚹⨀①🔽(ta)(abhisambuddha)  
fully awakened  
*`abhi` ("fully") + `saṁ` ("fully") + `√budh` ("wake"), two prefixes: `abhi` unchanged before `s`, `sam` before `b` {31} ([5: 9.1](../5-dhammacakka/#u9-1)).*

3. > **saṅkhārā**

🚹⨂①(saṅkhāra)  
mental constructions  
*`saṁ` ("together") + `√kar` ("make"): the `ṁ` becomes `ṅ` before `kh` {31} ([3: 3.1](../3-awakening/#u3-1)).*

4. > **pariññeyyaṁ**

🚻⨀①🔽(ṇya)(pariññeyya)  
to be fully understood  
*`pari` ("all round") + `√ñā` ("know"): the `ñ` doubles after the short `i` {28}; `-eyya` says "to be … -ed" (chapter 8) ([5: 5.2](../5-dhammacakka/#u5-2)).*

5. > **paṭipadā**

🚺⨀①(paṭipadā)  
way  
*`pati` ("towards") + `√pad` ("go"), the way: `pati` becomes `paṭi` {48}, nothing else changes ([5: 2.4](../5-dhammacakka/#u2-4)).*

6. > **nissaraṇaṁ**

🚻⨀①(nissaraṇa)  
way out of  
*`nī` ("out") + `√sar` ("go"), "going out": the `s` doubles after the prefix {28} and the `ī` written short ([Bonus: 50, 3.3](../11-pbt/#s50-u3-3)).*

7. > **ajjhattaṁ**

⏏️(ajjhattaṁ)  
internal  
*`adhi` ("in") + `atta` ("self"): `adhi` becomes `ajjh` before a vowel {45}; "in oneself", internal ([7: 7.1](../7-anatta/#u7-1)).*

8. > **uppajjati**

🟢⨀🤟▶️(uppajjati)  
arises  
*`u` ("up") + `√pad` ("go"), "goes up": the `p` doubles after `u` {28} ([9: 2.4](../9-fire/#u2-4)).*
