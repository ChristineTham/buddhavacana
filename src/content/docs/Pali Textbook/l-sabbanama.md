## Kinds of `sabbanāma`

1. Personal pronoun (puggalanāmaṁ).
2. Demonstrative pronoun (nidassananāmaṁ).
3. Relative pronoun (anvayīnāmaṁ).
4. Interrogative pronoun (pucchānāmaṁ).
5. Indefinite pronoun (anīyamanāmaṁ).
6. Possessive pronoun

## The `sabbanāma` rules in Kaccāyana

Each row gives the sutta number `{N}`, the sutta's own words, a one-line summary and the example words Kaccāyana cites; after "not" come the counter-examples, where the rule does not apply. The summaries use the book's symbols: ①–⑦ for the inflection forms, ⨀ singular, ⨂ plural, 🚹🚺🚻 for the genders. The full rules are at <https://kaccayana.github.io/kaccayana/2-namakappa>.

### `tumha`, `amha` and the pronominal endings {161}–{183}

| sutta | rule | summary | examples |
| :-: | --- | --- | --- |
| {161} | `Tumhamhehi namākaṁ` | `ākaṁ` for `naṁ` after `tumha`, `amha` | `yo amhākaṁ satthā`, `tumhākaṁ`, `amhākaṁ` — not `tumhehi`, `amhehi` |
| {162} | `Vā yvappaṭhamo` | Optionally `ākaṁ` for the `yo` that is not first | `tumhākaṁ passāmi`, `tumhe passāmi`, `amhākaṁ passasi`, `amhe passasi` — not `gacchatha tumhe`, `gacchāma mayaṁ` |
| {163} | `Sassaṁ` | `aṁ` for `sa` [after `tumha`, `amha`] | `tumhaṁ yevassa tena antarāyo`, `tumhaṁ dīyate`, `tava dīyate`, `tumhaṁ pariggaho` — not `tumhesu`, `amhesu` |
| {164} | `Sabbanāmakārate paṭhamo` | `e` for the first `yo` after a pronoun in `a` | `sabbe saṅkhārā aniccā`, `sabbe`, `ye`, `te` — not `amū purisā tiṭṭhanti`, `devā` |
| {165} | `Dvandaṭṭhā vā` | Optionally [`e`] after [a pronoun] standing in a `dvanda` | `katarakatame`, `katarakatamā` — not `devāsuranāgagandhabbamanussā`, `te` |
| {166} | `Nāññaṁ sabbanāmikaṁ` | No other pronominal operation [in a `dvanda`] | `pubbāparānaṁ`, `pubbuttarānaṁ`, `adharuttarānaṁ` |
| {167} | `Bahubbīhimhi ca` | And in a `bahubbīhi` | `piyapubbāya`, `piyapubbānaṁ`, `piyapubbe`, `piyapubbassa` — not `dakkhiṇapubbassaṁ`, `dakkhiṇapubbassā` |
| {168} | `Sabbato naṁ saṁ sānaṁ` | `saṁ`, `sānaṁ` for `naṁ` after any pronoun | `tesaṁ vūpasamo sukho`, `tāsaṁ itthīnaṁ`, `amūsaṁ bhedāya`, `sabbesaṁ` — not `sabbassa`, `yassa` |
| {169} | `Rājassa rāju sunaṁhisu ca` | `rāju` for `rāja` before `su`, `naṁ`, `hi` | `rājūnaṁ ādinnadaṇḍānaṁ ādinnasatthānaṁ`, `rājūhi vā corehi vā`, `rājūsu`, `rājūnaṁ` — not `rājā`, `rājesu` |
| {170} | `Sabbassimasse vā` | Optionally `e` for the whole of `ima` [before `su`, `naṁ`, `hi`] | `esu`, `imesu`, `esaṁ`, `imesaṁ` — not `etesu`, `etesaṁ` |
| {171} | `Animi nāmhi ca` | `ana`, `imi` for `ima` before `nā` | `anena dhammadānena, sukhitā hotu sā pajā`, `iminā buddhapūjena, patvāna amataṁ padaṁ`, `anena`, `iminā` — not `imesu`, `imesaṁ` |
| {172} | `Anapuṁsakassā yaṁ simhī` | `ayaṁ` for the non-neuter [`ima`] before `si` | `pariyuṭṭhito ayaṁ puriso`, `ayaṁ puriso`, `ayaṁ itthī` — not `pubbe kho me idaṁ cittaṁ parittaṁ ahosi`, `imaṁ purisaṁ passasi tvaṁ` |
| {173} | `Amussa mo saṁ` | `s` for the `m` of `amu` [before `si`] | `asu amutra upapanno`, `asu rājā`, `asu itthī`, `amuko rājā` — not `aduṁ pupphaṁ virocati`, `amuṁ purisaṁ passasi` |
| {174} | `Etatesaṁ to` | `s` for the `t` of `eta`, `ta` [before `si`] | `atha kho sā itthī sallakkhetvā`, `eso puriso`, `esā itthī`, `so puriso` — not `itaro puriso`, `itarā itthī` |
| {175} | `Tassa vā nattaṁ sabbattha` | Optionally `n` for the `t` of `ta` everywhere | `tato naṁ dukkhamanveti`, `detha nesaṁ cīvarānī`, `nāya`, `tāya` |
| {176} | `Sasmāsmiṁsaṁsāsvattaṁ` | `a` [for `ta`] before `sa`, `smā`, `smiṁ`, `saṁ`, `sā` | `na cassa hoti āpatti`, `assa`, `tassa`, `asmā` — not `amussaṁ`, `amussā` |
| {177} | `Imasaddassa ca` | And for the word `ima` | `asmiṁ loke paramhi ca`, `asmā lokā paramhā ca`, `assa`, `imassa` — not `etissaṁ`, `etissā` |
| {178} | `Sabbato ko` | Optionally `ka` after any pronoun [before `si`] | `asuko bhikkhu paṭhamassa jhānassa lābhī`, `sabbako`, `yako`, `sako` — not `sabbo`, `yo` |
| {179} | `Ghapato smiṁ sānaṁ saṁsā` | `saṁ`, `sā` for `smiṁ`, `sa` after `gha`, `pa` pronouns | `imissā makkaṭiyā`, `amussā itthiyā sāratto`, `sabbassaṁ`, `sabbassā` — not `itthiyaṁ`, `itthiyā` |
| {180} | `Netāhi smimāya yā` | No `āya`, `yā` for `smiṁ` after these | `imissaṁ parisāyaṁ`, `etissaṁ`, `etāyaṁ`, `imissaṁ` — not `tāya itthiyā mukhaṁ`, `kaññāya` |
| {181} | `Manogaṇādito smiṁnānamiā` | `i`, `ā` for `smiṁ`, `nā` after the `mano`-group | `sādhukaṁ manasi karotha`, `manasā ce paduṭṭhena`, `paṭhamena vayasā`, `uttamaṅge sirasmiṁ` — not `mano`, `siro` |
| {182} | `Sassa co` | `o` for `sa` [after the `mano`-group] | `manaso piyā hadayagāminiyo`, `thāmaso parāmāso na hoti`, `manaso`, `thāmaso` |
| {183} | `Etesamo lope` | `o` for the final of these when the inflection is elided | `manomayaṁ kāyaṁ upapanno`, `tejosamena cetasā viharāmi`, `kena tapoguṇena`, `manomayaṁ` — not `āposamena cetasā viharāmi`, `āposamena` |

### `atta`, `ta`, `eta`, `ima`, `amu`, `kiṁ` and the rest {211}–{236}

| sutta | rule | summary | examples |
| :-: | --- | --- | --- |
| {211} | `Attanto hismimanattaṁ` | `atta` final becomes `ana` before `hi` | `attanehi`, `attanebhi` — not `rājehi`, `rājebhi` |
| {212} | `Tato smiṁ ni` | after `atta`, `smiṁ` becomes `ni` | `attani kusalaṁ dhammaṁ`, `attani` |
| {213} | `Sassa no` | after `atta`, `sa` becomes `no` | `attano ca parassa ca`, `attano` |
| {214} | `Smā nā` | after `atta`, `smā` becomes `nā` | `attanā paribhuñji`, `attanā` |
| {215} | `Jhalato ca` | and after `jha`, `la`: `smā` becomes `nā` | `agginā vā daḍḍhaṁ hoti`, `agginā`, `daṇḍinā`, `bhikkhunā` — not `ahiṁsakā ye munayo`, `aggayo` |
| {216} | `Ghapato smiṁ yaṁ vā` | optionally, after `gha`, `pa`: `smiṁ` becomes `yaṁ` | `bhagavā sāvatthiyaṁ viharati`, `bhagavā nāḷandāyaṁ viharati`, `kaññāyaṁ`, `kaññāya` |
| {217} | `Yonaṁ ni napuṁsakehi` | `yo` becomes `ni` after neuters | `kāpotakāni aṭṭhīni bhavanti`, `aṭṭhīni`, `aṭṭhī`, `āyūni` — not `tisso itthiyo`, `itthiyo` |
| {218} | `Ato niccaṁ` | always, after neuter `a` | `cattārimāni, bhikkhave, bhayāni`, `kāni kammāni brūhayaṁ`, `yāni`, `tāni` |
| {219} | `Siṁ` | neuter `si` becomes `aṁ` | `sabbaṁ, bhikkhave, ādittaṁ`, `sabbaṁ`, `yaṁ`, `taṁ` |
| {220} | `Sesato lopaṁ gasipi` | after the rest, `ga` and `si` too are elided | `yena sā itthī tenupasaṅkami`, `bhoti itthi`, `sā itthī`, `bho daṇḍi` — not `satthussa sāsanenāpi`, `puriso gacchati` |
| {221} | `Sabbāsamāvuso pasagganipātādīhi ca` | all [inflections elided] after `āvuso`, prefixes, particles | `tvaṁ panāvuso gotama`, `tumhe panāvuso`, `padaso dhammaṁ vāceyya`, `vihāraṁ sve upagaccheyya` |
| {222} | `Pumassa liṅgādīsu samāsesu` | final of `puma` elided before `liṅga` etc. in compounds | `pulliṅgaṁ`, `pumbhāvo`, `puṅkokilo` — not `itthiyā ca itthiliṅgaṁ pāturahosi purisassa ca purisaliṅgaṁ`, `pumassa liṅgaṁ` |
| {223} | `Aṁ yamīto pasaññato` | `aṁ` becomes `yaṁ` after feminine `ī` | `taṁ itthiṁ etadavoca`, `itthiyaṁ`, `itthiṁ` — not `itthīhi sikkheyyāma`, `daṇḍinaṁ` |
| {224} | `Naṁ jhato katarassā` | `aṁ` becomes `naṁ` after a shortened `jha` | `nāhaṁ bhāyāmi bhoginaṁ`, `daṇḍinaṁ`, `bhoginaṁ` — not `vessabhuṁ vijitāvinaṁ`, `vātā kucchiṁ parikantanti` |
| {225} | `Yonaṁ no` | `yo` becomes `no` [after a shortened `jha`] | `bhogino bhogāni bhuñjanti`, `daṇḍino`, `bhogino`, `he daṇḍino` — not `sayambhuno pañcasatā`, `aggayo` |
| {226} | `Smiṁni` | `smiṁ` becomes `ni` [after a shortened `jha`] | `daṇḍini`, `bhogini` — not `byādhimhi uppajjantepi`, `byādhimhi` |
| {227} | `Kissa ka ve ca` | `kiṁ` becomes `ka` before `va`, and elsewhere | `kva gatosi tvaṁ devānaṁpiyatissa`, `tvaṁ pana, bhante, kva ahosi`, `ko taṁ ninditumarahati`, `kathaṁ bodhayituṁ dhammaṁ` — not `kuto āgatosi tvaṁ`, `kuto imesaṁ sāmaññaṁ` |
| {228} | `Ku hiṁhaṁsu ca` | `kiṁ` becomes `ku` before `hiṁ`, `haṁ` | `kuhiṁ gacchasi`, `kuhaṁ gacchasi`, `rattiṁ kuhiṁ gacchasi` |
| {229} | `Sesesu ca` | and `ka` before the remaining [inflections and suffixes] | `ko pakāro kathaṁ`, `kaṁ pakāraṁ kathaṁ`, `kathaṁ nu kho amhehi paṭipajjitabbaṁ` |
| {230} | `Tra to thesu ca` | And [`ku`] before `tra`, `to`, `tha` | `kuto imesaṁ sāmaññaṁ`, `kutra`, `kuto`, `kuttha` |
| {231} | `Sabbassetassākāro vā` | Optionally the whole of `eta` becomes `a` | `ato kho te, kaccāna, bahū hi bahutarā devā`, `ato`, `attha`, `etto` |
| {232} | `Tre niccaṁ` | Always before `tra` | `atra paviṭṭhassa suṅkaṁ gaṇhantu`, `atra` |
| {233} | `E tothesu ca` | And [optionally] `e` before `to`, `tha` | `ādīnavo ettha bhiyyo`, `etto`, `ato`, `ettha` |
| {234} | `Imassi thaṁ dāni ha to dhesu ca` | `i` for `ima` before `thaṁ`, `dāni`, `ha`, `to`, `dha` | `ito gaṇhanto pārājiko bhavissāmi`, `itthaṁ`, `idāni`, `iha` |
| {235} | `A dhunāmhi ca` | `a` [for `ima`] before `dhunā` | `adhunā kālaṅkato`, `adhunā` |
