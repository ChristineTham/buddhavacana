Most nouns follow [2.2](../2-2-nouns/). A few
dozen very common words do not. Their endings do the same jobs; what differs
is the shape, and the lemma never shows itself: no text has `rāja`, only
`rājā`, `rañño`.

So the question of 2.2, "which ending, and so which job?", gets one step in
front of it: "which of this word's few shapes is this?" The shapes recur:
`-ā` is ① (`rājā`, `bhagavā`), `-ānaṁ` is ② (`attānaṁ`), `-no` and `-to` are
④⑥ (`rañño`, `bhagavato`), `-nā` and `-tā` are ③ (`brahmunā`, `bhagavatā`),
`-ni` and `-i` are ⑦ (`attani`, `manasi`). The texts' symbol lines use the
dictionary's lemmas, `bhagavant`, `satthar`, `pitar`, `mano`; Kaccāyana's
are `bhagavantu`, `satthu`, `pitu`, `mana`. [Appendix G](../g-vibhatti/)
tables every rule cited below.

### `satthā`, `pitā`, `mātā`

The lemmas `satthu`, `pitu`, `mātu` 🚺 put `-ār-` or `-ar-` before most
endings {209}; in a compound the lemma is bare: `satthugāravena`.

| lemma | what you see | ending | rule |
| --- | --- | :-: | :-: |
| `satthu` (teacher), `pitu` (father), `mātu` (mother) | `satthā`, `pitā`, `mātā`; `satthāro`, `pitaro` | ①⨀; ①②⨂ | {199}; {205} |
| | `satthāraṁ`, `pitaraṁ`, `mātaraṁ`; `satthārā`, `pitarā`, `mātarā` | ②⨀; ③⨀ | {200}; {207}, {209} |
| | `satthu`, `satthuno`, `satthussa`; `pitu`; `mātu`, `mātuyā` | ④⑥⨀ | {203}, {117}, {61}; {112} |
| | `satthari`, `pitari`, `mātari` | ⑦⨀ | {206}, {208} |
| | `satthārānaṁ`, `mātānaṁ`; `pitūnaṁ`; `satthārehi` | ④⑥⨂; ③⑤⨂ | {201}, {202}; {200} |

### `bhagavā`, `āyasmā`, `arahaṁ` and the participles in `-ant`

`bhagavā` and `āyasmā` end in the affix `ntu` ("having"); the participles in
`-ant` and `mahanta` ("great") share these endings in every form but ① {187}.

| lemma | what you see | ending | rule |
| --- | --- | :-: | :-: |
| `bhagavantu`, `āyasmantu` (words in `ntu`) | `bhagavā`, `āyasmā` | ①⨀, ⓪ | {124}, {126} |
| | `bhagavantaṁ`, `āyasmantaṁ`, `mahantaṁ` | ②⨀ | {92} |
| | `bhagavatā`; `bhagavato`, `āyasmato`, `jānato`; `bhagavati` | ③⨀; ④⑥⨀; ⑦⨀ | {127}, {187} |
| | `guṇavanto`, `arahanto`; `guṇavantā`, `jānantā` | ①⨂ | {122}; {92} |
| | `sīlavataṁ`; `guṇavantehi`; `guṇavantesu` | ④⑥⨂; ③⑤⨂; ⑦⨂ | {128}; {92} |
| `arahanta`, `gacchanta`, `mahanta` | `arahaṁ`, `gacchaṁ`, `gacchanto` | ①⨀ | {186} |
| `santa` (being) | `santo`; `sataṁ`; `sabbhi` | ①⨀, ①⨂; ④⑥⨂; ③⨂ | {186}; {185} |
| `bhavanta` (sir) | `bho`, `bhante`; `bhavaṁ`; `bhoto` | ⓪; ①⨀; ④⑥⨀ | {243} |

:::note[`-ā`, `-ato`, `-ānaṁ`]
Here `-ā` is ① singular (`rājā`, `bhagavā`), on `purisa` ① plural or ⑤;
`-ato` is ④⑥ of a `ntu` or `-ant` word, but `-to` on a pronoun is "from"
(`tato`) {248}; `-ānaṁ` is ② singular {188} and ④⑥ plural {89}: the sentence
decides.
:::

### `kamma`, the `mano` group and `go`

The `s` of `manasā` is inserted before a vowel ending {184}.

| lemma | what you see | ending | rule |
| --- | --- | :-: | :-: |
| `kamma` 🚻 (action) | `kammunā`, `kammanā`, `kammena`; `kammani`, `kamme`; the rest as `citta` | ③⨀; ⑦⨀ | {160}; {197}, {108} |
| `mano` (mind), `cetas`, `vacas`, `tapas`, `tejo`, `yasas`, `siro`: the `mano` group | `mano`, `ceto`, `tejo`; `mano-` in a compound | ①⨀ | {104}; {183} |
| | `manasā`, `cetasā`, `vacasā`; `manaso`, `cetaso`; `manasi`, `vacasi` | ③⨀; ④⑥⨀; ⑦⨀ | {181}, {184}; {182} |
| `go` (cow) | `go`; `gāvo`; `gāvaṁ`, `gavaṁ`; `gāvena`; `gāvassa`; `gunnaṁ`; `gosu` | ①⨀; ①②⨂; ②⨀; ③⨀; ④⑥⨀; ④⑥⨂; ⑦⨂ | {220}; {74}; {75}; {74}; {73}; {81}; {81} |

### Examples

> bhikkhū **bhagavato** bhāsitaṁ abhinandunti

🚹⨂①(bhikkhu) 🚹⨀⑥(bhagavant) 🚻⨀②🔽(ta)(bhāsita) 🟢⨂🤟⏮(abhinandati) ⏏️(iti)  
bhikkhū / of the Bhagavā / what was spoken / rejoiced in / (end quote)  
*"Rejoiced in the Bhagavā’s words": `bhagavato`, `-ato`, ④⑥ singular of a word in `ntu` {127}, here ⑥; [5: 10.4](../5-dhammacakka/#u10-4).*

> **satthā** no garu

🚹⨀①(satthar) *️⃣👆⚧⨂⑥(ahaṁ) 🚹⨀①(garu)  
teacher / of us / respected  
*"Our teacher is respected": `satthā`, ① singular of `satthu` {199}, the `u` become `ā`; [Bonus: 1, 24.5](../11-pbt/#s1-u24-5).*

> mettāsahagatena **cetasā**

🚹⨀③🆎(mettā+sahagata) 🚹⨀③(cetas)  
filled with goodwill/friendliness / with intent  
*"With intent": `cetasā`, ③ singular of the `mano` group, `ā` for `nā` with `s` inserted {181}, {184}; [Bonus: 37, 1.11.52](../11-pbt/#s37-u1-11-52).*

### Exercises

Name the lemma, ending, number and rule of each word in bold; the sentences are
from chapters [5](../5-dhammacakka/), [7](../7-anatta/), [9](../9-fire/) and
the [bonus chapter](../11-pbt/).

6. Yāvajīvaṁ **arahanto** abrahmacariyaṁ pahāya

### Answers

*Note: please do the exercises first before checking the answers.*

1. > Pavattite ca pana **bhagavatā** dhammacakke

🚻⨀⑦🔽(ta)(pavattita) ⏏️(ca) ⏏️(pana) 🚹⨀③(bhagavant) 🚻⨀⑦🆎(dhamma+cakka)  
set in motion / and / now / by the Bhagavā / the wheel of the Dhamma  
*"Set in motion by the Bhagavā": `bhagavatā`, `-atā`, ③ singular of `bhagavantu` {127}; [5: 12.1](../5-dhammacakka/#u12-1).*

2. > mārena vā **brahmunā** vā kenaci vā

🚹⨀③(māra) ⏏️(vā) 🚹⨀③(brahma) ⏏️(vā) *️⃣🤟🚹⨀③(kaci) ⏏️(vā)  
by Māra / or / by Brahmā / or / by anyone / or  
*"Māra or Brahmā or by anyone": `brahmunā`, ③ singular of `brahma`, `a` become `u` before `nā` {198}; [5: 12.1](../5-dhammacakka/#u12-1).*

3. > aññaṁ **satthāraṁ** uddiseyyāthā”ti

*️⃣🤟🚹⨀②(añña) 🚹⨀②(satthar) 🟢⨂🤘⏯(uddisati) ⏏️(iti)  
another / teacher / appoint / (end quote)  
*"Will you appoint another teacher?": `satthāraṁ`, ② singular of `satthu`, with `-ār-` {200}; [Bonus: 1, 24.10](../11-pbt/#s1-u24-10).*

4. > **āyasmantaṁ** sāriputtaṁ bhagavā etadavoca

🚹⨀②(āyasmant) 🚹⨀②(sāriputta) 🚹⨀①(bhagavant) *️⃣🤟🚻⨀②(eta) 🟢⨀🤟⏮(vadati)  
Venerable / Sāriputta / the Bhagavā / this / said  
*"The Bhagavā said this to Venerable Sāriputta": `āyasmantaṁ`, ② singular of `āyasmantu`, `-antaṁ` {92}; `sāriputtaṁ` agrees with it; [Bonus: 44, 1.3](../11-pbt/#s44-u1-3).*

5. > eso me **attā**

*️⃣🤟🚹⨀①(eta) *️⃣👆⚧⨀⑥(ahaṁ) 🚹⨀①(atta)  
this / my / self  
*"This is my `attā` (self)": `attā`, ① singular of `atta`, `-ā` {189}, as `rājā`; [7: 6.5](../7-anatta/#u6-5).*

6. > Yāvajīvaṁ **arahanto** abrahmacariyaṁ pahāya

⏏️🆎(yāva+jīva) 🚹⨂①(arahant) 🚻⨀②🆎(na+brahma+cariya) 🔽(ya)(pajahati)  
life-long / liberated ones / uncelibacy / giving up  
*"Life-long arahants (liberated ones) give up uncelibacy": `arahanto`, ① plural of `arahanta` {122}, singular `arahaṁ` {186}; [Bonus: 32, 21.1](../11-pbt/#s32-u21-1).*

7. > Evaṁ **passaṁ**, bhikkhave, **sutavā** ariyasāvako

⏏️(evaṁ) 🚹⨀①🔽(anta)(passanta) 🚹⨂⓪(bhikkhu) 🚹⨀①(sutavant) 🚹⨀①🆎(ariya+sāvaka)  
thus / seeing / Bhikkhave / learned / awakened disciple  
*"Thus seeing, the learned awakened disciple": `passaṁ`, ① singular of the participle `passanta` {186}; `sutavā`, ① singular of a `ntu` word, as `bhagavā` {124}; [7: 11.1](../7-anatta/#u11-1).*

8. > **Mano** āditto, dhammā ādittā, **manoviññāṇaṁ** ādittaṁ, manosamphasso āditto.

🚹⨀①(mano) 🚹⨀①🔽(ta)(āditta) 🚹⨂①(dhamma) 🚹⨂①🔽(ta)(āditta) 🚻⨀①🆎(mano+viññāṇa) 🚻⨀①🔽(ta)(āditta) 🚹⨀①🆎(mano+samphassa) 🚹⨀①🔽(ta)(āditta)  
the mind / burning / mental phenomena / burning / mind-consciousness / burning / mind-contact / burning  
*"The mind is burning, mind-consciousness is burning": `mano`, the ① singular of the lemma `mano`, `-o` {104}; `mano-` kept before `viññāṇa` and `samphassa` in a compound {183}; [9: 7.1](../9-fire/#u7-1).*
