Some compounds name neither of their members. `chinnahattho` is `chinna` ("cut
off") and `hattha` ("hand"), yet it is not a hand and not a cutting: it is "a
man whose hand is cut off", `chinnahattho puriso`. Kaccāyana calls a compound
made "in the sense of another word" a `bahubbīhi` {328}; the name is itself
one, "much rice", said of a place that has it. The word named stands outside
the compound, which describes it as an adjective does.

So the question to ask when you meet a compound is: does it name one of its
own members, or something else? If something else, find the noun it agrees
with and unfold the compound with "whose", "by whom" or "in which". The
compound takes the gender, number and ending of that noun: `chinnahattho` is
🚹⨀① because `puriso` is. When the members join, their endings are dropped
{317} and each goes back to its lemma form {318}. The Buddha's epithets are
full of such compounds (`dasabalo`, "who has the ten powers"), and so is the
jhāna formula of chapter 10 (`avitakkaṁ avicāraṁ … jhānaṁ`).

### How to recognise a `bahubbīhi`

The vutti of {328} first shows the outside word in each ending from ② to ⑦:

| the outside word is | example | the phrase it stands for |
| --- | --- | --- |
| ② the thing reached | `āgatasamaṇo` | `āgatā samaṇā imaṁ saṅghārāmaṁ`: a monastery to which ascetics have come |
| ③ the doer | `jitindriyo` | `jitāni indriyāni anena samaṇena`: an ascetic by whom the senses are conquered |
| ④ the receiver | `dinnasuṅko` | `dinno suṅko yassa rañño`: a king to whom tax is paid |
| ⑤ the starting point | `niggatajano` | `niggatā janā asmā gāmā`: a village from which the people have gone out |
| ⑥ the owner | `chinnahattho` | `chinno hattho yassa purisassa`: a man whose hand is cut off |
| ⑦ the place | `sampannasasso` | `sampannāni sassāni yasmiṁ janapade`: a country in which the crops have ripened |

The ⑥ shape, "whose", is by far the commonest; the vutti's other examples sort
by the first member:

| the first member is | example | what it names |
| --- | --- | --- |
| a participle in `-ta` | `chinnahattho`, `jitindriyo` | see the table above |
| an adjective, often `a`- for `na` {333} | `anantañāṇo` | whose knowledge is endless |
| a numeral | `catuddiso`, `pañcacakkhu`, `dasabalo` | with the four directions, five eyes, ten powers |
| a comparison with `iva` or `viya` ("like") | `suvaṇṇavaṇṇo`, `brahmassaro` | whose colour is like gold's; whose voice is like Brahmā's |
| a compound nested inside; the vutti sorts these by whether the members agree (`tulyādhikaraṇa`) or not (`bhinnādhikaraṇa`) | `amitaghanasarīro`, `pīṇorakkhaṁsabāhu` | whose solid body is measureless (a `kammadhāraya` inside); whose chest, collar-bones, shoulders and arms are full (a `dvanda` inside) |

The name serves one later rule {167}: a `bahubbīhi` ending in a pronoun,
`piyapubbā` ("whose former [spouse] was dear"), keeps the ordinary noun
endings. Every rule number links to the full rule; the
[chapter overview](../6-0-samasa/) tables them all.

### Examples

> chinno hattho yassa purisassa so → **chinnahattho** puriso

🚹⨀①(🔽(ta)(chinno) hattho) *️⃣🤟🚹⨀⑥(yassa) 🚹⨀⑥(purisassa) *️⃣🤟🚹⨀①(so) → 🚹⨀①🆎(chinna+hattha) 🚹⨀①(puriso)  
cut off / hand / of whom / of the man / that [one] → whose hand is cut off / man  
*{328}: not a hand but the man whose hand it is; the compound takes the ending of `puriso`.*

> jitāni indriyāni anena samaṇena so → **jitindriyo** samaṇo

🚻⨂①(🔽(ta)(jitāni) indriyāni) *️⃣🤟🚹⨀③(anena) 🚹⨀③(samaṇena) *️⃣🤟🚹⨀①(so) → 🚹⨀①🆎(jita+indriya) 🚹⨀①(samaṇo)  
conquered / senses / by this / by the ascetic / that [one] → by whom the senses have been conquered / ascetic  
*{328}: the outside word is the doer, ③; `jita` + `indriya` gives `jitindriya`, `a` dropped before `i` {12}.*

> dasa balāni yassa so → **dasabalo** bhagavā

🚻⨂①(dasa balāni) *️⃣🤟🚹⨀⑥(yassa) *️⃣🤟🚹⨀①(so) → 🚹⨀①🆎(dasa+bala) 🚹⨀①(bhagavā)  
ten / powers / of whom / that [one] → who has the ten powers / the Blessed One  
*{328}: a numeral first; `balāni` is neuter plural, but the compound is masculine singular like `bhagavā`.*

### Exercises

Each of the following is a `bahubbīhi` from chapter 10, with the word it
agrees with where the sentence has one. Look each up in a dictionary, name its
members, unfold it into a phrase with `yassa` ("whose"), and say what it names.

You may find the [Digital Pali Dictionary](https://digitalpalidictionary.github.io/)
useful for looking up words, as well as doing the sandhi and compound word
deconstruction.
:::

1. passaddhakāyo
2. pītimanassa
3. maraṇadhammo
4. avitakkaṁ (jhānaṁ)
5. pītisukhaṁ (jhānaṁ)
6. sadevamanussāya (pajāya)
7. ekabhattikā (arahanto)
8. kaṇṇasukhā (vācā)

### Answers

*Note: please do the exercises first before checking the answers.*

1. > passaddho kāyo yassa so → **passaddhakāyo**

🚹⨀①(🔽(ta)(passaddho) kāyo) *️⃣🤟🚹⨀⑥(yassa) *️⃣🤟🚹⨀①(so) → 🚹⨀①🆎(passaddha+kāya)  
calmed / body / of whom / that [one] → whose body is calm  
*{328}: a participle first, like `chinnahattho`; selection 18, `passaddhakāyo sukhaṁ vediyati`.*

2. > pītiyā mano yassa so → **pītimano**

🚺⨀③(pītiyā) 🚹⨀①(mano) *️⃣🤟🚹⨀⑥(yassa) *️⃣🤟🚹⨀①(so) → 🚹⨀①🆎(pīti+mana)  
with joy / mind / of whom / that [one] → whose mind is filled with joy, delighted  
*{328}: a ③ inside, "with joy"; selection 18 has the ⑥, `pītimanassa`, "of one who is delighted".*

3. > maraṇaṁ dhammo yassa so → **maraṇadhammo**

🚻⨀①(maraṇaṁ) 🚹⨀①(dhammo) *️⃣🤟🚹⨀⑥(yassa) *️⃣🤟🚹⨀①(so) → 🚹⨀①🆎(maraṇa+dhamma)  
death / nature / of whom / that [one] → whose nature is death, subject to death  
*{328}: `-dhammo`, "whose nature is": so too `jātidhammo`, `jarādhammo`, `byādhidhammo`, `sokadhammo`, `saṅkilesadhammo` in selection 46.*

4. > natthi vitakko yasmiṁ taṁ → **avitakkaṁ** jhānaṁ

🟢⨀🤟▶️(natthi) 🚹⨀①(vitakko) *️⃣🤟🚻⨀⑦(yasmiṁ) *️⃣🤟🚻⨀①(taṁ) → 🚻⨀①🆎(na+vitakka) 🚻⨀①(jhānaṁ)  
there is no / thinking / in which / that → without thinking / jhāna  
*{328}: `a`- for `na` {333}, the outside word in the ⑦; neuter like `jhānaṁ` (selection 39), as is `avicāraṁ`.*

5. > pīti ca sukhañca yassa taṁ → **pītisukhaṁ** jhānaṁ

🚺⨀①(pīti) ⏏️(ca) 🚻⨀①(sukhaṁ) ⏏️(ca) *️⃣🤟🚻⨀⑥(yassa) *️⃣🤟🚻⨀①(taṁ) → 🚻⨀①🆎(pīti+sukha) 🚻⨀①(jhānaṁ)  
delight / and / ease / and / of which / that → having delight and ease / jhāna  
*{328}: a `dvanda`, "delight and ease", made "having delight and ease"; agreeing with `jhānaṁ` (selections 38, 39).*

6. > saha devamanussehi yā sā → **sadevamanussā** pajā

⏏️(saha) 🚹⨂③🆎(deva+manussa) *️⃣🤟🚺⨀①(yā) *️⃣🤟🚺⨀①(sā) → 🚺⨀①🆎(sa+devamanussa) 🚺⨀①(pajā)  
with / gods and humans / who / that → with its gods and humans / population  
*{328}: `sa`- is `saha` ("with"), a `dvanda` inside; feminine like `pajā`; selection 43 has the ⑦, `sadevamanussāya pajāya`.*

7. > ekaṁ bhattaṁ yassa so → **ekabhattiko**

🚻⨀①(ekaṁ bhattaṁ) *️⃣🤟🚹⨀⑥(yassa) *️⃣🤟🚹⨀①(so) → 🚹⨀①🆎(eka+bhattika)  
one / meal / of whom / that [one] → whose meal is one, eating one meal a day  
*{328}: a numeral first, like `dasabalo`, with the affix `ika` at the end; plural like `arahanto` (selection 32).*

8. > kaṇṇānaṁ sukhaṁ yassā sā → **kaṇṇasukhā** vācā

🚹⨂④(kaṇṇānaṁ) 🚻⨀①(sukhaṁ) *️⃣🤟🚺⨀⑥(yassā) *️⃣🤟🚺⨀①(sā) → 🚺⨀①🆎(kaṇṇa+sukha) 🚺⨀①(vācā)  
for the ears / ease / of which / that → pleasant to the ear / speech  
*{328}: a ④ inside, "ease for the ears"; feminine like `vācā` (selection 30).*
