Kaccāyana's Ākhyātakappa {406}–{523} is the chapter on verbs: the endings (`vibhatti`) that mark person, number, tense and mood, the suffixes that stand between root and ending, and the changes the root undergoes. The complete table of the ninety-six endings of the eight tenses and moods {423}–{431}, in both the `parassapada` (🟢) and the `attanopada` (🔵), is in the Basics chapter ([verb terminations](../1-basics/#verb-terminations-423-430)); this appendix gives the suffixes and the rules.

## Suffixes between root and ending

| suffix | sutta | use | example |
| :-: | :-: | --- | --- |
| `kha`, `cha`, `sa` | {433}, {434} | desire to do (`tumicchattha`), after `tija`, `gupa`, `kita`, `māna`, `bhuja`, `ghasa`, `hara`, `su`, `pā` and the like | `titikkhati` ("endures"), `bubhukkhati` ("wishes to eat"), `jigucchati` ("is disgusted") |
| `āya` | {435} | behaving like something, after a noun of comparison | `samuddāyati` ("behaves like the sea") |
| `īya` | {436}, {437} | treating like something; wishing something for oneself | `puttīyati` ("treats as a son"), `pattīyati` ("wishes for a bowl") |
| `ṇe`, `ṇaya`, `ṇāpe`, `ṇāpaya` | {438}, {439} | the causative (`kārita`, 🟠): making someone do | `kāreti`, `kārayati`, `kārāpeti`, `kārāpayati` ("has it done") |
| `ya` | {440}–{444} | the passive (`kamma`, 🔴) and the impersonal (`bhāva`) | `karīyati`, `kayirati` ("is done"); `ṭhīyate` ("there is standing") |
| `a` | {445} | the `bhū` class, active (🟢) | `bhavati` |
| `a` with `niggahīta` | {446} | the `rudha` class | `rundhati` |
| `ya` | {447} | the `divu` class | `dibbati` |
| `ṇu`, `ṇā`, `uṇā` | {448} | the `su` class | `suṇoti`, `suṇāti`, `pāpuṇāti` |
| `nā` | {449} | the `kī` class | `kiṇāti`, `jānāti` |
| `ppa`, `ṇhā` | {450} | the `gaha` class | `gheppati`, `gaṇhāti` |
| `o`, `yira` | {451} | the `tanu` class | `tanoti`, `kayirati` |
| `ṇe`, `ṇaya` | {452} | the `cura` class (also called `kārita`) | `coreti`, `corayati` |

The `attanopada` endings are used in the passive and the impersonal {453} and also in the active {454}; the `parassapada` endings in the active {456}. The endings come after these suffixes, never directly after the root {455}.

## Rules

Each row gives the sutta number `{N}`, the sutta's own words, a one-line summary and the example words Kaccāyana cites; after "not" come the counter-examples, where the rule does not apply. The summaries use the book's symbols: ①–⑦ for the inflection forms, ⨀ singular, ⨂ plural, 🚹🚺🚻 for the genders. The full rules, with the vutti and every derivation, are at <https://kaccayana.github.io/kaccayana/6-akhyatakappa>.

### Paṭhamakaṇḍa: the endings, the persons, the tenses and moods {406}–{431}

| sutta | rule | summary | examples |
| :-: | --- | --- | --- |
| {406} | `Atha pubbāni vibhattīnaṁ cha parassapadāni` | Now, the first six of the inflections are `parassapada` |  |
| {407} | `Parāṇyattanopadāni` | The latter [six] are `attanopada` |  |
| {408} | `Dve dve paṭhama majjhimuttamapurisā` | Two by two: first, middle and highest person |  |
| {409} | `Sabbesamekābhidhāne paro puriso` | When all [persons] are expressed together, the later person | `so ca paṭhati, tvañca paṭhasi, tumhe paṭhatha`, `so ca pacati, tvañca pacasi, tumhe pacatha` |
| {410} | `Nāmamhi payujjamānepi tulyādhikaraṇe paṭhamo` | With a co-referent noun, expressed or not: the first person | `na te gacchanti punabbhavaṁ`, `aggiṁ āharissāmīti gāmaṁ gacchati` — not `tena haññase tvaṁ devadattena` |
| {411} | `Tumhe majjhimo` | With `tumha`: the middle person | not `tayā paccate odano` |
| {412} | `Amhe uttamo` | With `amha`: the highest person | not `mayā ijjate buddho` |
| {413} | `Kāle` | `kāle`, "in [the sense of] time": a governing rule |  |
| {414} | `Vattamānā paccuppanne` | The `vattamānā` in the present | `yo koci avītarāgo taṁ vanasaṇḍaṁ pavisati`, `aggiṁ āharissāmīti gāmaṁ gacchati` |
| {415} | `Āṇatyā siṭṭhe’nuttakāle pañcamī` | In command and in a wish or blessing, time unstated: the `pañcamī` | `vacanīyamevāyasmā attānaṁ karotu`, `sukhī hotu, brāhmaṇa, ambaṭṭho māṇavo` |
| {416} | `Anumatiparikappatthesu sattamī` | In the senses of permission and supposition: the `sattamī` | `gaccheyyāsi pana tvaṁ, mārisa, tassa bhagavato upaṭṭhānaṁ`, `na cāpi appiyaṁ tuyhaṁ, kareyyāmi ahaṁ puna` |
| {417} | `Apaccakkhe parokkhātīte` | In the unwitnessed past: the `parokkhā` | `saccaṁ, bhikkhave, moggallāno āha`, `āhu sabbagihibandhanacchidaṁ` |
| {418} | `Hiyyopabhuti paccakkhe hiyyattanī` | From yesterday back, [witnessed or not]: the `hiyyattanī` | `agamā rājagahaṁ buddho, magadhānaṁ giribbajaṁ` |
| {419} | `Samīpe’jjatanī` | In the near [past]: the `ajjatanī` | `agamī buddhavarassa santikaṁ`, `agamuṁ satthu dassanaṁ` |
| {420} | `Māyoge sabbakāle ca` | And with `mā`, in every time | `sabbe bhadrāni passantu, mā kiñci pāpamāgamā` |
| {421} | `Anāgate bhavissantī` | In the future: the `bhavissantī` | `bhikkhunī ekā gāmantaraṁ gacchissati`, `moghapuriso sabbamattikāmayaṁ kuṭikaṁ karissati` |
| {422} | `Kriyātipanne’tīte kālātipatti` | In the past, when the action has lapsed: the `kālātipatti` | `so ce taṁ yānaṁ alabhissā, agacchissā`, `te ce taṁ yānaṁ alabhissaṁsu, agacchissaṁsu` |
| {423} | `Vattamānā ti anti, si tha, mi ma, te ante, se vhe, e mhe` | `vattamānā`: the twelve endings of the present |  |
| {424} | `Pañcamī tu antu, hitha, mima, taṁ antaṁ, ssu vho, e āmase` | `pañcamī`: the twelve endings of command and of a wish or blessing |  |
| {425} | `Sattamī eyya eyyuṁ, eyyā si eyyā tha, eyyāmi eyyāma, etha eraṁ, etho eyyāvho, eyyaṁ eyyāmhe` | `sattamī`: the twelve endings of permission and supposition |  |
| {426} | `Parokkhā au ettha, aṁmha, tthare, ttho vho, iṁ mhe` | `parokkhā`: the twelve endings of the unwitnessed past |  |
| {427} | `Hiyyattanī āū, ottha, aṁmhā, tthatthuṁ, se vhaṁ, iṁ mhase` | `hiyyattanī`: the twelve endings of the past before today |  |
| {428} | `Ajjatanī ī uṁ, o ttha, iṁ mhā, ā ū, se vhaṁ, aṁ mhe` | `ajjatanī`: the twelve endings of the near past |  |
| {429} | `Bhavissantī ssati ssanti, ssasi ssatha, ssāmi ssāma, ssate ssante, ssase ssavhe, ssaṁ ssāmhe` | `bhavissantī`: the twelve endings of the future |  |
| {430} | `Kālātipatti ssā ssaṁsu, sse ssatha, ssaṁ ssāmhā, ssatha ssisu, ssase ssavhe, ssiṁ ssāmhase` | `kālātipatti`: the twelve endings of the unrealised |  |
| {431} | `Hiyyattanī sattamī pañcamī vattamānā sabbadhātukaṁ` | `hiyyattanī`, `sattamī`, `pañcamī` and `vattamānā` are `sabbadhātuka` | `agamā rājagahaṁ buddho`, `no ce sāmaṁ vā gaccheyya`, `sakāni vā ñātikulāni gacchatu`, `aggiṁ āharissāmīti gāmaṁ gacchati` |

### Dutiyakaṇḍa: the suffixes, the causative and the passive, the root classes {432}–{457}

| sutta | rule | summary | examples |
| :-: | --- | --- | --- |
| {432} | `Dhātuliṅgehi parā paccayā` | the suffixes come after roots and lemmas: a governing rule | `atha kho so guḷo udake pakkhitto cicciṭāyati ciṭiciṭāyati` |
| {433} | `Tija gupa kita mānehi kha cha sā vā` | optionally `kha`, `cha`, `sa` after `tija`, `gupa`, `kita`, `māna` | `akkosaṁ vadhabandhañca, aduṭṭho yo titikkhati`, `so tena pāpakena kammena aṭṭīyati harāyati jigucchati`, `paṭiggaṇhāti vīmaṁsati paccāharati, āpatti saṁghādisesassa` — not `so tathāgataṁ sakkaroti garuṁ karoti māneti pūjeti` |
| {434} | `Bhuja ghasa hara su pādīhi tumicchatthesu` | [`kha`, `cha`, `sa`] after `bhuja`, `ghasa`, `hara`, `su`, `pā` and the like, in the sense of wishing to | `na sussūsati, na sotaṁ odahati` |
| {435} | `Āya nāmato kattūpamānādācāre` | `āya` after a noun to which the agent is likened, in the sense of behaving | `so habyaseso udake pakkhitto cicciṭāyati ciṭiciṭāyati` |
| {436} | `Īyūpamānā ca` | and `īya` after a noun that is a standard of comparison |  |
| {437} | `Nāmamhā’tticchatthe` | [`īya`] after a noun in the sense of wishing [it] for oneself |  |
| {438} | `Dhātūhi ṇe ṇaya ṇāpe ṇāpayā kāritāni hetvatthe` | the causative suffixes `ṇe`, `ṇaya`, `ṇāpe`, `ṇāpaya` after roots, named `kārita` | `kāreyyāti karoti vā kārāpeti vā`, `mātā rakkhati gopeti issariyaṁ kāreti vasaṁ vatteti`, `āmāvasesaṁ pāceti` |
| {439} | `Dhāturūpe nāmasmā ṇayo ca` | and `ṇaya` after a noun when it is to take the form of a root |  |
| {440} | `Bhāvakammesu yo` | `ya` [after all roots] in the impersonal and the passive | `kassa sāsanamāgamma, labbhate taṁ asokatā` |
| {441} | `Tassa cavaggayakāravakārattaṁ sadhātvantassa` | that `ya`, with the root's final, becomes a `c`-group letter, `y` or `v` | `rañño taṁ vuccate aghaṁ`, `sakammunā haññati pāpadhammo` |
| {442} | `Ivaṇṇāgamo vā` | optionally an `i`-vowel is inserted [before `ya`] | `karīyati hidaṁ buddhānaṁ sāsanaṁ` |
| {443} | `Pubbarūpañca` | and [`ya`] takes the form of the preceding [consonant] | `kāmesu adhipannānaṁ, dissate byasanaṁ bahuṁ` |
| {444} | `Tathā kattari ca` | likewise in the active too [`ya` is so replaced] | `dhammasmiṁ bhaññamānasmiṁ, atthaṁ bālo na bujjhati`, `sibbeyyāti sayaṁ sibbati` |
| {445} | `Bhūvādito a` | `a` after the `bhū` class [in the active] | `tassa taṁ kathinuddhāro, bhavati nāsanantiko`, `agārikassa yāguṁ vā bhattaṁ vā khādanīyaṁ vā pacati` |
| {446} | `Rudhādito niggahitapubbañca` | [`a`] after the `rudha` class, with a niggahīta before [the root's final]; and `i`, `ī`, `e`, `o` | `samaggaṁ saṁghaṁ bhindati, kappaṭṭhikaṁ kibbisaṁ pasavati, kappaṁ nirayamhi paccati` |
| {447} | `Divādito yo` | `ya` after the `divu` class [in the active] | `tathāgatappavedito dhammavinayo loke dibbati`, `muṭṭhīhipi yujjhanti` |
| {448} | `Svādito ṇu ṇā uṇā ca` | `ṇu`, `ṇā` and `uṇā` after the `su` class [in the active] | `so tattha ṭhito āsavānaṁ khayaṁ pāpuṇāti` |
| {449} | `Kiyādito nā` | `nā` after the `kī` class [in the active] | `sabbadānaṁ dhammadānaṁ jināti`, `dhunāti pāpake dhamme, dumapattaṁva māluto` |
| {450} | `Gahādito ppa ṇhā` | `ppa` and `ṇhā` after the `gaha` class [in the active] | `hatthena gaṇhāti, āpatti pārājikassa` |
| {451} | `Tanādito oyirā` | `o` and `yira` after the `tanu` class [in the active] | `kuṭi kira me kayirati`, `tesaṁ kayirāhi bhojanaṁ` |
| {452} | `Curādito, ṇe ṇayā` | `ṇe` and `ṇaya` after the `cura` class [in the active], named `kārita` | `sakko, bhikkhave, devānamindo sahassampi atthānaṁ muhuttena cinteti`, `api nu so rājabhaṇitaṁ vā bhaṇati rājamantanaṁ vā manteti` |
| {453} | `Attanopadāni bhāve ca kammani` | the `attanopada` endings in the impersonal and the passive |  |
| {454} | `Kattari ca` | and [the `attanopada` endings] in the active | `abbudā jāyate pesi`, `upekkhako sadā sato, na loke maññate samaṁ`, `kiṁ tuyhaṁ na rocate putta` |
| {455} | `Dhātuppaccayehi vibhattiyo` | the endings come after the root-suffixes |  |
| {456} | `Kattari parassapadaṁ` | the `parassapada` endings in the active | `ahaṁ karomīti na tena maññati` |
| {457} | `Bhūvādayo dhātavo` | `bhū` and the rest are `dhātu`, roots | `sokā na bhavanti tādino` |

### Tatiyakaṇḍa: reduplication (`abbhāsa`) and the special roots {458}–{481}

| sutta | rule | summary | examples |
| :-: | --- | --- | --- |
| {458} | `Kvacādivaṇṇānamekassarānaṁ dvebhāvo` | Sometimes the root's first syllable is doubled | `akkosaṁ vadhabandhañca, aduṭṭho yo titikkhati`, `aṭṭīyati harāyati jigucchati`, `kālena sakkacca dadāti yāguṁ`, `kusalo ca jahāti pāpakaṁ` — not `tadāyaṁ pathavī kampati saṅkampati sampakampati`, `anissito na calati` |
| {459} | `Pubbo’bbhāso` | The first [of the two] is called `abbhāsa` | `tatthappanādo tumulo babhūva` |
| {460} | `Rasso` | [The vowel of the `abbhāsa` is] short | `āpadāsu na jahāti` |
| {461} | `Dutiyacatutthānaṁ paṭhamatatiyā` | Second and fourth [letters in the `abbhāsa`] become first and third | `ajesi yakkho naravīraseṭṭhaṁ, tatthappanādo tumulo babhūva` |
| {462} | `Kavaggassa cavaggo` | `k` group [in the `abbhāsa`] becomes `c` group | `anāpucchā purato caṅkamati vā tiṭṭhati vā nisīdati vā`, `kaṅkhati vicikicchati nādhimuccati na sampasīdati` |
| {463} | `Mānakitānaṁ vatattaṁ vā` | Optionally `v`, `t` for [the `abbhāsa` of] `māna`, `kita` | `paṭiggaṇhāti vīmaṁsati paccāharati`, `yehi osadhehi so bhagavā devamanusse tikicchati` |
| {464} | `Hassa jo` | `h` [in the `abbhāsa`] becomes `j` |  |
| {465} | `Antassivaṇṇākāro vā` | The final [vowel of the `abbhāsa`] optionally becomes an `i`-vowel or `a` | `so tena pāpakena kammena aṭṭīyati harāyati jigucchati` |
| {466} | `Niggahitañca` | And [optionally] a `niggahīta` [at the end of the `abbhāsa`] | `aññataro bhikkhu paṇidhāya caṅkamati … paṇidhāya tiṭṭhati` |
| {467} | `Tato pāmānānaṁ vā maṁ sesu` | After it, `vā`, `maṁ` for `pā`, `māna` before `sa` | `paṭiggaṇhāti vīmaṁsati paccāharati, āpatti saṁghādisesassa` |
| {468} | `Ṭhā tiṭṭho` | [Optionally] `tiṭṭha` for `ṭhā` | `yo koci viññū dutiyo hoti, tiṭṭhati na nisīdati`, `tiṭṭhatu bhagavā kappaṁ, tiṭṭhatu sugato kappaṁ`, `bhedanasaṁvattanikaṁ vā adhikaraṇaṁ samādāya paggayha tiṭṭheyya`, `dve naḷakalāpiyo aññamaññaṁ nissāya tiṭṭheyyuṁ` — not `asampajāno mātukucchismiṁ ṭhāti` |
| {469} | `Pā pivo` | [Optionally] `piva` for `pā` | `theyyacitto ekena payogena pivati, āpatti pārājikassa`, `pivatu, bhante, ayyo sāgato kāpotikaṁ pasannaṁ`, `yo piveyya, yathādhammo kāretabbo`, `yaṁ amusmiṁ taḷāke manussā vā piveyyuṁ, gopasū vā piveyyuṁ` |
| {470} | `Ñāssa jā jaṁ nā` | [Optionally] `jā`, `jaṁ`, `nā` for `ñā` | `chandañca ruciñca ādāya voharati jānāti no bhāsati`, `chaḍḍitāchaḍḍitañca jāneyya`, `yaṁ jaññā— ‘sakkomi ajjeva gantun’ti`, `tesaṁ sabhāgadhammānaṁ, nāmamattaṁ na nāyati` — not `na cekassa bhāsitassa attho viññāyati` |
| {471} | `Disassa passa dissa dakkhā vā` | Optionally `passa`, `dissa`, `dakkha` for `disa` | `orato ṭhito puriso pārato ṭhitaṁ purisaṁ na passati`, `corānaṁ niviṭṭhokāso dissati, bhuttokāso dissati`, `sāvako evarūpaṁ ñassati vā dakkhati vā sakkhiṁ vā karissati` — not `addasa kho sudinno kalandaputto bhagavantaṁ` |
| {472} | `Byañjanantassa co chappaccayesu ca` | And a final consonant becomes `c` before `cha` | `aññataro nāgo nāgayoniyā aṭṭīyati harāyati jigucchati` |
| {473} | `Ko khe ca` | And `k` before `kha` | `yadā naṁ maññati bālo, bhayā myāyaṁ titikkhati` |
| {474} | `Harassa gīse` | `gī` for `hara` before `sa` | `pāpakammaṁ jigīsasi`, `uccāvacehupāyehi, paresamabhijigīsati` |
| {475} | `Brūbhūnamāhabhūvā parokkhāyaṁ` | `āha`, `bhūva` for `brū`, `bhū` in the `parokkhā` | `āyasmā upāli evamāha— “anāpatti, āvuso, supinantenā”ti`, `āhu byañjananimittakovidā` |
| {476} | `Gamissanto ccho vā sabbāsu` | Optionally `ccha` for the final of `gamu` in all [suffixes and inflections] | `vāṇijja raṭṭhādhipa gacchamāno, pathe addasāsimhi bhojaputte`, `bhārukacchaṁ gacchanto antarāmagge āyasmantaṁ upāliṁ passitvā`, `vesāliṁ agamā muni`, `agamī buddhavarassa santikaṁ` — not `“ayyā icchati tekaṭulayāguṁ pātun”ti` |
| {477} | `Vacassajjatanimhi makāro o` | The `a` of `vaca` becomes `o` in the `ajjatanī` | `“etha bhikkhavo”ti bhagavā avoca`, `te avocuṁ ‘vijānanti, taṁ atthaṁ sakyabhikkhavo’` — not `māyasmā evaṁ avaca` |
| {478} | `Akāro dīghaṁ hi mi mesu` | `a` is lengthened before `hi`, `mi`, `ma` | `tameva bhagavantaṁ saraṇaṁ gacchāhi yamahaṁ saraṇaṁ gato`, `āgamehi, bhante, yāva gharaṁ gacchāmi`, `gacchāma, bhagini, gacchāmāyya` |
| {479} | `Hi lopaṁ vā` | Optionally `hi` is elided | `gaccha, bhante, lomena tvaṁ muttosi`, `suvīra tattha gacchāhi, mañca tattheva pāpayā` |
| {480} | `Hotissarehohe bhavissantimhissassa ca` | `eha`, `oha`, `e` for the vowel of `hū` in the `bhavissantī`; `ssa` elided | `imaṁ sujāta pūjehi, taṁ te atthāya hehiti`, `pabbajjā vā hohiti, maraṇaṁ vā me`, `parivāravā hessati sattumaddano`, `upatisso kolito ca, aggā hessanti sāvakā` — not `idheva me maraṇaṁ bhavissati pabbajjā vā` |
| {481} | `Karassa sappaccayassa kāho` | [Optionally] `kāha` for `kara` with its `sa` [in the `bhavissantī`] | `sā caṇḍī kāhati kodhaṁ`, `akulaṁ kāhiti khippamattano`, `kiṁ kāhasi byañjanaṁ bahun”ti`, `kāhāmi kusalaṁ bahuṁ` — not `kathañhi nāma so, bhikkhave, moghapuriso sabbamattikāmayaṁ kuṭikaṁ karissati` |

### Catutthakaṇḍa: `vuddhi`, the changes of roots and endings, the insertions {482}–{523}

| sutta | rule | summary | examples |
| :-: | --- | --- | --- |
| {482} | `Dādantassaṁ mi mesu` | the final of `dā` becomes `aṁ` before `mi`, `ma` | `ahaṁ tesaṁ dammi`, `sumanā damma te mayaṁ` |
| {483} | `Asaṁyogantassa vuddhi kārite` | `vuddhi` of a root not ending in a conjunct, before the causative | `mātā rakkhati gopeti issariyaṁ kāreti vasaṁ vatteti`, `āvāhānipi kārāpeti` — not `naraṁ upasaṅkamitvā evaṁ cintayati` |
| {484} | `Ghaṭādīnaṁ vā` | optionally for `ghaṭa` and the like | `byantiṁ karoti anabhāvaṁ gameti`, `yuñjati ghaṭeti vāyamati` |
