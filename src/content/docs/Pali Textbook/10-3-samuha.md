A noun can take an affix that adds a sense to it instead of naming a new
thing. `janatā` ("the people") is `jana` ("person") with `tā`, "a collection
of persons"; `abhijjhālu` ("covetous") is `abhijjhā`
("covetousness") with `ālu`, "one in whom covetousness abounds". Kaccāyana
gives six such rules, one for each sense {354}–{359}, and in each the phrase
that spells the sense out, `gāmānaṁ samūho` ("a collection of villages"), and
the one word, `gāmatā`, say the same thing.

The question to ask when a noun you know turns up with `-tā`, `-iya`,
`-āyitattaṁ`, `-llaṁ` or `-ālu` on the end is what the affix has added: a
collection of them, an occasion of it, a likeness to it, a dependence on it,
an abundance of it. `tā` is the one you will meet; the others are rare, and
[Appendix H](../h-taddhita/) tables them all.

### How to recognise the five senses

| affix | what you see | what it means | example | rule |
| :-: | --- | --- | --- | :-: |
| `kaṇ`, `ṇa` | the first vowel lengthened, `-ako` or `-o` | a collection of what the word names (`samūha`, "a collection") | `rājaputtako` ("a body of princes") | {354} |
| `tā` | `-tā` after `gāma`, `jana`, `bandhu`, `sahāya` and the like | a collection of them | `gāmatā` ("a group of villages"), `janatā` ("the people") | {355} |
| `iya` | `-iyaṁ` after a word for an act | what is an occasion of it (`ṭhāna`, "occasion") | `madaniyaṁ` ("an occasion of intoxication"), `dassaniyaṁ` ("fit to be seen") | {356} |
| `āyitatta` | `-āyitattaṁ` | a likeness (`upamā`, "comparison"): a state that looks like it | `dhūmāyitattaṁ` ("smokiness, a cloud of smoke") | {357} |
| `la` | `-llaṁ`, the `l` doubled | what depends on it (`nissita`, "dependent on"), or is an occasion of it | `duṭṭhullaṁ` ("coarse, lewd"), `vedallaṁ` ("a catechism") | {358} |
| `ālu` | `-ālu` in place of the final vowel | one in whom it abounds (`bahula`, "abundant") | `abhijjhālu` ("covetous"), `dayālu` ("compassionate") | {359} |

The `ṇ` of `kaṇ` and `ṇa` is the signal of section [10.1](../10-1-apacca/):
it is dropped {396} and the first vowel of the word is lengthened {400}, so
`manussa` ("human being") gives `mānussako`. The new word's gender tells you
the affix too: a word in `tā` is feminine and keeps its `-ā` as its ① ending,
the words in `iya`, `āyitatta` and `la` are neuter in Kaccāyana's examples and
end in `-aṁ`, with the `l` of `la` doubled {404}, and a word in `ālu`
describes a person and ends in `-u`.

### Examples

> gāmānaṁ samūho → **gāmatā**

🚹⨂⑥(gāma) 🚹⨀①(samūha) → 🚺⨀①🔽(tā)(gāmatā)  
of villages / a collection → a group of villages  
*`tā` {355}: `-tā` added to `gāma` ("village"); the new word is feminine and ends in `-ā`.*

> madanassa ṭhānaṁ → **madaniyaṁ**

🚻⨀⑥(madana) 🚻⨀①(ṭhāna) → 🚻⨀①🔽(iya)(madaniya)  
of intoxication / the occasion → an occasion of intoxication  
*`iya` {356}: `-iya` in place of the final `a` of `madana` ("intoxication"); Kaccāyana's own example.*

### Exercises

Each of the following is a noun with one of this section's affixes. Say which
affix it carries, which word it is made from and what it means. Look the word
up in a dictionary and check the sense against the table above.

:::tip[Hint]
You may find the [Digital Pali Dictionary](https://digitalpalidictionary.github.io/)
useful for looking up words, as well as doing the sandhi and compound word
deconstruction.
:::

1. janatā
2. sahāyatā
3. timirāyitattaṁ
4. abhijjhālu
5. sītālu
6. dayālu

### Answers

*Note: please do the exercises first before checking the answers.*

1. > janānaṁ samūho → **janatā**

🚹⨂⑥(jana) 🚹⨀①(samūha) → 🚺⨀①🔽(tā)(janatā)  
of people / a collection → the people, the populace  
*`tā` {355}: `-tā` added to `jana` ("person"); a word you will meet often.*

2. > sahāyānaṁ samūho → **sahāyatā**

🚹⨂⑥(sahāya) 🚹⨀①(samūha) → 🚺⨀①🔽(tā)(sahāyatā)  
of companions / a collection → companionship  
*`tā` {355}: `-tā` added to `sahāya` ("companion"): a company of companions; Kaccāyana's own example.*

3. > timiraṁ viya dissati aduṁ vanaṁ → **timirāyitattaṁ**

🚻⨀①(timira) ⏏️(viya) 🔴⨀🤟▶️(dissati) *️⃣🤟🚻⨀①(amu) 🚻⨀①(vana) → 🚻⨀①🔽(āyitatta)(timirāyitatta)  
darkness / like / appears / that / forest → gloom, a darkness-like state  
*`āyitatta` {357}: `-āyitattaṁ` in place of the final `a` of `timira` ("darkness"): what looks like darkness; Kaccāyana's own example.*

4. > abhijjhā assa pakati → **abhijjhālu**

🚺⨀①(abhijjhā) *️⃣🤟🚹⨀⑥(ima) 🚺⨀①(pakati) → 🚹⨀①🔽(ālu)(abhijjhālu)  
covetousness / his / nature → covetous  
*`ālu` {359}: `-ālu` in place of the final `ā` of `abhijjhā`; the word describes the person.*

5. > sītaṁ assa pakati → **sītālu**

🚻⨀①(sīta) *️⃣🤟🚹⨀⑥(ima) 🚺⨀①(pakati) → 🚹⨀①🔽(ālu)(sītālu)  
cold / his / nature → sensitive to cold  
*`ālu` {359}: `-ālu` in place of the final `a` of `sīta` ("cold"); one in whom cold abounds.*

6. > dayā assa pakati → **dayālu**

🚺⨀①(dayā) *️⃣🤟🚹⨀⑥(ima) 🚺⨀①(pakati) → 🚹⨀①🔽(ālu)(dayālu)  
compassion / his / nature → kind, compassionate  
*`ālu` {359}: `-ālu` in place of the final `ā` of `dayā` ("compassion"); one in whom compassion abounds.*
