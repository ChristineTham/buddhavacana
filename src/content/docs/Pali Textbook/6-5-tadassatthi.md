The usage examples provided here are not exhaustive, other usages are possible.

A derived word (`taddhitanāma`, "secondary derivative") is a noun made from
another noun by adding an affix (`paccaya`) in the sense a rule names: the
`vibhatti` ending of the base is elided {317} and the new word takes a
`vibhatti` ending of its own {601}. This section covers the affixes that make
a word meaning "having that" (`tadassatthi`), the one who, or that which, has
the thing the base names: `vī` {364}, and `sa` by the `ca` of that rule; `sī`
after `tapa` and the like {365}; `ika` and `ī` after `daṇḍa` and the like
{366}; `ra` after `madhu` and the like {367}; `vantu` after `guṇa` and the
like {368}; `mantu` after `sati` and the like {369}; `ṇa` after `saddhā` and
the like {370}; and the change of `āyu` before `mantu` {371}. It ends with
`maya` in the sense "made of" (`tappakati`), the material a thing is made of
{372}. Kaccāyana analyses each "having that" word in two ways, `medhā yassa
atthi` ("he has wisdom", literally "of whom there is wisdom") or `tasmiṁ vā
vijjati` ("or in whom it is found"); this book shows the first. The vutti
gives every word in the masculine ①; the table of the Basics chapter notes
that the gender can vary with the noun the word qualifies. Appendix
[H](../h-taddhita/) tables every `taddhita` rule with its affix and example.

### 6.5.1 `vī` in the sense "he has that" {364}

* `vī` is added to a noun in the sense "having that" (`tadassatthi`): the one
  who has wisdom (`medhā`) is `medhāvī` ("wise"). The `vibhatti` ending of the
  base is elided {317}, the derived word takes a `vibhatti` ending of its own
  {601}, and as the word ends in `ī` its ① `si` is elided {220}.
* By the `ca` of the rule the affix `sa` is used as well: `sumedhaso` ("very
  wise"), where the final `ā` of `sumedhā` is shortened before the affix {403}.

#### Example

> medhā yassa atthi → **medhāvī**

🚺⨀①(medhā) *️⃣🤟🚹⨀⑥(yassa) 🟢⨀🤟▶️(atthi) → 🚹⨀①🔽(vī)(medhāvī)  
wisdom / of whom / there is → one who has wisdom, wise  
*`medhā yassa atthi` ("he has wisdom", literally "of whom there is wisdom") becomes `medhāvī` ("wise") with the affix `vī` {364}: the `vibhatti` ending of `medhā` is elided {317}, and the ① `si` of the new word is elided after `ī` {220}.*

### 6.5.2 `sī` after `tapa` and the like {365}

* After `tapa` ("austerity") and the like, the affix is `sī` in the same
  sense: `tapassī` ("an ascetic"), `yasassī` ("renowned"), `tejassī`
  ("majestic"). The `s` of the affix is doubled {28}, and the ① `si` is
  elided after `ī` {220}.

#### Example

> tapo yassa atthi → **tapassī**

🚹⨀①(tapo) *️⃣🤟🚹⨀⑥(yassa) 🟢⨀🤟▶️(atthi) → 🚹⨀①🔽(sī)(tapassī)  
austerity / of whom / there is → one who has austerity, an ascetic  
*`tapo yassa atthi` ("he has austerity") becomes `tapassī` ("an ascetic") with the affix `sī` {365}: the `s` is doubled {28} and the ① `si` is elided after `ī` {220}.*

### 6.5.3 `ika`, `ī` after `daṇḍa` and the like {366}

* After `daṇḍa` ("a staff") and the like, the affixes are `ika` and `ī`, both
  in use for the same base: `daṇḍiko` and `daṇḍī` ("one who has a staff"),
  `māliko` and `mālī` ("garlanded"). The final vowel of the base is elided
  before the affix {83}; a word in `ika` takes `o` in the ① {104}, a word in
  `ī` loses its `si` {220}.

#### Example

> daṇḍo yassa atthi → **daṇḍiko**

🚹⨀①(daṇḍo) *️⃣🤟🚹⨀⑥(yassa) 🟢⨀🤟▶️(atthi) → 🚹⨀①🔽(ika)(daṇḍiko)  
a staff / of whom / there is → one who has a staff  
*`daṇḍo yassa atthi` ("he has a staff") becomes `daṇḍiko` with the affix `ika` {366}: the final `a` of `daṇḍa` is elided before the affix {83} and `si` becomes `o` {104}.*

### 6.5.4 `ra` after `madhu` and the like {367}

* After `madhu` ("honey", "sweetness") and the like, the affix is `ra`:
  `madhuro` ("sweet"), `kuñjaro` ("an elephant", which has `kuñja`, jaws),
  `mukharo` ("talkative", who has a mouth), `susiro` ("hollow"), `ruciro`
  ("beautiful").
* The vutti lists eleven words; three of them (`sīsaro`, `sukaro`, `suṅkaro`)
  are bracketed in the CST edition as missing from the Sinhalese manuscripts,
  and the dictionaries explain some otherwise, `subharo` as `su` + `bhara`
  ("easy to support") and `suciro` as `su` + `cira` ("very long").

#### Example

> madhu yassa atthi → **madhuro**

🚻⨀①(madhu) *️⃣🤟🚹⨀⑥(yassa) 🟢⨀🤟▶️(atthi) → 🚹⨀①🔽(ra)(madhuro)  
honey, sweetness / of which / there is → having sweetness, sweet  
*`madhu yassa atthi` ("it has sweetness") becomes `madhuro` ("sweet") with the affix `ra` {367}; `si` becomes `o` {104}.*

### 6.5.5 `vantu` after `guṇa` and the like {368}

* After `guṇa` ("virtue") and the like, the affix is `vantu`: `guṇavā`
  ("virtuous"), `yasavā` ("famous"), `dhanavā` ("wealthy"), `paññavā`
  ("wise"), `balavā` ("strong"), `bhagavā` ("the Blessed One"). The derived
  lemma ends in `ntu`; in the ① singular `ntu` with `si` becomes `ā` {124}.
* A final `ā` of the base is shortened before the affix {403}: `paññā` gives
  `paññavā`.

#### Example

> guṇo yassa atthi → **guṇavā**

🚹⨀①(guṇo) *️⃣🤟🚹⨀⑥(yassa) 🟢⨀🤟▶️(atthi) → 🚹⨀①🔽(vantu)(guṇavā)  
virtue / of whom / there is → one who has virtue, virtuous  
*`guṇo yassa atthi` ("he has virtue") becomes `guṇavā` ("virtuous") with the affix `vantu` {368}: the derived lemma `guṇavantu` takes the ① `si` {601}, and `ntu` with `si` becomes `ā` {124}.*

### 6.5.6 `mantu` after `sati` and the like {369}

* After `sati` ("mindfulness") and the like, the affix is `mantu`: `satimā`
  ("mindful"), `jutimā` ("brilliant"), `rucimā` ("splendid"), `thutimā`
  ("praised"), `dhitimā` ("steadfast"), `matimā` ("wise"), `bhāṇumā`
  ("radiant", the sun). The ① is formed as with `vantu`: `ntu` with `si`

### 6.5.7 `ṇa` after `saddhā` and the like {370}

* After `saddhā` ("faith") and the like, the affix is `ṇa`: `saddho`
  ("faithful"), `pañño` ("wise"), `amaccharo` ("unselfish", free from
  stinginess). The `ṇ` is elided {396} and the final vowel of the base is
  elided before the affix {83}; in `saddho` and `pañño` a conjunct follows the
  first vowel, so there is no `vuddhi` {400} (section [6.1](../6-1-apacca/)).

#### Example

> saddhā yassa atthi → **saddho**

🚺⨀①(saddhā) *️⃣🤟🚹⨀⑥(yassa) 🟢⨀🤟▶️(atthi) → 🚹⨀①🔽(ṇa)(saddho)  
faith / of whom / there is → one who has faith, faithful  
*`saddhā yassa atthi` ("he has faith") becomes `saddho` ("faithful") with the affix `ṇa` {370}: the `ṇ` is elided {396}, the final `ā` of `saddhā` is elided before the affix {83}, and `si` becomes `o` {104}.*

### 6.5.8 `asa` for the `u` of `āyu` before `mantu`: `āyasmā` {371}

* Before the affix `mantu` {369}, the final `u` of `āyu` ("life") is
  replaced by `asa`: `āyasmantu`, whose ① is `āyasmā` ("venerable") {124}.
  The rule reshapes the word only; its sense is still "having that".

#### Example

> āyu assa atthi → **āyasmā**

🚻⨀①(āyu) *️⃣🤟🚹⨀⑥(assa) 🟢⨀🤟▶️(atthi) → 🚹⨀①🔽(mantu)(āyasmā)  
life / his / there is → one who has life, venerable  
*`āyu assa atthi` ("he has life") becomes `āyasmā` ("venerable") with the affix `mantu` {369}: the final `u` of `āyu` becomes `asa` before `mantu` {371}, and `ntu` with the ① `si` becomes `ā` {124}.*

### 6.5.9 `maya` in the sense "made of that" {372}

* `maya` is added to a noun in the sense "made of" (`tappakati`), the noun
  naming the material: a work made of gold (`suvaṇṇena pakataṁ kammaṁ`) is
  `sovaṇṇamayaṁ`; so `rūpiyamayaṁ` and `rajatamayaṁ` ("made of silver"),
  `jatumayaṁ` ("of lac"), `iṭṭhakamayaṁ` ("of brick"), `ayomayaṁ` ("of
  iron"), `mattikāmayaṁ` ("of clay"), `dārumayaṁ` ("of wood") and `gomayaṁ`
  ("made of cow", cow-dung). The vutti gives every word in the neuter ①
  singular, agreeing with `kammaṁ`: `si` becomes `aṁ` {219}.
* The base may change before the affix: `suvaṇṇa` becomes `sovaṇṇa` by
  direct statement {391}, `aya` ("iron") takes `o` as its final {183}, and
  the final `ā` of `iṭṭhakā` ("brick") is shortened {403}.

#### Example

> suvaṇṇena pakataṁ kammaṁ → **sovaṇṇamayaṁ**

🚻⨀③(suvaṇṇena) 🚻⨀①🔽(ta)(pakataṁ) 🚻⨀①(kammaṁ) → 🚻⨀①🔽(maya)(sovaṇṇamayaṁ)  
with gold / made / a work → made of gold  
*`suvaṇṇena pakataṁ kammaṁ` ("a work made of gold") becomes `sovaṇṇamayaṁ` ("made of gold") with the affix `maya` {372}: the `u` of `suvaṇṇa` becomes `o` by direct statement {391}, and the neuter ① `si` becomes `aṁ` {219}.*

1. > rūpiyena pakataṁ kammaṁ → **rūpiyamayaṁ**

🚻⨀③(rūpiyena) 🚻⨀①🔽(ta)(pakataṁ) 🚻⨀①(kammaṁ) → 🚻⨀①🔽(maya)(rūpiyamayaṁ)  
with silver / made / a work → made of silver  
*`rūpiyena pakataṁ kammaṁ` ("a work made of silver") becomes `rūpiyamayaṁ` ("made of silver") with the affix `maya` {372}.*

2. > iṭṭhakāya pakataṁ kammaṁ → **iṭṭhakamayaṁ**

🚺⨀③(iṭṭhakāya) 🚻⨀①🔽(ta)(pakataṁ) 🚻⨀①(kammaṁ) → 🚻⨀①🔽(maya)(iṭṭhakamayaṁ)  
with brick / made / a work → made of brick  
*`iṭṭhakāya pakataṁ kammaṁ` ("a work made of brick") becomes `iṭṭhakamayaṁ` ("made of brick") with the affix `maya` {372}: the final `ā` of `iṭṭhakā` is shortened before the affix {403}.*

3. > dārunā pakataṁ kammaṁ → **dārumayaṁ**

🚻⨀③(dārunā) 🚻⨀①🔽(ta)(pakataṁ) 🚻⨀①(kammaṁ) → 🚻⨀①🔽(maya)(dārumayaṁ)  
with wood / made / a work → made of wood, wooden  
*`dārunā pakataṁ kammaṁ` ("a work made of wood") becomes `dārumayaṁ` ("wooden") with the affix `maya` {372}.*
