Most suttas open with a present verb: `bhagavā … viharati`, "the Bhagavā
dwells". The present (`vattamānā`, "going on") is the set of endings for
what happens now {414}. Of a present form ask who acts, how many, and what
stands between root and ending: the dictionary lists the root.

Kaccāyana names twelve endings {423}: `ti`, `anti`, `si`, `tha`, `mi`, `ma`,
the `parassapada` (🟢), carry nearly every verb of the texts; `te`, `ante`,
`se`, `vhe`, `e`, `mhe`, the `attanopada` (🔵), serve the passive and the
impersonal {453}. [8.1](../8-1-reading-a-verb/) has the whole key; this
section takes the first six.

### The six endings of the present

| what you see | person | number | ending | from the texts | rule |
| :-: | :-: | :-: | :-: | --- | :-: |
| `-ti` | third 🤟 | ⨀ | `ti` | `viharati`, `hoti`, `labhati` | {423} |
| `-nti` | third 🤟 | ⨂ | `anti`, one `a` dropped {510} | `sambhavanti`, `pātubhavanti` | {423} |
| `-si` | second 🤘 | ⨀ | `si` | `viharasi` ([Bonus: 44, 2.5](../11-pbt/#s44-u2-5)) | {423} |
| `-tha` | second 🤘 | ⨂ | `tha` | `maññatha` ([7: 6.1](../7-anatta/#u6-1)) | {423} |
| `-āmi` | first 👆 | ⨀ | `mi` after a lengthened `a` {478} | `gacchāmi`, `viharāmi`, `vadāmi` ([9: 2.6](../9-fire/#u2-6)) | {423} |
| `-āma` | first 👆 | ⨂ | `ma`, likewise {478} | none; Kaccāyana's `gacchāma` | {423} |

The root `asa` (to be) reshapes them: `atthi` ("there is", `-tthi` for `-ti`
{494}; `natthi` is `na` + `atthi`), `santi` ("there are" {506}); `hoti`
(root `hū`) is the other "is", both in [8.7](../8-7-common-verbs/).

:::note[`-si` now, `-si` then]
`viharasi` ends in `-si`; so do `āmantesi` ("he addressed") and `ahosi`
("it was") in chapter [5](../5-dhammacakka/), the past ([8.4](../8-4-past/)), third person: their
`-si` follows `-e-`, `-o-` or `-ā-`, while the present `-si` follows the
class piece (`-asi`) and has `tvaṁ` ("you") as subject.
:::

### The eight classes and their pieces

A piece (`vikaraṇa`) fixed by the root's class (`gaṇa`) stands between root
and ending: Kaccāyana sorts the roots into eight classes and gives each its
piece {445}–{452}; the dictionary prints the class beside the root
(`√ñā (5, nā)`). Take off the ending, the piece and any `upasagga` (🔼), and
the root is left ([Appendix D](../d-roots/)); the `ṇ` of `ṇe`, `ṇaya` is a
signal, dropped {523}.

| class | piece | what you see before the ending | example | rule |
| :-: | :-: | --- | --- | :-: |
| `bhū` | `a` | `-ati` | `viharati`, `labhati` | {445} |
| `rudha` | `a`, with `ṁ` put into the root | a nasal before the last consonant, `-nd-`, `-ñj-` | `nibbindati`, `payuñjati` | {446} |
| `divu` | `ya` | a double consonant, `-jj-`, `-bb-` | `uppajjati`, `jāyati` | {447} |
| `su` | `ṇu`, `ṇā`, `uṇā` | `-ṇo-`, `-ṇā-` | none; Kaccāyana's `suṇāti` | {448} |
| `kī` | `nā` | `-nā-` | `pajānāti`, `sañjānāti` | {449} |
| `gaha` | `ppa`, `ṇhā` | `-ṇhā-` | `paggaṇhāti` | {450} |
| `tanu` | `o`, `yira` | `-oti` | `sacchikaroti`; Kaccāyana's `karoti` | {451} |
| `cura` | `ṇe`, `ṇaya` | `-eti`, `-ayati` | `paleti`, `vedayati` | {452} |

Two shapes have a second owner, settled by the dictionary: a double from
`ya` is also the passive (`vuccati`, "is called"), `-e-` also the causative
(`bhāveti`, "develops"), both in [8.6](../8-6-voice/);
[Appendix I](../i-verbs/) tables the verb chapter.

### Examples

> Ekaṁ samayaṁ bhagavā bārāṇasiyaṁ **viharati** isipatane migadāye.

*️⃣🤟🚹⨀②(eka) 🚹⨀②(samaya) 🚹⨀①(bhagavant) 🚺⨀⑦(bārāṇasī) 🟢⨀🤟▶️(viharati) 🚻⨀⑦(isipatana) 🚹⨀⑦(migadāya)  
one / time / the Bhagavā / at Bārāṇasī / dwells / at Isipatana / in the Deer Park  
*"At one time the Bhagavā was dwelling at Bārāṇasī, at Isipatana in the Deer Park." ([5: 1.1](../5-dhammacakka/#u1-1)) `viharati`: `-ti`, third person singular {423}; the piece `a`, `bhū` class {445}; `vi` + `har`, the root `hara`.*

> “Yadā have **pātubhavanti** dhammā,

⏏️(yadā) ⏏️(have) 🟢⨂🤟▶️(pātubhavati) 🚹⨂①(dhamma)  
when / indeed / appear / phenomena  
*"When indeed `dhammā` (phenomena) become manifest." ([3: 6.1](../3-awakening/#u6-1)) `pātubhavanti`: `-nti`, third person plural {423}, one `a` dropped {510}; `pātu` + `bhav`, the root `bhū` {445}.*

> Taṁ kiṁ **maññatha**, bhikkhave, rūpaṁ niccaṁ vā aniccaṁ vā”ti?

*️⃣🤟🚻⨀②(ta) *️⃣🤟🚻⨀②(kiṁ) 🟢⨂🤘▶️(maññati) 🚹⨂⓪(bhikkhu) 🚻⨀①(rūpa) 🚻⨀①(nicca) ⏏️(vā) 🚻⨀①🆎(na+nicca) ⏏️(vā) ⏏️(iti)  
that / what / do you think / bhikkhave / form / permanent / or / impermanent / or / (end quote)  
*"Bhikkhave, what do you consider: is `rūpa` (form) `nicca` (permanent) or `anicca` (impermanent)?" ([7: 6.1](../7-anatta/#u6-1)) `maññatha`: `-tha`, second person plural {423}, the subject `tumhe` unsaid {411}; `-ññ-` is `ya` of the `divu` class fused with the root `mana` (to think) {447}.*

> bhikkhu gacchanto vā ‘**gacchāmī**’ti **pajānāti**

🚹⨀①(bhikkhu) 🚹⨀①🔽(anta)(gacchanta) ⏏️(vā) 🟢⨀👆▶️(gacchati) ⏏️(iti) 🟢⨀🤟▶️(pajānāti)  
bhikkhu / walking / or / I walk / (end quote) / knows clearly  
*"A bhikkhu when walking knows clearly 'I am walking'." ([Bonus: 36, 3.1](../11-pbt/#s36-u3-1)) `gacchāmi`: `-mi`, first person singular, after a lengthened `a` {478}, on `gaccha`, the shape of `gamu` {476}. `pajānāti`: `-ti` after `nā`, `kī` class {449}; `pa` + `jā`, the root `ñā` {470}.*

### Exercises

Name each bold verb's ending, person and number, then the piece and the
root.

:::tip[Hint]
You may find the [Digital Pali Dictionary](https://digitalpalidictionary.github.io/)
useful for looking up words, as well as doing the sandhi and compound word
deconstruction.
:::

1. tasmā rūpaṁ ābādhāya **saṁvattati**,
2. **natthi** dāni punabbhavo
3. Cakkhuñca paṭicca rūpe ca **uppajjati** cakkhuviññāṇaṁ.
4. Evaṁ passaṁ, bhikkhave, sutavā ariyasāvako cakkhusmimpi **nibbindati**,
5. sabyābajjhaṁ vedanaṁ **vedayati** ekantadukkhaṁ,
6. Dhammatā esā, bhikkhave, yaṁ nibbinno viratto vimuttiñāṇadassanaṁ **sacchikaroti**.

### Answers

*Note: please do the exercises first before checking the answers.*

1. > tasmā rūpaṁ ābādhāya **saṁvattati**,

⏏️(tasmā) 🚻⨀①(rūpa) 🚹⨀④(ābādha) 🟢⨀🤟▶️(saṁvattati)  
therefore / form / to affliction / leads  
*"therefore `rūpa` leads to affliction," ([7: 2.3](../7-anatta/#u2-3)) `saṁvattati`: `-ati`, third person singular {423}, `bhū` class {445}; `saṁ` + `vatt`, the root `vatta` (to proceed).*

2. > **natthi** dāni punabbhavo

⏏️(na) 🟢⨀🤟▶️(atthi) ⏏️(dāni) 🚹⨀①🆎(puna+bhava)  
not / there is / now / appearing again  
*"There is now no more appearing again (of suffering)." ([5: 10.2](../5-dhammacakka/#u10-2)) `natthi` is `na` + `atthi`: the root `asa` (to be) with `-tthi` for `-ti` {494}, third person singular, no piece.*

3. > Cakkhuñca paṭicca rūpe ca **uppajjati** cakkhuviññāṇaṁ.

🚻⨀②(cakkhu) ⏏️(ca) 🔽(ya)(paṭicceti) 🚻⨂②(rūpa) ⏏️(ca) 🟢⨀🤟▶️(uppajjati) 🚻⨀①🆎(cakkhu+viññāṇa)  
faculty of seeing / and / dependent on / forms / and / arises / visual-consciousness  
*"Visual consciousness (`cakkhuviññāṇa`) arises dependent on `rūpa` (form) and the faculty of seeing." ([Bonus: 19, 2.2](../11-pbt/#s19-u2-2)) `uppajjati`: `-jj-` before `-ati`, `ya` of the `divu` class fused with the root `pada` (to go) {447}.*

4. > Evaṁ passaṁ, bhikkhave, sutavā ariyasāvako cakkhusmimpi **nibbindati**,

⏏️(evaṁ) 🚹⨀①🔽(anta)(passanta) 🚹⨂⓪(bhikkhu) 🚹⨀①(sutavant) 🚹⨀①🆎(ariya+sāvaka) 🚻⨀⑦(cakkhu) ⏏️(api) 🟢⨀🤟▶️(nibbindati)  
thus / seeing / bhikkhave / learned / awakened disciple / the eye / too / gives up  
*"Thus seeing, bhikkhave, the `sutavā` (learned) `ariyasāvaka` (awakened disciple) `nibbindati` (gives up) the eye" ([9: 8.1](../9-fire/#u8-1)) `nibbindati`: `-nd-` before `-ati`, the `rudha` class's `ṁ` put into the root `vida` (to feel) {446}; the prefix `ni`.*

5. > sabyābajjhaṁ vedanaṁ **vedayati** ekantadukkhaṁ,

🚺⨀②🆎(sa+byābajjha) 🚺⨀②(vedanā) 🟢⨀🤟▶️(vedayati) 🚺⨀②🆎(ekanta+dukkha)  
harmful / feelings / experiences / totally painful  
*"experiences totally painful harmful feelings" ([Bonus: 23, 1.6](../11-pbt/#s23-u1-6)) `vedayati`: `-ayati`, `ṇaya` of the `cura` class, its `ṇ` dropped {452}, {523}; the root `vida` (to feel).*

6. > Dhammatā esā, bhikkhave, yaṁ nibbinno viratto vimuttiñāṇadassanaṁ **sacchikaroti**.

🚺⨀①(dhammatā) *️⃣🤟🚺⨀①(eta) 🚹⨂⓪(bhikkhu) *️⃣🤟🚻⨀①(ya) 🚹⨀①🔽(ta)(nibbinna) 🚹⨀①🔽(ta)(viratta) 🚻⨀②🆎(vimutti+ñāṇa+dassana) 🟢⨀🤟▶️(sacchikaroti)  
characteristic / this / bhikkhave / whoever / one who is dispassionate / one who is detached / understanding and insight into liberation / personally realises  
*"This characteristic, Bhikkhave, one personally realises understanding and insight into liberation for whoever is dispassionate and detached." ([Bonus: 18, 1.27](../11-pbt/#s18-u1-27)) `sacchikaroti`: `-oti`, `o` of the `tanu` class {451}; `sacchi` + `kar`, the root `kara` (to do).*
