Many words of the texts end in `-ika`: `nāviko` ("a sailor"), `kāyikaṁ`
("bodily"), `nerayikā` ("beings in hell"), `dhammiko` ("righteous"). The
ending is the affix `ṇika`, and the word names something connected with its
base: `nāviko` is `nāvā` ("a boat") with `ṇika`, one who crosses by boat.
Kaccāyana gives this one affix a long list of senses in two rules {350},
{351}, from "mixed with" to "plays with"; it is an affix of many meanings
(`anekattha`), and two more rules add `ṇa` {352} and `ima`, `iya` {353} of
the same kind.

When you meet such a word, ask what the base is and what connection with it
the word names. The base is what is left when the affix is taken off and a
lengthened first vowel put back; the connection is one of the senses tabled
below, and the dictionary will tell you which one the word settled on. The
phrase beside each sense shows the base at work, with the ending the sense
needs: ③ "by which" (`nāvāya`), ② the thing studied (`vinayaṁ`), ⑦ the
place (`dvāre`), `assa` ("his") for what is someone's.

`ṇika` and `ṇa` carry the `ṇ` of section [10.1](../10-1-apacca/): the `ṇ` is
dropped {396} and the first vowel of the base is lengthened, `a` to `ā`, `i`
to `e`, `u` to `o` {400}, unless two consonants follow it or it is long
already. So `sakaṭa` ("a cart") gives `sākaṭiko` and `urabbha` ("a ram")
gives `orabbhiko`, but `gandha` ("perfume") gives `gandhiko` and `kāya` ("the
body") gives `kāyikaṁ`. `ima` and `iya` have no `ṇ` and change nothing:
`pacchā` ("after") gives `pacchimo`. [Appendix H](../h-taddhita/) tables every
rule with its examples.

### The senses of `ṇika`

| the sense | the phrase the word replaces | the word | meaning | rule |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | :-: |
| mixed with | `tilena saṁsaṭṭhaṁ` | `telikaṁ` | food with sesame in it | {350} |
| crosses by | `nāvāya tarati` | `nāviko` | a sailor | {350} |
| goes by | `sakaṭena carati` | `sākaṭiko` | a carter | {350} |
| carries by | `sīsena vahati` | `sīsiko` | one who carries on the head | {350} |
| lives in, born in, and other senses the rule's `vā` admits | `rājagahe vasati`, `rājagahe jāto` | `rājagahiko` | a man of Rājagaha | {350} |
| studies | `vinayaṁ adhīte` | `venayiko` | one who studies the Vinaya | {351} |
| made by | `kāyena kataṁ` | `kāyikaṁ` | bodily | {351} |
| located in | `sarīre sannidhānā` | `sārīrikā` | bodily (of a feeling) | {351} |
| appointed to | `dvāre niyutto` | `dovāriko` | a doorkeeper | {351} |
| his craft | `vīṇā assa sippaṁ` | `veṇiko` | a lute-player | {351} |
| his merchandise | `gandho assa bhaṇḍaṁ` | `gandhiko` | a perfume-seller | {351} |
| his livelihood | `urabbhaṁ hantvā jīvati` (lives by killing sheep) | `orabbhiko` | a sheep-butcher | {351} |
| and, by the rule's "and the like": killed with, bound with, his weapon, his ailment, devoted to, the property of, bought with, measuring, a heap of, worth, plays with | `akkhena dibbati` (plays with dice) | `akkhiko` | a gambler | {351} |

### `ṇa`, `ima` and `iya`

| affix | the sense | the phrase the word replaces | the word | meaning | rule |
| :-: | --- | --- | --- | --- | :-: |
| `ṇa` | dyed with | `kasāvena rattaṁ` (dyed with the brown dye) | `kāsāvaṁ` | the ochre robe | {352} |
| `ṇa` | this is his | `sūkarassa idaṁ maṁsaṁ` (this meat is the pig's) | `sokaraṁ` | pork | {352} |
| `ṇa` | born in, come from, lord of | `madhurāya jāto` | `mādhuro` | a man of Madhurā | {352} |
| `ṇa` | the month of a constellation | `kattikāya niyutto māso` | `kattiko` | the month of Kattikā, October–November | {352} |
| `ṇa` | a collection, whose deity, who studies, the land of, where a thing grows, produced by, whose dwelling | `sāgarehi nibbatto` (produced by the Sāgaras) | `sāgaro` | the ocean | {352} |
| `ima` | born after, in the middle, at the end | `pacchā jāto` | `pacchimo` | last; western | {353} |
| `iya` | born of a kind | `bodhisattajātiyā jāto` | `bodhisattajātiyo` | one of the Bodhisatta's kind | {353} |
| `ima`, `iya`, `ika` | set at, having (the rule's "and the like") | `anto niyutto`, `putto assa atthi` (he has a son) | `antimo`, `puttiko` | final, one who has a son | {353} |
| `kiya` | assigned to | `jātiyaṁ niyutto` | `jātikiyo` | assigned to a caste | {353} |

The `-ima` words `purimo` ("former"), `majjhimo` ("middle"), `pacchimo`
("last"), `uparimo` ("upper"), `heṭṭhimo` ("lower") and `antimo` ("final")
are the ones you will meet most.

### Examples

> nāvāya tarati → **nāviko**

🚺⨀③(nāvāya) 🟢⨀🤟▶️(tarati) → 🚹⨀①🔽(ṇika)(nāviko)  
by boat / crosses → one who crosses by boat, a sailor  
*`ṇika` {350}, "crosses by": `-ika` added to `nāvā`, whose first vowel is long already.*

> kāyena kataṁ kammaṁ → **kāyikaṁ**

🚹⨀③(kāyena) 🚻⨀①🔽(ta)(kataṁ) 🚻⨀①(kammaṁ) → 🚻⨀①🔽(ṇika)(kāyikaṁ)  
by the body / done / act → a bodily act  
*`ṇika` {351}, "made by": `-ika` on `kāya`; chapter 11's `kāyikaṁ vā cetasikaṁ vā`, "relating to the body or to the mind".*

> pacchā jāto → **pacchimo**

⏏️(pacchā) 🚹⨀①🔽(ta)(jāto) → 🚹⨀①🔽(ima)(pacchimo)  
after / born → born after, last  
*`ima` {353}, "born": `-ima` added to `pacchā`, nothing lengthened; chapter 3 has `pacchimaṁ yāmaṁ`, "the last watch".*

### Exercises

Each of the following is a word you met in the texts of chapters 3, 5 and 11.
Say which affix it carries, what the base is and what connection with the
base the word names; then look the word up and compare. (`sandiṭṭhiko`, in
the same sentence as the first three, is `sandiṭṭhi` with the affix `ka`, not
`ṇika`.)

### Answers

*Note: please do the exercises first before checking the answers.*

1. > akālo → **akāliko**

🚹⨀①(akālo) → 🚹⨀①🔽(ṇika)(akāliko)  
not the time → not tied to time, immediate  
*`ṇika` {350}, a further sense (the rule's `vā`): `-ika` added to `akāla`, nothing lengthened; said of the Dhamma in chapter 11.*

2. > ehi passa → **ehipassiko**

🟢⨀🤘⏹(ehi) 🟢⨀🤘⏹(passa) → 🚹⨀①🔽(ṇika)(ehipassiko)  
come! / see! → inviting one to come and see, verifiable  
*`ṇika` {350}, a further sense (the rule's `vā`): `-ika` added to the saying `ehi passa`; chapter 11 glosses it "verifiable".*

3. > upaneyyo → **opaneyyiko**

🚹⨀①(upaneyyo) → 🚹⨀①🔽(ṇika)(opaneyyiko)  
to be led towards (`upa` + `√nī`, "lead") → leading onward  
*`ṇika` {350}, a further sense (the rule's `vā`): the first `u` becomes `o`, `-ika` added.*

4. > cetasā kataṁ → **cetasikaṁ**

🚹⨀③(cetasā) 🚻⨀①🔽(ta)(kataṁ) → 🚻⨀①🔽(ṇika)(cetasikaṁ)  
by the mind / done → mental  
*`ṇika` {351}, "made by", as `kāyikaṁ`: `-ika` added to `cetas`; chapter 11's `kāyikaṁ vā cetasikaṁ vā`.*

5. > punabbhavo → **ponobbhavikā**

🚹⨀①🆎(puna+bhava) → 🚺⨀①🔽(ṇika)(ponobbhavikā)  
rebirth → leading to rebirth  
*`ṇika` {350}, a further sense (the rule's `vā`): `puna` becomes `pono`, `-ika` added; chapter 11's `taṇhā ponobbhavikā`.*

6. > niraye jātā → **nerayikā**

🚹⨀⑦(niraye) 🚹⨂①🔽(ta)(jātā) → 🚹⨂①🔽(ṇika)(nerayikā)  
in hell / born → born in hell, living in hell  
*`ṇika` {350}, "born in", as `rājagahiko`: the first `i` becomes `e`, `-ika` added; chapter 11's `sattā nerayikā`.*

7. > majjhe jātaṁ → **majjhimaṁ**

🚹⨀⑦(majjhe) 🚹⨀②🔽(ta)(jātaṁ) → 🚹⨀②🔽(ima)(majjhimaṁ)  
in the middle / born → middle  
*`ima` {353}, "born in": `-ima` added to `majjha`, nothing lengthened; chapter 3's `majjhimaṁ yāmaṁ`, "the middle watch".*

8. > anto niyuttā → **antimā**

⏏️(anto) 🚺⨀①🔽(ta)(niyuttā) → 🚺⨀①🔽(ima)(antimā)  
at the end / set → final, the last  
*`ima` {353}, "set at the end", by the rule's "and the like": `-ima` added to `anta`; chapter 11's `ayamantimā jāti`.*
